# C08 · Map Nodes (Module C Mind Map: "The Bank's Trading Desk")
Nodes added by Lesson C08 in order of appearance, matching course/bfm/C/plans_C.md §C08 (Derivatives: purposes, OTC vs exchange, forwards, futures, IRF):
1. Derivatives
Branch location: 10 o'clock on Module C Mind Map; centre = integrated treasury desk
Purposes (N017, N021)
* Value from underlying [RBI Act s. 45U(a), SC(R)A s. 2(ac)]
* Three treasury uses: hedging/ALM, customer service, proprietary trading/market-making
* Market risk only, not operational/legal risk
* Market-makers vs retail/non-retail users [₹500 cr net worth / ₹1,000 cr turnover]
* RBI is neither
OTC vs exchange (N033, N039, N047)
* Customised bilateral vs standardised CCP-cleared
* Upfront initial & extreme loss margin + daily T+1 cash M2M
* AD Cat-I membership norms [₹500 cr net worth, min capital incl. CCB, Net NPA <= 3%, 3-yr profit; else client]
* 10 Oct 2026 USD 5 million no-proof cap across all exchanges/ADs & no rebooking of cancelled INR FX derivatives
Forwards (N059, N069)
* OTC binding obligation > 2 business days
* FX forward & FX swap recall
* OTC G-Sec Bond Forwards permitted from 2 May 2025 on dated G-Secs excl. T-Bills for non-retail users, CCIL physical or cash settlement, SLR-eligible cover
Futures (N061, N064)
* Standardised exchange-traded forward
* NSE launch 29 Aug 2008
* 7 pairs up to 12 months
* USD/INR lot USD 1,000, tick ₹0.0025, cash-settled in INR at FBIL Reference Rate
* Linear payoff direction & convergence at expiry
IRF (N076, N080)
* Interest Rate Futures incl. Money Market Futures
* Jun 2003 launch, Jan 2014 cash-settled 10-yr G-Sec
* ₹2,00,000 notional per lot [2,000 units]
* FPI ₹5,000 cr net long cap
* Short IRF to hedge a bond book against rising yields
