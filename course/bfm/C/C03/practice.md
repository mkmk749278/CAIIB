# C03 · Practice (c1, 2026-11-24)
Our own questions, written from C03_fact_sheet and C03_numericals. All figures illustrative. Rules as at 31 December 2026. Answers and one-line reasons at the end.


1. Which of the following statements about Cash Management Bills (CMBs) is incorrect?
(a) They are issued to meet temporary cash flow mismatches of the Government of India
(b) Their maturity is less than 91 days
(c) Investments in CMBs qualify as eligible SLR securities
(d) Retail investors can bid for CMBs under the Non-Competitive Bidding Scheme
2. What is the minimum bid amount and multiple for a 91-day Treasury Bill auction conducted by RBI?
(a) ₹10,000 and in multiples of ₹10,000
(b) ₹25,000 and in multiples of ₹25,000
(c) ₹1,00,000 and in multiples of ₹1,00,000
(d) ₹5,00,000 and in multiples of ₹5,00,000
3. Under RBI's auction methodology in force as at 31 December 2026, how are dated Government of India securities (including 5-year and 10-year benchmark G-Secs) and Treasury Bills auctioned?
(a) Uniform Price for benchmark G-Secs up to 14 years and Multiple Price for T-Bills
(b) Multiple Price for all securities under the GoI market borrowing programme and T-Bills
(c) Uniform Price for all dated G-Secs and T-Bills
(d) Yield-based Uniform Price for T-Bills and Price-based Multiple Price for new G-Secs
4. A retail investor submits a non-competitive bid for ₹5 lakh in a weekly 182-day T-Bill auction through RBI Retail Direct. At what price will the allotment be made?
(a) The lowest competitive price accepted in the auction
(b) The highest competitive price accepted in the auction
(c) The weighted average price of successful competitive bids
(d) Par value of ₹100 less the prevailing RBI Repo rate
5. A company issues Commercial Paper (CP) of ₹1,200 crore during a calendar year and wishes to buy back part of the issue early. Under RBI's CP Master Direction, 2024, which pair is correct?
(a) Mandatory rating from two CRAs; buyback allowed only after 30 days
(b) Mandatory rating from one CRA (min A3); buyback allowed only after 30 days
(c) Mandatory rating from two CRAs; buyback allowed only after 7 days
(d) Mandatory rating from one CRA (min A3); buyback allowed only after 7 days
6. Compare buyback rules for Certificates of Deposit (CDs) as at 31 December 2026. Which statement is correct?
(a) Neither banks nor AIFIs are permitted to buy back their own CDs before maturity
(b) Both banks and AIFIs may buy back their own CDs after 7 days from issue
(c) Issuing banks may buy back CDs after 7 days from issue, whereas AIFIs are prohibited from buying back their own CDs
(d) Issuing banks may buy back CDs only after 30 days from issue, whereas AIFIs may buy back after 7 days
7. (Keyed-in numerical — no options) A Scheduled Commercial Bank issues a 180-day Certificate of Deposit (illustrative) of face value ₹5,00,000 at an issue price of ₹4,82,500. Compute the annualised yield (%, rounded to 4 decimal places).
8. (Keyed-in numerical — no options) A treasury desk targets an annualised yield of 6.80% on a 364-day Treasury Bill (illustrative). Compute the bid price per ₹100 face value (rounded to 4 decimal places).
Answers and One-Line Reasons
   1. (d) — CMBs are explicitly excluded from the Non-Competitive Bidding Scheme ([F11]); they are <91-day GoI bills for temporary cash mismatches and are SLR-eligible ([F8, F9, F10]).
   2. (a) — Both competitive and non-competitive bids in T-Bill auctions have a minimum of ₹10,000 and multiples of ₹10,000; ₹25,000 is the pre-2016 figure ([F6]).
   3. (b) — Effective 1 April 2024, RBI reversed its July 2021 Uniform Price split and mandated Multiple Price for all GoI market borrowing securities and T-Bills ([F21]).
   4. (c) — Non-competitive bids are allotted at the weighted average price/yield of successful competitive bids, not the cut-off price ([F18]).
   5. (d) — Under the 2024 CP Master Direction, only one CRA rating (min A3) is mandatory (the old ₹1,000 crore dual-rating rule was removed) and buyback is permitted after 7 days (cut from 30 days) ([F27, F28]).
   6. (c) — Banks (SCBs, RRBs, SFBs) may buy back CDs 7 days after issue under the 2021 CD Directions, whereas AIFIs are barred from buying back their own CDs under the Nov 2025 AIFI Directions ([F30]).
   7. 7.3546% — Using Actual/365 rear-ended discount: [(5,00,000 − 4,82,500) ÷ 4,82,500] x (365 ÷ 180) x 100 = 7.3546% ([F22, F23]).
   8. ₹93.6493 — Using Actual/365 price-from-yield: 100 ÷ [1 + (6.80 x 364) ÷ 36500] = 100 ÷ 1.0678137 = ₹93.6493 ([F22, F24]).
