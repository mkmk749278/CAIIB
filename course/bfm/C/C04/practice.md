# C04 · Practice (c1, 2026-11-24)
Original questions written from C04_fact_sheet (rules as at 31 December 2026; subject to 2–4 Dec 2026 MPC freeze check). All numericals verified two ways in C04_numericals. All bank names and figures are illustrative. Answers and one-line reasons at the end.
1. Under the Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025 and RBI's Amendment Directions effective 15 December 2025, a "fortnight" for CRR and SLR maintenance means:
(a) Saturday to the second following Friday, both days inclusive
(b) 1st to 15th and 16th to the last day of each calendar month, both days inclusive
(c) Monday to the second following Sunday, both days inclusive
(d) 1st to 14th and 15th to 28th of each calendar month
2. Which of the following items is INCLUDED in a commercial bank's NDTL for computing CRR and SLR?
(a) Refinance taken from NABARD and SIDBI
(b) Net income-tax provision
(c) Borrowings by the bank through repo in corporate bonds and debentures (from non-banks)
(d) Amount received from DICGC towards claims pending adjustment
3. A scheduled commercial bank has ₹3,000 crore of outstanding 10-year plain-vanilla INR infrastructure bonds (LB) and ₹2,400 crore of standard infrastructure loans of original maturity over 7 years (EC). What amount is exempted from its DTL for CRR and SLR?
(a) ₹3,000 crore
(b) ₹2,400 crore
(c) ₹600 crore
(d) Nil, because the infrastructure-bond exemption expired in 2025
4. Which of the following statements about statutory reserve limits and returns is CORRECT as at 31 December 2026?
(a) CRR has a statutory floor of 3% and ceiling of 20% under RBI Act s. 42
(b) SLR has a statutory floor of 25% and ceiling of 40% under BR Act s. 24
(c) CRR has neither a statutory floor nor ceiling, while SLR has no statutory floor and a 40% statutory ceiling
(d) Banks file a Provisional Form A within 7 days and a Final Form A within 20 days
5. Which of the following assets does NOT qualify for SLR maintenance by a Scheduled Commercial Bank?
(a) Balances held with RBI under the Standing Deposit Facility (SDF)
(b) Cash Management Bills issued by the Government of India
(c) Gold valued at a price not exceeding the current market price
(d) Unencumbered bonds issued by a development bank not notified by RBI as SLR securities
6. Which statement correctly distinguishes CRR and SLR asset eligibility for a Scheduled Commercial Bank?
(a) Cash in hand at branches and SDF balances count for SLR, but neither counts for CRR
(b) Cash in hand at branches counts for both CRR and SLR
(c) Mandatory CRR balances held with RBI also count toward the 18% SLR requirement
(d) SDF balances count for CRR, while excess CRR balances count for SLR
7. Under RBI's Monetary Policy Framework (as at 24 November 2026), what happens if average headline CPI inflation stays above 6% for three consecutive quarters?
(a) The Monetary Policy Committee is dissolved automatically
(b) It constitutes a failure to achieve the inflation target, and RBI must submit a report to the Central Government stating reasons, remedial actions, and timeline
(c) CRR is automatically raised by 100 basis points
(d) The inflation target automatically resets to 6%
8. (Keyed-in numerical) A Scheduled Commercial Bank has a daily CRR shortfall of ₹20 crore below the 99% daily minimum on Day 1 of a reporting fortnight, and the exact same ₹20 crore shortfall continues on Day 2. With the Bank Rate at 5.75%, compute the total penal interest payable to RBI for the two days combined (use 365 days; round to 2 decimals).
Answers (one-line reasons)
   1. (b) — Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025 redefined fortnight to 1st–15th and 16th–month end w.e.f. 15 Dec 2025, replacing alternate Fridays [F19].
   2. (c) — Corporate-bond repo borrowings are reckoned as liabilities for CRR/SLR (unlike G-sec market repo), whereas NABARD/SIDBI refinance, net tax provision, and pending DICGC claims are excluded under Para 19 [F8, F9, F11].
   3. (b) — Under RBI's Nov 2025 Resource Raising Norms Directions, the LTB exemption remains in force and equals Min(EC, LB) = Min(2,400, 3,000) = ₹2,400 crore [F13, F14].
   4. (c) — RBI (Amendment) Act, 2006 removed CRR floor/ceiling, BR (Amendment) Act, 2007 removed SLR's 25% floor while keeping the 40% ceiling, and from 15 Dec 2025 SCBs file a single Form A within 5 days [F16, F21, F28].
   5. (d) — SLR securities are GoI dated securities, T-Bills/CMBs, State Government Securities, and RBI-notified instruments; development-bank bonds are not automatically SLR assets [F24, F25, F26].
   6. (a) — SCBs hold CRR solely as RBI cash balances (neither branch cash nor SDF counts for CRR), whereas branch cash, SDF, and excess CRR (not mandatory CRR) count for SLR [F18, F23, F24].
   7. (b) — Under RBI Act s. 45ZA (CPI 4% ± 2% retained through 31 Mar 2031), 3 consecutive quarters outside 2%–6% is a target failure requiring an RBI report to the Central Government [F33].
   8. ₹1,06,849.32 — Day 1 at Bank Rate + 3% = 8.75% (20,00,00,000 × 8.75% ÷ 365 = ₹47,945.21) + Day 2 at Bank Rate + 5% = 10.75% (20,00,00,000 × 10.75% ÷ 365 = ₹58,904.11) [F20, F30, F35; C04_numericals].
