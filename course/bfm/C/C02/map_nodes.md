1. Money market: instruments (root branch introduced at N011)
   * Call, Notice & Term (completed at N033)
      * Tenors: Call (overnight), Notice (2–14 days), Term (15 days–1 year) — all unsecured
      * Participants: Banks (excl. LABs) & Primary Dealers (all both borrow & lend)
      * Borrowing caps: SCBs (Board within IBL since Jun 2023); SFBs Call/Notice 100%/125%; PBs/RRBs 100%/125%; Co-op 2% deposits; PDs 225% NOF; Lending = Board within exposure norms
      * Operations: NDS-CALL mandatory, OTC report ≤ 15 min, Call hours 9:00 AM–7:00 PM (Jul 2025)
   * Repo & TREPS (completed at N056)
      * Market Repo / Reverse Repo (Repo MD Nov 2025, excl. LAF/MSF): 1 day–1 year, Leg 1 T+0/T+1, DvP
      * Eligible collateral: G-Secs, listed corporate bonds, CP/CD, Debt ETFs, Municipal Debt Securities (Nov 2025, min 2% haircut); own/related-entity securities barred
      * TREPS (Clearcorp screen + CCIL CCP & Tri-Party Agent): replaced CBLO on 5 Nov 2018
   * CP & CD (completed at N086)
      * Commercial Paper (2024 MD): unsecured, demat, discount, 7d–1yr, min ₹5 lakh, Standard across banks/AIFIs/NBFCs, min 'A3' (2017 dual rating dropped), SCB IPA, buyback ≥ 7 days (not 30), individual+HUF cap 25%, related parties barred
      * Certificate of Deposit (2021 MD & AIFI 2025 MD): SCBs/RRBs/SFBs (7d–1yr) & AIFIs (1–3yr), min ₹5 lakh, demat, discount or fixed/floating, CRR/SLR applies, no loans against CD, bank buyback ≥ 7 days vs AIFI buyback barred
   * BRDS & OMO (completed at N098)
      * Bill Rediscounting (Credit Facilities MD 2025 Ch. X): genuine trade bills only, DUPN 15–90 days, up-front discount, MFs lenders only; accommodation bills, services bills & bill-repo barred
      * Open Market Operations (OMO): RBI outright G-sec buy (inject) / sell (absorb) for durable liquidity (links to next class: corridor)
