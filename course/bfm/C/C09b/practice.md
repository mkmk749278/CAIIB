# C09b · Practice (c1, 2026-11-24)
Eight original exam-style questions written from C09b_fact_sheet (rules as at 31 December 2026). All names, rates, and amounts are illustrative. Covers all blueprint question kinds (define, purpose, who/when/how much, compare, exception, calculate, apply to a case). Answers and one-line reasons at the end.
Questions
1. (Apply to a case) On 18 November 2026, an Indian exporter cancels an INR-USD forward contract booked with Bank A and requests Bank B to book a fresh INR-USD forward against the same export order at a better rate. What must Bank B do?
(a) Allow rebooking if the exporter submits the original shipping bill
(b) Allow rebooking up to USD 5 million without proof of underlying
(c) Refuse rebooking, as cancelled INR forex derivatives cannot be rebooked with any Authorised Dealer
(d) Allow rebooking only if the exporter is classified as a non-retail user
2. (Who / how much) Under RBI's Master Direction – Risk Management and Inter-Bank Dealings, a resident manufacturing company qualifies automatically as a non-retail user if its latest audited financial statements show at least:
(a) ₹100 crore net worth or ₹500 crore turnover
(b) ₹500 crore net worth or ₹1,000 crore turnover
(c) ₹250 crore net worth or ₹1,000 crore turnover
(d) ₹500 crore net worth and ₹1,000 crore turnover together
3. (Exception) Under the RBI (Market-makers in OTC Derivatives) Directions, 2021, pre-trade suitability and appropriateness due diligence is NOT mandatory for which of the following?
(a) Structured currency option with tenor of 6 months
(b) Plain-vanilla INR-USD forward contract with tenor of 12 months
(c) Plain-vanilla INR-USD forward contract with tenor of 18 months
(d) Rupee interest rate swap with tenor of 12 months
4. (Compare) Under Section 1(b) of the ISDA 2002 Master Agreement, if there is an inconsistency between the Schedule and a trade Confirmation, which document prevails?
(a) The Schedule always prevails over the Confirmation
(b) The printed Master Agreement prevails over both
(c) The Confirmation prevails for the purpose of that specific Transaction
(d) Both are void and the trade must be novated
5. (Who / when) Which of the following pairings of Indian market bodies and their founding/incorporation years is correct?
(a) FIMMDA — 1958; FEDAI — 1998; FBIL — 2014
(b) FEDAI — 1958; FIMMDA — 1998; FBIL — 2014
(c) FEDAI — 1998; FIMMDA — 1958; FBIL — 2008
(d) FEDAI — 1958; FIMMDA — 2008; FBIL — 2019
6. (Calculate — keyed-in numerical) An Indian importer books a current-account INR-USD forward contract on 20 November 2026 to buy USD 3.5 million at ₹86.00/USD. Calculate the Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve (FERR) in rupees (in crore, to 2 decimal places) that the Authorised Dealer must maintain in cash with RBI.
7. (Exception) Under RBI's October 2026 directions on Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve (FERR) and the 15 October 2026 FAQs, which of the following contracts does attract the 20% FERR requirement?
(a) A USD 4 million forward where an Indian exporter sells USD against INR
(b) A USD 2 million exact forward where an Indian importer buys USD against INR
(c) A USD 5 million forward where a corporate buys USD to hedge interest on an ECB
(d) A USD 3 million forward where an importer buys USD to hedge a current-account merchandise import
8. (Status / trap) Which statement accurately reflects RBI's derivative rules as at 31 December 2026?
(a) Gross PV01 of non-option rupee derivatives is capped at 0.25% of bank net worth
(b) Two corporate users in the same group may novate an OTC currency swap directly between themselves
(c) The Unique Transaction Identifier (UTI) mandate has been issued and takes effect from 1 January 2027
(d) Users may hedge contracted FX exposure up to USD 100 million without establishing the underlying
Answers and One-Line Reasons
   1. (c) — From 10 October 2026, ADs cannot permit users to rebook any INR forex derivative (deliverable or NDD) cancelled with any AD; only rollover on maturity is permitted [F27].
   2. (b) — Resident users qualify as non-retail with minimum ₹500 crore net worth or ₹1,000 crore turnover per latest audited financials [F4].
   3. (b) — Pre-trade due diligence is exempt only for plain-vanilla FX forwards and European FX call/put options with tenor up to 13 months [F11].
   4. (c) — Under ISDA 2002 Master Agreement s. 1(b), the Confirmation prevails over the Master Agreement (including the Schedule) for that specific Transaction [F17].
   5. (b) — FEDAI was set up in 1958, FIMMDA was incorporated on 4 May 1998, and FBIL was incorporated on 9 December 2014 [F18, F19, F20].
   6. ₹6.02 crore (₹6,02,00,000) — All five FERR conditions hold (USD 3.5m > USD 2m, current-account hedge, user buys FC); 3500000 × 86 = ₹30,10,00,000 × 20 % = ₹6,02,00,000 (check: 3500000 × 20% = 700000 × 86 = ₹6.02 cr) [F30].
   7. (d) — Exporter selling FC (a), notional ≤ USD 2m (b), and user hedges of capital-account-origin outflows like ECB interest (c) are all excluded from FERR; only (d) satisfies all five conditions [F30, F32].
   8. (c) — UTI takes effect 1 January 2027 ("issued, coming") [F26]; the 0.25% PV01 cap was deleted on 19 Oct 2026 [F34], novation is market-maker to market-maker only [F35], and the no-proof limit was cut to USD 5 million on 10 Oct 2026 [F28].
