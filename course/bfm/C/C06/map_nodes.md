	Nodes added in order of appearance
* Macaulay duration (N026, Chapter 1) — PV-weighted balance point in years; D <= Maturity (D = Maturity only for zero-coupon / STRIPS); higher coupon -> shorter D.
* Modified duration (N046, Chapter 2) — MD = D ÷ (1 + y/n); %ΔP ≈ −MD × Δy per 100 bps parallel shift; semi-annual G-sec uses 1 + y/2; convexity softens large shifts.
* BPV / PV01 (N061, Chapter 3) — MV × MD × 0.0001 (rupee value change per 1 bps); ₹1,000 × MD per ₹1 crore; RBI's 0.25% of net worth PV01 cap deleted 19 Oct 2026 (RBI/2026-27/294).
* Portfolio duration (N082, Chapter 4) — MD_p = Σ (w_i × MD_i) weighted by market value; BPV_p = Σ BPV_i; link to RBI Standardised Duration Method for market-risk capital.
