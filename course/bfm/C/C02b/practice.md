Q8 · Apply to a case / Compare (C2.4)
A corporate treasurer tells a junior officer: "We should price our new USD loan off 3-month USD LIBOR, and we cannot use MIBOR-OIS because MIBOR is only for interbank participants." Which correction is accurate?


* A. Both statements are right — synthetic USD LIBOR still runs for new loans, and MIBOR is interbank-only.
* B. USD LIBOR ceased permanently (panel after 30 Jun 2023; synthetic 30 Sep 2024) and is replaced by ARRs such as SOFR; and non-banks (including corporates) are permitted to use MIBOR-OIS under RBI's Rupee Interest Rate Derivatives Directions, 2025.
* C. USD LIBOR is replaced by SONIA, and MIBOR was discontinued on 7 July 2025 when SORR launched.
* D. USD LIBOR is replaced by Modified MIFOR for offshore USD loans, and only NBFCs may use MIBOR-OIS.
Answers (one-line reasons)
1. B — Under the Revised LMF (30 Sep 2025), the overnight Weighted Average Call Rate (WACR) continues as the single operating target of monetary policy [F3].
2. B — SDF replaced FRRR as the corridor floor on 8 April 2022, requires no collateral, has no upper ceiling, and counts toward SLR (not CRR) (RBI PR 53539; CRR & SLR Directions 2025) [F5, F6, F7, F8].
3. 450 (₹450 crore) — 22500 × 2 % = 450; MSF allows dipping up to 2% of NDTL (end of second preceding fortnight) into stipulated SLR without a waiver (3% was the old COVID relaxation) (CRR & SLR Directions 2025) [F9, F10].
4. B — VRR is a multiple-price auction (winners pay their own bid rate), bids at or below the repo rate are rejected, and bids are arranged in descending order (RBI VRR Guidelines) [F17, F18].
5. C — On 30 Sep 2025, RBI discontinued 14-day VRR/VRRR as the main operation and shifted to primarily 7-day VRR/VRRR plus overnight-to-14-day tenors, normally with at least one day's advance notice (Revised LMF) [F13, F14].
6. B — RBI/2025-26/205 subsumed VRR's standalone ₹2.5 lakh crore cap into General Route limits from 1 Apr 2026 while retaining VRR as an active channel, and RBI/2026-27/97 (5 Jun 2026) withdrew the three General Route G-Sec limits [F25, F26].
7. 50 (USD 50 million) — 420 × 10 ÷ 84 = 50; AD Cat-I overseas borrowing ceiling is 100% of unimpaired Tier I capital (USD 50 million) or USD 10 million, whichever is higher (RBI MD Risk Management & Inter-Bank Dealings) [F27].
8. B — All 35 LIBOR settings have permanently ceased (FCA End of LIBOR; replaced by SOFR for USD), and RBI Rupee Interest Rate Derivatives Directions, 2025 permit non-banks to use FBA benchmarks such as MIBOR in OTC IRDs [F32, F33, F38].
