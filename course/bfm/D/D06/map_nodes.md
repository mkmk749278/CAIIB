# D06 · Map nodes (Module D Mind Map: "Reading a bank's balance sheet")
Branch: 8 o'clock — Interest rate risk (shared with D05 and D06b). Nodes added by D06 in order of appearance:


1. Duration gap (Ch 1, N035)
   * MDG = MDA − (RSL / RSA) × MDL (W = RSL / RSA)
   * Global balance sheet + separate currency DGA if ≥ 5% of global assets/liabilities
2. Change in equity value (ΔMVE) (Ch 2, N053)
   * ΔE = −MDG × RSA × Δi · MDOE = MDG × (RSA / Equity)
   * Economic impact (not accounting loss) · Pillar II outlier: banking-book drop > 20% of MVE at 200 bps
3. MCLR (Ch 3, N076)
   * Internal benchmark (w.e.f. 1 Apr 2016) · 4 parts (MCF [92/8], CRR negative carry, operating costs, tenor premium)
   * 5 mandatory maturities (O/N, 1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y) · monthly review · reset ≤ 1 yr anchored to first disbursement
4. EBLR (Ch 3, N076)
   * External benchmark for floating retail & MSME loans (Repo / FBIL 3M or 6M T-Bill / other FBIL rate; uniform per category)
   * Reset at least once in 3 months · Credit risk premium on substantial change · Other spread: once in 3 yrs, early reduction allowed (1 Oct 2025)
