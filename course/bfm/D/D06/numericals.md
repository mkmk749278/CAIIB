Q1 · Modified Duration Gap — Bank A (Worked Full, N025–N029) [F5]
* Data: RSA = ₹10,000 crore, MDA = 3.20 years; RSL = ₹9,000 crore, MDL = 1.50 years; Equity (Net Worth) = ₹1,000 crore.
* Formula: MDG = MDA − (RSL / RSA) × MDL
* Method 1 (Basic calculator key presses):
   * 9000 ÷ 10000 × 1.50 = → 1.3500
   * 3.20 − 1.35 = → 1.8500 years
* Method 2 (Rupee-duration net method):
   * Asset rupee-duration = 3.20 × 10000 = 32,000
   * Liability rupee-duration = 1.50 × 9000 = 13,500
   * Net rupee-duration ÷ RSA = (32000 − 13500) ÷ 10000 = 18500 ÷ 10000 = 1.8500 years (exact match).
* Commonest wrong answer (trap): Forgetting W = RSL / RSA = 0.90 gives 3.20 − 1.50 = 1.70 years (wrong).

Practice Sum · Macaulay Duration Conversion (Practice Q7) [F3, F5, F6]
* Data: RSA = ₹5,000 crore, RSL = ₹4,500 crore, Macaulay DA = 3.24 years, Macaulay DL = 1.62 years, initial yield y = 8% (0.08), parallel rate rise Δi = +1% (+0.01).
* Method 1 (Convert each to Modified Duration first):
   * MDA = 3.24 ÷ 1.08 = 3.0000; MDL = 1.62 ÷ 1.08 = 1.5000
   * W = 4500 ÷ 5000 = 0.9000
   * MDG = 3.0000 − (0.9000 × 1.5000) = 3.0000 − 1.3500 = 1.6500 years
   * ΔE = −1.6500 × 5000 × 0.01 = −₹82.50 crore
* Method 2 (Compute Macaulay DGAP first, then divide by 1 + y):
   * DGAP_Mac = 3.24 − (0.90 × 1.62) = 3.24 − 1.4580 = 1.7820
   * MDG = 1.7820 ÷ 1.08 = 1.6500 years
   * ΔE = −1.7820 × 5000 × (0.01 ÷ 1.08) = −₹82.50 crore (exact match).
