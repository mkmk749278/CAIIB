# D06 · Practice (c1, 2026-11-24)
Eight original questions (6 MCQs and 2 keyed-in numericals) written from D06_fact_sheet and verified in D06_numericals. All figures are illustrative. Answer keys are balanced across options; distractors use real exam traps (omitting W = RSL/RSA, outdated pre-Oct 2025 spread/switchover rules, confusable reset periods).
Questions
1. Under the RBI (Commercial Banks – Asset Liability Management) Directions, 2025, a commercial bank's Modified Duration Gap (MDG) is computed as:
(a) MDA − MDL
(b) MDA − (RSL / RSA) × MDL
(c) MDL − (RSA / RSL) × MDA
(d) (MDA + MDL) × (RSL / RSA)
2. A bank's Duration Gap Analysis shows that a +200 bps interest rate shock reduces the economic value of its banking book by 24% of its Market Value of Equity (MVE). Under RBI's ALM Directions, 2025, which statement is correct?
(a) The bank must book an immediate 24% accounting loss in its Profit & Loss account
(b) The bank is within the 25% Pillar II supervisory tolerance limit
(c) The bank is treated as a supervisory outlier under Pillar II, though the drop is an economic impact and not an accounting loss
(d) The outlier threshold applies only to the combined banking and trading book, not the banking book alone
3. Which of the following is NOT one of the four prescribed components of MCLR under the RBI (Commercial Banks – Interest Rates on Advances) Directions, 2025?
(a) Marginal cost of funds
(b) Negative carry on account of CRR
(c) Tenor premium
(d) Credit risk premium
4. Which statement about EBLR spread changes is TRUE as at 31 December 2026?
(a) Other spread components (such as operating costs) can never be reduced earlier than three years
(b) Credit risk premium can be increased every quarter whenever the policy repo rate rises
(c) Banks may reduce other spread components earlier than three years for a loan category for customer retention, on justifiable, non-discriminatory grounds
(d) Banks must reset both the external benchmark and all spread components once every six months
5. Under the RBI circular on Reset of Floating Interest Rate on EMI-based Personal Loans (as updated on 1 October 2025), at the time of an interest rate reset, providing a choice to switch over to a fixed interest rate is:
(a) Optional at the bank's discretion, as per its Board-approved policy
(b) Mandatory for all regulated entities on every reset date
(c) Prohibited for home loans linked to the RBI policy repo rate
(d) Allowed only once after five years of loan repayment
6. Which of the following rupee loans is EXEMPT from being priced with reference to Base Rate, MCLR, or EBLR under the 2025 Directions?
(a) A floating-rate housing loan to a retail individual
(b) A floating-rate working capital loan to a Medium Enterprise
(c) A fixed-rate term loan of two years' tenor
(d) A fixed-rate term loan of five years' tenor
7. [Keyed-in numerical] A bank has RSA = ₹5,000 crore (Macaulay DA = 3.24 years) and RSL = ₹4,500 crore (Macaulay DL = 1.62 years). Current market yield y = 8% p.a. Compute (i) Modified Duration Gap (MDG) in years and (ii) Change in Market Value of Equity (ΔE) in ₹ crore for a parallel interest rate rise of 100 bps (+1%).
8. [Keyed-in numerical] A commercial bank has Marginal Cost of Borrowings = 6.20% and Return on Net Worth = 12.00%. Required CRR is 3.00%. Operating costs are 0.55% and 1-year tenor premium is 0.30%. Compute (i) Marginal Cost of Funds (%), (ii) Negative Carry on CRR (%), and (iii) 1-Year MCLR (%).
Answers and reasons
   1. (b) — RBI defines MDG as MDA − (RSL / RSA) × MDL; omitting the weight W = RSL / RSA is a classic error [F5].
   2. (c) — A banking-book decline exceeding 20% of MVE under a 200 bps shock marks a Pillar II supervisory outlier, and the decline is an economic impact rather than an accounting loss because the banking book is not marked to market [F9, F10].
   3. (d) — Credit risk premium is part of the spread added over MCLR, not one of the four components of MCLR itself (MCF, negative carry on CRR, operating costs, tenor premium) [F16, F21].
   4. (c) — Effective 1 October 2025, RBI inserted a proviso allowing banks to reduce other spread components earlier than three years for customer retention on justifiable, non-discriminatory grounds per policy; credit risk premium changes only on substantial change in credit assessment [F26].
   5. (a) — Effective 1 October 2025, RBI amended paragraph 2(ii) from "shall" to "may, at its option", making the floating-to-fixed switchover discretionary per Board-approved policy (while no negative amortisation and quarterly statements remain mandatory) [F29].
   6. (d) — Fixed-rate loans of tenor above three years are exempt; fixed-rate loans up to three years have a floor equal to the sum of MCLR components, and floating retail/MSME loans must link to EBLR [F23, F28].
   7. MDG = 1.65 years; ΔE = −₹82.50 crore — MDA = 3.24 / 1.08 = 3.00; MDL = 1.62 / 1.08 = 1.50; W = 4500 / 5000 = 0.90; MDG = 3.00 − (0.90 × 1.50) = 1.65; ΔE = −1.65 × 5000 × 0.01 = −₹82.50 crore [F3, F5, F6].
   8. MCF = 6.6640%; Negative Carry on CRR = 0.2061%; 1-Year MCLR = 7.7201% (7.72%) — MCF = 0.92 × 6.20 + 0.08 × 12.00 = 6.6640%; Negative Carry = (3 × 6.6640) / 97 = 0.2061%; MCLR = 6.6640 + 0.2061 + 0.55 + 0.30 = 7.7201% [F16, F17, F18].
