# D06b · Practice (c1, 2026-11-24)
Our own questions, written from D06b_fact_sheet and D06b_numericals (rules as at 31 December 2026). All bank names and figures are illustrative. Answers with one-line reasons at the end.


1. A commercial bank is liability-sensitive (RSL > RSA) and its ALCO expects interest rates to rise next quarter. Which combination is an appropriate defensive action?
(a) Shorten fixed-deposit tenors and enter a receive-fixed interest rate swap
(b) Lengthen fixed-deposit tenors, shift new loans to floating rates, or enter a pay-fixed / receive-floating IRS
(c) Actively trade Held to Maturity (HTM) government securities each month to zero out the gap
(d) Widen the negative gap further to profit from rising rates
2. Under the RBI (Commercial Banks – Classification, Valuation and Operation of Investment Portfolio) Directions, 2025, what is the annual threshold on non-exempt sales out of the HTM category beyond which prior RBI DoS approval is required?
(a) 2% of total investment portfolio
(b) 5% of the opening carrying value of the HTM portfolio
(c) 10% of the closing market value of the HTM portfolio
(d) 15% of Tier 1 capital
3. A dealer claims that as at 31 December 2026, a commercial bank's gross PV01 of non-option rupee interest rate derivatives is capped by RBI at 0.25% of net worth. Which statement is accurate?
(a) Correct; the 0.25% cap remains in para 37 of the Miscellaneous Directions, 2025
(b) Incorrect; the cap was raised to 0.50% of net worth in October 2026
(c) Incorrect; RBI deleted the 0.25% PV01 cap on 19 October 2026 (RBI/2026-27/294) with immediate effect
(d) Incorrect; the 0.25% cap applies only to exchange-traded Interest Rate Futures
4. Under RBI's ALM Directions, 2025 and BCBS sound practices on interest rate risk governance, which statement is incorrect?
(a) The Board or Risk Management Committee approves internal limits on EaR and MVE volatility
(b) ALCO gives prior approval for DGA yields, assumptions, bucketing, and behavioural studies
(c) The treasury position-taking desk should also run the bank's IRR measurement and limit-compliance reporting to save time
(d) Independent audit of IRR models is vetted for suitability by the Audit Committee of the Board (ACB)
5. As at 31 December 2026, what is the exact regulatory status of RBI's BCBS-aligned IRRBB framework (6 shock scenarios, 19 time buckets, 15% of Tier 1 outlier test)?
(a) Fully in force since 1 April 2024; TGA and DGA have been abolished
(b) Issued as Chapter V of Commercial Banks – Forthcoming Instructions Directions, 2025 (issued, coming; effective date to be communicated separately), with interim quarterly Table 12 reporting to RBI DoR
(c) Withdrawn by RBI in November 2025 in favour of Pillar 1 capital charges
(d) Applicable only to Small Finance Banks and Regional Rural Banks
6. Keyed-in numerical: An illustrative commercial bank has Equity (MVE) of ₹1,000 crore, RSA = ₹10,000 crore (MDA = 2.50 years), and RSL = ₹9,000 crore (MDL = 1.50 years). Under RBI's currently in-force +200 bps parallel interest rate shock, what is the percentage fall in MVE, and is the bank an outlier?
(a) 20.00% — not an outlier
(b) 23.00% — outlier (exceeds 20% of MVE)
(c) 15.00% — outlier (exceeds 15% of MVE)
(d) 11.50% — not an outlier
7. Under the currently in-force ALM Directions, 2025 (para 268), behavioural studies for non-maturity deposits (savings and current accounts) must be based on at least how many years of data, and when are they reviewed?
(a) At least 1 year of data, reviewed monthly
(b) At least 3 years of data, reviewed/revised once a year in Q1 of the financial year
(c) At least 5 years of data, reviewed every two years
(d) At least 10 years of data, reviewed semi-annually
8. Under RBI's Forthcoming Instructions Directions, 2025 (Ch. V, IRRBB — issued, coming), which pair correctly matches ΔEVE and ΔNII disclosure assumptions?
(a) ΔEVE: constant balance sheet including own equity · ΔNII: run-off balance sheet over 5 years
(b) ΔEVE: run-off balance sheet excluding own equity · ΔNII: constant balance sheet over a rolling 12-month period
(c) ΔEVE: dynamic balance sheet · ΔNII: run-off balance sheet excluding commercial margins
(d) Both ΔEVE and ΔNII use a run-off balance sheet over 3 years
Answers (one-line reasons)
   1. (b) — A liability-sensitive bank loses NII when rates rise, so it lengthens liabilities, shortens/floats assets, or enters a pay-fixed / receive-floating IRS [F6, F8].
   2. (b) — Non-exempt sales from HTM are capped at 5% of opening carrying value per financial year without prior RBI DoS approval [F3].
   3. (c) — Section C (para 37) of Commercial Banks – Miscellaneous Directions, 2025 was deleted on 19 Oct 2026 (RBI/2026-27/294) [F10].
   4. (c) — Risk measurement, monitoring, and control must be independent of position-taking functions to avoid conflicts of interest [F14].
   5. (b) — Consolidated into Forthcoming Instructions Directions, 2025 (effective date pending), with interim quarterly Table 12 submission to RBI DoR; TGA/DGA remain in force [F24, F25].
   6. (b) — MDG = 2.50 − (9000/10000)×1.50 = 1.15; ΔE = −1.15 × 10000 × 0.02 = −₹230 crore; 230 / 1000 = 23.00% > 20% outlier [F20, F22; numericals Q1].
   7. (b) — ALM Directions 2025 para 268 requires at least 3 years of data, reviewed/revised annually in Q1 (10-year data is under the forthcoming IRRBB framework) [F23, F28].
   8. (b) — Forthcoming Instructions Directions 2025 para 87 prescribes run-off balance sheet (excluding own equity) for ΔEVE and constant balance sheet over a rolling 12-month period for ΔNII [F27].
