# D05 · Map Nodes (c1, 2026-11-24)
Nodes added by lesson D05 ("Interest rate risk 1: sources, gap, NII, NIM", syllabus D5.1 & D5.2 gap/earnings) to Mind Map D ("Reading a bank's balance sheet"), Branch 8 (Interest rate risk), in exact order of appearance in D05_script_class. Names match course/bfm/D/plans_D.md §D05.
1. Interest rate risk (branch 8 introduced at N018)
2. Sources (introduced N018, completed N038)
   * Deregulation history: 1990s → 1997 → Savings bank deposit rate deregulated 25 Oct 2011
   * Six core sources: Gap/Mismatch, Basis, Embedded Option (automatic & behavioural), Yield Curve, Price, Reinvestment (+ Net Interest Position Risk)
   * Trading book (price risk) vs Banking book (earnings & economic value)
3. Gap (introduced N047, completed N067)
   * Gap = RSA − RSL; slot by residual maturity or next repricing date, whichever is earlier
   * RBI 11 IRS time buckets (1–28d to >15y + Non-sensitive); global position + ≥ 5% significant currencies
   * Non-sensitive excluded (Cash, Fixed Assets, Current Deposits, Capital & Reserves); behavioural studies (≥ 3 yrs data) for savings/current/mortgages
   * Positive (asset-sensitive): Rate↑ → NII↑, Rate↓ → NII↓; Negative (liability-sensitive): Rate↑ → NII↓, Rate↓ → NII↑
4. NII (introduced N070, completed N077)
   * ΔNII = Gap × Δr (signed Gap × signed rate change)
   * NII = Interest Earned − Interest Expended (reverse all accrued unrealised NPA interest; SNFA rule eff. 1 Oct 2026)
5. NIM (introduced N078, completed N097)
   * RBI / IIBF standard: NIM = (NII ÷ Average Total Assets) × 100
   * Denominator trap: Average Earning Assets excludes non-earning assets and yields a higher %
6. EaR (introduced N107, completed N118)
   * Earnings at Risk: potential 1-year NII drop under adverse rate scenarios; Board/RMC limits linked to NII
   * Gap blind spots (6): intra-bucket timing, basis risk, embedded options, non-parallel shifts, time value/MVE, non-interest items
   * Two perspectives: Earnings (TGA, short-term) vs Economic Value (DGA, long-term MVE; 200 bps / >20% MVE outlier operative; 6-shock 15% Tier 1 IRRBB "issued, coming")
   * Techniques ladder: Gap → Duration → Simulation (static vs dynamic)
