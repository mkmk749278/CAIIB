# D02 · Practice (c1, 2026-11-24)

Question 1 (Define)

* (a) it has remained in the doubtful category for more than three years
* (b) loss has been identified by the bank, internal/external auditors, or RBI inspection, and not written off wholly
* (c) its realisable security has fallen below 50 per cent of the last assessed value
* (d) interest or principal has remained overdue for 180 days

Question 5 (Apply to a case — Working capital & OD/CC)

Question 7 (Calculate — Security erosion)
A borrower's term loan has an outstanding balance of ₹80 lakh. Tangible collateral was assessed at ₹60 lakh at the last RBI inspection. A fresh valuation places the realisable value at ₹24 lakh. How should the bank classify this account straightaway?


* (a) Sub-standard
* (b) Doubtful

Question 8 (Current 2026 rule & "issued, coming" contrast)
Which statement is correct as on 31 December 2026?


* (a) Accrued unrealised interest prior to acquiring an SNFA may be booked as interest income on the date of acquisition
* (b) The RBI Expected Credit Loss (ECL) Directions, 2026 are already mandatory from 1 October 2026
* (c) Accrued unrealised interest prior to acquiring an SNFA cannot be recognised as income from 1 October 2026, and any legacy amount already recognised as on 30 September 2026 must be reversed by 30 September 2027
* (d) Commercial banks must report SMA accounts to CRILC only when aggregate borrower exposure is ₹25 crore and above

Answers & Explanations
1. (b) — A loss asset is one where loss is identified by the bank, internal/external auditors, or RBI inspection and not wholly written off [F34] (RBI IRACP Directions 2025, para 5(5)).
2. (b) 29 August 2026 — 60 exact days after 30 June: 31 days in July + 29 days in August = 60 days at day-end on 29 August 2026 [F19] (RBI IRACP Directions 2025, para 31).
3. (c) — Revolving CC/OD facilities have no SMA-0; SMA-1 begins only when continuous excess crosses 30 days [F17] (RBI Resolution of Stressed Assets Directions 2025, para 15).
4. (a) Standard — Short-duration crops (<= 1 year) become NPA only after two overdue crop seasons, and crop-season farm credit is exempt from SMA tagging [F18, F22] (RBI IRACP Directions 2025, para 42(6)).
5. (c) — Stock statements older than 3 months make DP irregular, and irregular drawings for 90 continuous days trigger NPA even if the unit is profitable [F25] (RBI IRACP Directions 2025, para 15(3)–(4), 42(3)).
6. (c) — Advances against Term Deposits, eligible NSCs, KVPs, and LIC policies with adequate margin are exempt; gold ornaments, G-secs, and State Government guarantees are not exempt [F10] (RBI IRACP Directions 2025, para 55, 58).
7. (b) Doubtful — Realisable value ₹24 lakh ÷ ₹60 lakh last assessed value = 40% (< 50% → straightaway Doubtful), while ₹24 lakh ÷ ₹80 lakh outstanding = 30% (>= 10% → not Loss) [F35, F36] (RBI IRACP Directions 2025, para 68).
8. (c) — Under the Second Amendment Directions effective 1 October 2026, prior unrealised interest cannot be booked on SNFA acquisition and legacy amounts must be reversed by 30 September 2027 [F15] (RBI IRACP Second Amendment Directions 2026); ECL takes effect on 1 April 2027 [F38] (RBI ECL Directions 2026); CRILC threshold is ₹5 crore [F21].
