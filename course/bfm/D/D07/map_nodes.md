1. Profit planning (Branch 10 o'clock · Ch 1–2)
* Six P&L levers (N014): 3 income (interest Sch 13, fees Sch 14, treasury Sch 14) vs 3 expense (interest Sch 15, staff Sch 16, other opex Sch 16) + provisions above the line
* Profit at acceptable risk (N018): sustainable value vs accounting profit; treasury volatility; G-Sec 0% RW lower yield; NPA double hit; 25% Statutory Reserve transfer
* Banking landscape (N033): Narasimham I (1991) & II (1998) reforms · SFBs (₹300 cr, 15% CRAR, 60% PSL, 50% <= ₹25 lakh) · Payments Banks (₹100 cr, ₹2 lakh cap, no lending, 75% SLR) · NaBFID (2021 Act AIFI, ₹1 lakh cr auth cap, no public deposits) · 12 PSBs (1 Apr 2020 mergers)
2. EL / UL (Ch 3)
* Expected Loss (EL) (N053): average loss (PD × EAD × LGD) → covered by pricing and provisions, never capital
* Unexpected Loss (UL) (N057): tail loss above EL up to VaR confidence level → absorbed by capital
* Variability & correlation (N059): same EL can hide very different UL when correlation differs
3. Economic capital (Ch 3)
* Regulatory vs Economic capital (N061): RBI Pillar 1 floor (9% + 2.5% CCB = 11.5%) vs internal all-risk estimate at chosen confidence level & horizon
4. Aggregation & allocation (Ch 4)
* Risk aggregation (N069): harmonised horizon & confidence level; √(EC1² + EC2² + 2 × ρ × EC1 × EC2); diversification benefit (ρ < 1) vs additive stress (ρ = 1)
* Concentration effect (N081): higher correlation raises economic capital even with unchanged profit
* Capital allocation & ICAAP (N085): allocated by risk contribution (not asset size) under Board-approved annual ICAAP (Pillar 2) linked to hurdle rate
