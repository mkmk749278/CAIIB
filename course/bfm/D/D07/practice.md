Q7 (Calculate · Keyed-in numerical, no options)

Q8 (Calculate · Keyed-in numerical, no options)
Using the same two desks from Q7 (EC1 = ₹15 crore, EC2 = ₹20 crore), suppose market stress pushes their correlation to ρ = +0.5. Compute the new aggregated Economic Capital (in ₹ crore, to 2 decimals) using only the basic calculator.
Answers (one-line reasons)
* Q1: B — Net loss on sale of investments is shown as a deduction in Schedule 14 (Other Income), and staff salaries sit under Schedule 16(I) (Payments to and provisions for employees) [F1, F2, F4].
* Q2: C — Under RBI's 2025 SFB Licensing Guidelines and the 20 June 2025 PSL circular, SFB minimum paid-up voting equity is ₹300 crore (up from ₹200 crore) and PSL target is 60% of ANBC/CEOBE from FY 2025-26 (down from 75%) [F12, F13].
* Q3: C — Payments Banks cannot lend or issue credit cards and cap balances at ₹2 lakh (raised from ₹1 lakh on 8 Apr 2021), while NaBFID is an RBI-regulated AIFI funded via wholesale/institutional borrowings under s. 17 without retail public deposits [F16, F19, F20].
* Q4: C — EL is covered by pricing and provisions, whereas capital absorbs UL; higher asset correlation widens loss variability and UL even when EL is identical [F23, F24, F25].
* Q5: B — Regulatory capital is RBI's Pillar 1 statutory floor (9% CRAR + 2.5% CCB = 11.5%), whereas economic capital is the bank's internal estimate across all material risks at its chosen confidence level and horizon [F26, F27].
* Q6: C — Sector concentration raises correlation and economic capital even with unchanged profit, lowering risk-adjusted return and requiring Pillar 2 coverage in the Board-approved ICAAP reviewed at least annually [F30, F31].
* Q7: (a) ₹25.00 crore; (b) ₹10.00 crore — Method 1: √(15² + 20²) = √(225 + 400) = √625 = 25.00; simple sum = 15 + 20 = 35.00; diversification benefit = 35.00 − 25.00 = 10.00. Method 2: √(35² − 2(1)(15)(20)) = √(1225 − 600) = √625 = 25.00 [F29].
* Q8: ₹30.41 crore — Method 1: √(15² + 20² + 2(0.5)(15)(20)) = √(225 + 400 + 300) = √925 = 30.4138 → ₹30.41 crore. Method 2: √(35² − 2(0.5)(15)(20)) = √(1225 − 300) = √925 = 30.41 crore [F29, F30].
