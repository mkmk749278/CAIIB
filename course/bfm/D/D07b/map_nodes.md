1. RAROC (introduced at N014; completed at N061)
   * Three-step method: (1) measure risk, (2) allocate economic capital (confidence level & 1-yr horizon), (3) measure risk-adjusted performance [F4]
   * Formula: (Revenue − Costs − EL) ÷ Economic Capital; hurdle rate decision (> hurdle = value created, < hurdle = reject/reprice) [F5, F8, F9]
   * ROA/ROE vs RAROC: ROA/ROE ignore risk and ROE rewards leverage [F10, F11, F12]
   * Project choice & risk-based loan pricing: Minimum Rate = Cost + EL + (EC % × Hurdle); RBI credit risk premium reflects rating, EL & collateral [F13, F14, F15, F16]
2. RAPM (introduced at N064; completed at N085)
   * Compares desks/traders on risk-adjusted capital; equal accounting profit ≠ equal performance [F17]
   * Parametric Risk Capital: z × Notional × Volatility (standard normal z supplied in exam: 95% = 1.645, 99% = 2.326/2.33, 99.9% = 3.090) [F18, F20]
   * Desk RAPM: Net Profit ÷ Risk Capital × 100 [F19]
3. EVA (introduced at N088; completed at N114)
   * Formula (Stern & Stewart, 1991): EVA = PAT (or NOPAT) − (Invested Capital × Cost of Capital) (absolute ₹ value created/destroyed) [F24, F25]
   * Cost of Capital inputs: CAPM Ke = Rf + β × (Rm − Rf) (recognised by RBI) and WACC (We × Ke) + [Wd × Kd × (1 − t)] [F21, F22, F23]
   * Positive PAT can still yield negative EVA when full capital charge exceeds profit [F25, F26]
   * Contrast with Earnings at Risk (EaR): EaR = 1-year NII shock in ₹ from interest-rate shifts (ALM), whereas RAROC = % return on economic capital across risks [F27, F28]
