## D01 · The bank's balance sheet, ALM and ALCO
- **Goal**: read a bank's balance sheet (sources and uses of funds), and explain ALM, ALCO and transfer pricing.
- **Evidence**: D-ALM: ALCO and ALM (Jun 2023 3 questions, Jul 2024 ×3), sources and applications of funds (Dec 2023);
  FP1 Q44, Q47, Q53.
- **Opening**: a bank's annual report on a phone screen: "Where does the money come from and where does it go?"
- **Misconceptions**: some think deposits are assets of the bank; others think ALM is only about liquidity.
- **Picture**: two shelves: capital, reserves, deposits, borrowings (left); cash, investments, advances, fixed assets
  (right); ALCO as a meeting table between them.
- **Chapters**: 1 The forms (Third Schedule): what sits where · 2 ALM: purpose, liquidity and interest-rate risk, the
  link to B13 · 3 ALCO: who sits, what it decides · 4 Funds transfer pricing (syllabus C7). ~12 min.
- **Traps**: sources vs uses; ALCO decides vs board approves; FTP purpose.
- **Practice**: 3.
- **Map nodes**: Components, ALM, ALCO, FTP.
- **Facts to verify**: Banking Regulation Act Third Schedule; ALM Directions 2025 (ALCO composition and role).

## D02 · Asset classification: NPA and SMA
- **Goal**: classify any loan as standard, SMA-0/1/2, sub-standard, doubtful or loss, including agriculture by crop
  season, OD/CC and credit cards.
- **Evidence**: D-NPA seen 6/7, 29 mentions: SMA-1/SMA-2 days (Jun 2023, Jun 2025), crop season (Dec 2023 sugarcane,
  Jul 2024 paddy, Jun 2026), credit card NPA (Jul 2024, Dec 2025), OD norms (Dec 2025); FP1 Q16, Q17, Q27, Q46, Q51, Q54;
  FP2 Q10, Q11.
- **Opening**: a farmer's sugarcane loan, a shop's overdraft and a credit card, all unpaid since August. Which are NPAs
  on 31 March?
- **Misconceptions**: some count 90 days from the due date for crop loans; others think an OD becomes NPA only when
  interest is unpaid.
- **Picture**: the 90-day clock with green/amber/red; a crop calendar (short and long duration crops).
- **Chapters**: 1 The idea: record of recovery, 90 days · 2 SMA-0/1/2 and the daily (day-end) rule · 3 Special cases:
  agriculture by crop seasons, OD/CC "out of order", credit cards, upgrade · 4 Sub-standard, doubtful (D1/D2/D3), loss;
  significant erosion of security; ECL coming 1 Apr 2027. ~14 min.
- **Traps**: crop season counts; "out of order" definition; credit card from the statement due date; erosion test.
- **Practice**: 3 classification questions.
- **Map nodes**: Asset quality: SMA, NPA classes.
- **Facts to verify**: IRACP directions as in force on 31 Dec 2026 (SMA days, crop seasons, OD, credit cards,
  erosion test); ECL directions (27 Apr 2026, effective 1 Apr 2027: "issued, coming"); Narasimham Committee (FP1 Q27).

## D03 · Provisioning
- **Goal**: compute provisions for every class, PCR, accelerated provisioning, and wilful defaulter effects.
- **Evidence**: provisioning (Dec 2023 ×2 case, Dec 2025 ×4, Jun 2026 "loan became NPA on 31 March 2025, outstanding
  1.2…"), PCR (Dec 2023, Jul 2024), accelerated provisioning (Jul 2024, Jun 2026 ×2), wilful defaulter (Jul 2024 ×2,
  Jun 2025 ×2, Jun 2026 ×2); FP1 Q18 (fraud provision), Q20, Q55, Q56; FP2 Q47, Q48.
- **Opening**: a fully secured loan, but one director is on the wilful defaulters' list.
- **Misconceptions**: some apply doubtful rates to the whole balance; others think a fully secured loan needs no
  provision.
- **Picture**: the 90-day clock (D02) with a provision jar filling as the account ages; secured and unsecured parts
  as two colours.
- **Chapters**: 1 Standard asset provisions · 2 Sub-standard, doubtful (secured vs unsecured), loss; fraud
  provisions · 3 Accelerated provisioning and wilful defaulters (rules now; the 2027 change "issued, coming") ·
  4 PCR and the drill. ~13 min.
- **Numericals**: 4 (doubtful D2 with security; loss asset; PCR; accelerated).
- **Traps**: secured vs unsecured split; age in doubtful category; fraud provisions; PCR formula.
- **Practice**: 3, no options.
- **Map nodes**: Asset quality: provisions, PCR, wilful defaulters.
- **Facts to verify**: provisioning percentages in the IRACP directions; wilful defaulter directions (current, and
  the change from 1 Apr 2027); fraud provisioning; accelerated provisioning (CRILC/SMA reporting).

## D05 · Interest rate risk 1: gap, NII, NIM
- **Goal**: build a repricing gap, compute the change in NII for a rate move, and compute NIM.
- **Evidence**: D-IRR seen 4/7: NII/NIM (Jun 2025 ×2 incl. case, Dec 2025), RSA/RSL and gap (Jun 2026 ×3); FP1 Q59, Q95.
- **Opening**: rates rise 1%; one bank's profit goes up, another's goes down. Same size, different gaps.
- **Misconceptions**: some think a positive gap always helps; others mix up RSA and RSL.
- **Picture**: gap bars per time bucket (assets up, liabilities down); a ruler for NII change = gap × Δrate.
- **Chapters**: 1 Rate-sensitive assets and liabilities · 2 Gap and its sign · 3 ΔNII = gap × Δr; NIM ·
  4 Earnings vs economic value; IRRBB basics. ~12 min.
- **Numericals**: 3 (gap; ΔNII; NIM).
- **Traps**: sign convention; NIM denominator (average earning assets).
- **Practice**: 3, no options.
- **Map nodes**: Interest rate risk: gap, NII, NIM.
- **Facts to verify**: IRRBB in the ALM Directions 2025.

## D06 · Interest rate risk 2: duration gap, MCLR, EBLR
- **Goal**: compute duration gap and the change in equity value; explain loan pricing benchmarks and how a policy
  rate change passes to borrowers.
- **Evidence**: "if repo and MCLR are reduced by 25 bps…" (Jul 2024); MCLR and base rate case with scenarios (Jun 2026),
  MCLR (Jun 2026).
- **Opening**: RBI cuts the repo rate. A borrower on EBLR sees his EMI fall next quarter; one on MCLR waits. Why?
- **Misconceptions**: some think MCLR changes with every repo change; others think EBLR resets daily.
- **Picture**: the see-saw (C06) placed on the balance sheet shelves; a dial for repo → EBLR → loan rate.
- **Chapters**: 1 Duration gap = DA − (L/A) × DL · 2 Change in equity value · 3 Base rate, MCLR, EBLR: how each is set
  and reset · 4 Case. ~13 min.
- **Numericals**: 3 (duration gap; Δ equity; EBLR reset).
- **Traps**: L/A weight; reset periods; spread changes.
- **Practice**: 3.
- **Map nodes**: Interest rate risk: duration gap, MCLR, EBLR.
- **Facts to verify**: interest rate on advances directions (MCLR, EBLR reset at least once in three months, spread
  rules) as in force on 31 Dec 2026.

## D07 · RAROC and economic capital
- **Goal**: compute RAROC, compare projects, and explain economic capital and risk aggregation.
- **Evidence**: D-RAROC (Jun 2023, Jul 2024 definition, Jun 2025); FP2 Q42 (two projects), Q43.
- **Opening**: two loans with the same interest rate but different risk. Which one earns more for the bank?
- **Misconceptions**: some divide by the loan amount instead of economic capital; others forget expected loss.
- **Picture**: a scale with return on one side and capital at risk on the other.
- **Chapters**: 1 Economic vs regulatory capital · 2 RAROC = (revenue − cost − EL) ÷ economic capital · 3 Using it:
  hurdle rate, pricing, capital allocation · 4 Risk aggregation and profit planning. ~11 min.
- **Numericals**: 2 (RAROC; project choice).
- **Traps**: denominator; EL subtracted; hurdle comparison.
- **Practice**: 3.
- **Map nodes**: RAROC.
- **Facts to verify**: standard definitions (no RBI number).
