Questions
1. Multi-borrower provision (keyed-in numerical): A bank branch has three NPAs on 31 March 2026: (i) Sub-standard secured ₹60 lakh; (ii) Doubtful D2 (2 years in doubtful) ₹100 lakh with realisable tangible security ₹40 lakh (book value ₹75 lakh); and (iii) Loss asset retained in books ₹25 lakh. Compute the total provision required (in ₹ lakh).
2. Interest Suspense & CGTMSE cover (keyed-in numerical): An MSME term loan in Doubtful D1 (up to 1 year) has a gross balance of ₹54 lakh and ₹4 lakh in the Interest Suspense Account. Book collateral is ₹25 lakh; after a 20% haircut and ₹2 lakh legal/realisation costs, net realisable security is ₹18 lakh. The account has valid 75% CGTMSE cover on the unsecured shortfall. Compute the total provision required (in ₹ lakh).
3. Security erosion threshold (MCQ): An NPA has a net balance of ₹80 lakh after deducting Interest Suspense. Approved valuers assess the realisable value of tangible security at ₹6 lakh. Under the RBI IRACP Directions, 2025, the bank must:
(a) retain it as sub-standard and provide 25%
(b) classify it straightaway as Doubtful and provide 100% on ₹74 lakh
(c) ignore the security and classify it straightaway as a Loss asset
(d) deduct ₹6 lakh and apply Loss Given Default (LGD) of 65%
4. Sub-standard ECGC trap (MCQ): A secured export advance of ₹100 lakh has been NPA for 7 months and carries 50% ECGC guarantee cover. The required provision under RBI IRACP Directions, 2025 is:

7. IBC 2016 CIRP basics (MCQ): Under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (as amended), which statement about Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) is correct?

8. NARCL vs regular ARC (MCQ): Which statement accurately distinguishes NARCL from a regular private ARC operating under the RBI (Asset Reconstruction Companies) Directions, 2025?
(a) NARCL is 100% private-owned, whereas regular ARCs must be 51% PSB-owned
(b) NARCL acquires stressed loans exclusively for 100% cash, whereas regular ARCs issue only Security Receipts
(c) NARCL (51% PSB-owned) acquires large stressed assets (>₹500 crore) using a 15% cash / 85% SR structure backed by a ₹30,600 crore Government of India guarantee valid for 5 years, whereas regular ARCs have no sovereign guarantee on their SRs
(d) Regular ARCs are exempt from RBI registration and Net Owned Fund requirements, whereas NARCL requires ₹300 crore NOF
Answers and reasons
   1. ₹110.00 lakh — Sub-standard secured: 60 × 15% = ₹9.00L [F9]; Doubtful D2: unsecured (100 − 40) × 100% = ₹60.00L + secured 40 × 40% = ₹16.00L = ₹76.00L [F10, F11]; Loss: 25 × 100% = ₹25.00L [F12]; Total = 9 + 76 + 25 = ₹110.00L.
   2. ₹12.50 lakh — Net balance 54 − 4 = ₹50.00L [F15]; net realisable security 25 × 0.80 − 2 = ₹18.00L [F18]; unsecured shortfall 50 − 18 = ₹32.00L; CGTMSE 75% cover 32 × 75% = ₹24.00L (0% provision) [F17]; uncovered unsecured 32 − 24 = ₹8.00L @ 100% + secured 18 × 25% = ₹4.50L [F11]; Total = 8.00 + 4.50 = ₹12.50L.
   3. (c) — Realisable security ₹6 lakh is less than 10% of ₹80 lakh (₹8 lakh), so security is ignored and the asset is classified straightaway as Loss [F8]; LGD belongs to ECL 2026 coming 1 April 2027, not IRACP [F19].
   4. (b) — Sub-standard secured advances attract a 15% general provision on total outstanding (100 × 15% = ₹15.00L) without making any allowance for ECGC cover or securities [F9].
   5. (b) — Upgrade requires satisfactory performance during the monitoring period (10% principal + capitalised interest repaid, min 1-year floor) [F22] plus investment-grade bank-loan ratings (BBB- or better) from two CRAs where aggregate exposure is ≥ ₹500 crore [F24].
   6. (b) — Restructured MSME accounts with aggregate commercial-bank exposure below ₹25 crore upgrade after satisfactory performance during the 1-year specified period from commencement of first payment of interest or principal (whichever is later) on the longest-moratorium facility [F23].
   7. (b) — Financial creditors (s. 7), operational creditors (s. 9), and corporate debtors (s. 10) may initiate CIRP [F27]; s. 14 moratorium halts parallel suits and SARFAESI, control shifts to IRP/RP and CoC [F30], and s. 12(3) imposes a mandatory 330-day outer cap [F29].
   8. (c) — NARCL (51% PSBs, paired with IDRCL) uses a 15% cash / 85% SR structure backed by a ₹30,600 crore GoI guarantee valid for 5 years [F32–F34], whereas regular ARCs under RBI ARC Directions 2025 (min NOF ₹300 crore) have no sovereign guarantee [F35].
