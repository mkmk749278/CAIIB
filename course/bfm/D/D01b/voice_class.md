The Liquidity Policy defines risk tolerance, funding diversification, short-term mismatch caps, stress tests, and the Contingency Funding Plan. The Investment Policy covers objectives, permitted securities, dealing authority, issuer and duration limits, valuation, and half-yearly Board portfolio review.
