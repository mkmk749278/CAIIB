# D01b · Mind Map Nodes (c1, 2026-11-24)
Module D Mind Map: "Reading a bank's balance sheet" — Branch 12 o'clock (shared with D01: Components, Form A/B, P&L heads → D01b: ALM, ALCO, Treasury, Policies, FTP).


Nodes added by D01b in exact order of appearance in D01b_script_class:


1. ALM (objectives, why now) — Introduced at N014 (Chapter 1)
   * Co-ordinated hub joining assets, liabilities, income, and expenses; three pillars (MIS, Organisation, Process); objectives (stable NII/NIM, MVE/Net Worth, risk within limits); five "why now" drivers; price matching vs liquidity matching; Economic Equity Ratio; gap sign & bucket placement.
2. ALCO — Introduced at N037 (Chapter 2)
   * Four-tier governance (Board → RMC → ALCO → ALM Support Group); headed by CEO/CMD or ED; implements Board strategy; decides maturity/funding mix, product pricing (incl. 1 Oct 2026 daily 10:00 am bulk deposit rate disclosure), stress tests, and FTP policy.
3. Treasury — Introduced at N044 (Chapter 2)
   * Central pool of funds & HQLA; executes ALCO decisions; supplies market pricing inputs; hedges only residual risk (why not perfect matching); derivative shields in ALM (IRS for repricing gap, FRA/IRF for future gap, Currency Swap for FX mismatch).
4. Policies — Introduced at N056 (Chapter 3)
   * Five core policies (ALM, Liquidity, Investment, Derivatives, FTP); Board-approved and reviewed at least annually; backed by SRO codes (FIMMDA for debt/Rupee IRDs; FEDAI / FX Global Code 2024 for FX).
5. FTP — Introduced at N071 (Chapter 4)
   * Funds Transfer Pricing: transfer rate components (market rate + liquidity/hedging cost + CRR/SLR reserve cost); three-way margin split (Deposit unit + Lending unit + Treasury mismatch); risk split (branches keep credit risk, Treasury holds rate & liquidity risk centrally).
