# D01b · Practice (c1, 2026-11-24)
Our own questions, written strictly from D01b_fact_sheet ([F1]–[F39]) and D01b_numericals. All names, rates, and figures are illustrative. Answers with one-line reasons at the end.


1. Under the RBI (Commercial Banks – Asset Liability Management) Directions, 2025, which body is responsible for approving the bank's Liquidity Risk Management Policy and reviewing it at least annually?
(a) Asset Liability Management Committee (ALCO)
(b) Board of Directors (or its delegated Board committee)
(c) ALM Support Group
(d) Treasury Front Office
2. Under the RBI ALM Directions, 2025, who heads the Asset Liability Management Committee (ALCO) of a commercial bank?
(a) Chief Risk Officer (CRO)
(b) Head of Treasury
(c) CEO / CMD or Executive Director (ED)
(d) Statutory Auditor
3. Effective 1 October 2026, under the RBI (Commercial Banks – Interest Rate on Deposits) Second Amendment Directions, 2026, by what time must a commercial bank disclose interest rates payable on bulk deposits (₹3 crore and above) on its website each business day?
(a) 9:00 am, with no grace period
(b) 10:00 am, with a 10-minute grace time up to 10:10 am
(c) 12:00 noon, with a 15-minute grace time
(d) 5:00 pm, before close of business
4. A bank implements Funds Transfer Pricing (FTP) across its deposit branches, lending branches, and central Treasury. After FTP is applied, who carries the credit risk and who carries the interest-rate and liquidity mismatch risk?
(a) Treasury carries credit risk; branches carry interest-rate and liquidity risk
(b) Branches carry all three risks equally
(c) Lending units carry credit risk; Treasury carries interest-rate and liquidity risk centrally
(d) ALCO carries credit risk; the Board carries liquidity risk
5. A commercial bank holds a 4-year fixed-rate corporate bond with 2 years and 4 months of remaining maturity. In the Interest Rate Sensitivity (IRS) statement under RBI ALM Directions, 2025, where is this bond slotted?
(a) Non-sensitive bucket, because its coupon is fixed
(b) 1–28 days bucket, because it can be sold anytime
(c) Over 1 year and up to 3 years bucket, based on its residual maturity
(d) Over 3 years and up to 5 years bucket, based on its original maturity
6. Numerical (Three-way FTP split): On an illustrative ₹10 crore book, a bank raises a 3-year deposit at 6.80% p.a. (3-year Deposit FTP = 7.60% p.a.) and grants a 5-year loan at 10.10% p.a. (5-year Lending FTP = 8.20% p.a.). What are the spreads earned by the Deposit Unit, Lending Unit, and Treasury respectively?
(a) 0.80%, 1.90%, and 0.60%
(b) 0.80%, 2.50%, and 0.00%
(c) 1.40%, 1.90%, and 0.00%
(d) 0.60%, 1.90%, and 0.80%
7. Numerical (Economic Equity Ratio): An illustrative bank has Owners' Funds (Net Worth) of ₹1,800 crore and Total Assets of ₹20,000 crore. What is its Economic Equity Ratio?
(a) 8.20%
(b) 9.00%
(c) 11.11%
(d) 18.00%
8. Mini Case (Mixed risks & gap sign): Market interest rates fall by 100 bps. Borrowers prepay fixed-rate 5-year loans while depositors keep fixed-rate 3-year deposits locked in at older high rates. In the 15–30 days bucket, the bank's RSA is ₹420 crore and RSL is ₹500 crore. Which statement is correct?
(a) The bucket has a positive gap of ₹80 crore and only credit risk is present
(b) The bucket has a negative gap of ₹80 crore, and the bank faces liquidity/refinancing risk, embedded-option reinvestment risk, and interest-rate risk together
(c) Perfect maturity matching would eliminate both earnings and economic value risk completely
(d) Prepayment of fixed-rate loans is a credit default event, not an embedded option
Answers (one-line reasons)
   1. (b) — The Board (or its delegated Board committee) approves the Liquidity Risk Management Policy and reviews it at least annually; ALCO implements it [F17, F21, F28].
   2. (c) — RBI ALM Directions 2025 para 25 mandates that ALCO be headed by the CEO/CMD or Executive Director [F19].
   3. (b) — From 1 Oct 2026, bulk deposit rates (₹3 crore+) must be published on the website at 10:00 am with a 10-minute grace till 10:10 am each business day [F23].
   4. (c) — FTP centralises interest-rate and liquidity mismatch risk in Treasury while leaving credit risk and customer franchise margin with lending/deposit units [F38].
   5. (c) — Rate-sensitive items are slotted by residual maturity or next repricing date, whichever is earlier; a fixed-rate item sits in its residual maturity bucket (over 1 to 3 years) [F12, F13].
   6. (a) — Deposit unit: 7.60 − 6.80 = 0.80%; Lending unit: 10.10 − 8.20 = 1.90%; Treasury: 8.20 − 7.60 = 0.60%; sum = 3.30% = 10.10 − 6.80 [F37; numericals Q1].
   7. (b) — Economic Equity Ratio = Owners' Funds ÷ Total Assets = 1,800 ÷ 20,000 × 100 = 9.00% [F15; numericals P1].
   8. (b) — Gap = RSA − RSL = 420 − 500 = −₹80 crore (negative gap), and falling rates with loan prepayments and locked deposits combine liquidity/refinancing, embedded-option, and interest-rate risks [F9, F10, F11, F14; numericals P2].
