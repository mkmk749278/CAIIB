1. Form A/B (introduced at N017, completed at N036)
   * BR Act 1949 s. 29: mandatory Balance Sheet (Form A) & P&L (Form B) in Third Schedule, as on 31 March; no current vs non-current split
   * Central Govt amends Third Schedule (≥ 3 months' notice); s. 29(2) signing; s. 30 audit (prior RBI approval); s. 31 filing (3 copies to RBI within 3 months + max 3 months ext.)
   * 16-schedule map: Form A (Sch 1–5 Sources, Sch 6–11 Uses, Sch 12 Contingent Liabilities, Bills for Collection unnumbered) & Form B (Sch 13–16 + Provisions & Contingencies)
2. Components (introduced at N039, completed at N089)
   * Sources of Funds (Sch 1–5): Sch 1 Capital (permanent, loss-absorbing, repaid last); Sch 2 Reserves (≥ 25% Statutory Reserve transfer vs 20% s. 17 floor; IFR discontinued 18 May 2026); Sch 3 Deposits (cheapest, main source; demand = highest liquidity risk; overdue FD stays in Sch 3(I)); Sch 4 Borrowings (costlier, volatile; RBI/NABARD/EXIM refinance); Sch 5 Other Liabilities & Provisions (bills payable, accrued interest, net credit IOA, Standard Asset provisions)
   * Uses of Funds (Sch 6–11): Sch 6 Cash & RBI (zero interest on 3% CRR; non-earning); Sch 7 Banks & Call/Short Notice (≤ 14 days; Call 1d / Notice 2–14d / Term 15d–1yr); Sch 8 Investments (G-Secs dominate for 18% SLR; corporate debentures sit here); Sch 9 Advances (highest yield & credit risk; net of NPA provisions; classified by facility, security, sector; rights/licences = unsecured); Sch 10 Fixed Assets & Sch 11 Other Assets (non-earning; non-banking assets max 7 yrs + 5 yrs RBI ext.; net debit IOA; non-interest-bearing staff loans)
   * Off-Balance-Sheet (dotted shelf): Sch 12 Contingent Liabilities (guarantees, LCs, forwards, DEA Fund unclaimed liabilities — attracts credit-risk capital via CCF) & Bills for Collection
3. P&L heads (introduced at N092, completed at N109)
   * Income: Sch 13 Interest Earned (advances/bills, investments, RBI/interbank; NPA cash basis only) & Sch 14 Other Income (commission/brokerage, net inv. sale/reval excl. NPI, forex, PSLC sale)
   * Expenditure: Sch 15 Interest Expended (deposits, borrowings, broken period interest expensed; NII = Sch 13 − Sch 15) & Sch 16 Operating Expenses (12 sub-heads incl. DICGC premium under Insurance, depreciation incl. leasehold & non-banking assets, municipal tax, publicity printing under Advertisement)
   * Provisions & Contingencies: separate expenditure line outside Sch 16, booked above the line (NPA, NPI, income tax provisions); reserve transfers & dividends below the line
