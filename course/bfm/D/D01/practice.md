Questions
1. Under Section 29 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, a bank's annual Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss Account must be prepared in the forms set out in the Third Schedule, where:
(a) Form A is the Profit & Loss Account and Form B is the Balance Sheet
(b) Form A is the Balance Sheet and Form B is the Profit & Loss Account
(c) Form A is the Balance Sheet with a current/non-current split and Form B is the Cash Flow Statement
(d) Both Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss Account are combined into Form A
2. Which authority has the statutory power to amend the Forms set out in the Third Schedule to the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, and with what minimum notice?
(a) Reserve Bank of India, with 30 days' notice
(b) Securities and Exchange Board of India, with 60 days' notice
(c) Central Government, by Official Gazette notification with at least three months' notice
(d) Indian Banks' Association, with prior RBI approval
3. A customer's fixed deposit of ₹25 lakh matured two months ago and has not yet been claimed or renewed. On the bank's Form A Balance Sheet, this amount must be shown under:
(a) Schedule 5 — Other Liabilities and Provisions
(b) Schedule 3 — Term Deposits
(c) Schedule 3 — Demand Deposits
(d) Schedule 12 — Contingent Liabilities
4. A commercial bank obtains ₹500 crore refinance from NABARD against its agricultural loan portfolio. How should this refinance be presented in Form A?
(a) Under Schedule 4 Borrowings, while keeping the underlying advances at their gross amount in Schedule 9
(b) Under Schedule 3 Deposits from other institutions, while keeping advances gross in Schedule 9
(c) Netted off against Schedule 9 Advances so only the bank's own-funded portion appears
(d) Under Schedule 5 Other Liabilities and Provisions
5. Under the RBI (Commercial Banks – Financial Statements: Presentation and Disclosures) Directions, 2025, where are Provisions against Standard Assets presented in Form A as at 31 December 2026?
(a) Netted off from Gross Advances in Schedule 9
(b) Shown separately under Schedule 5 (Other Liabilities and Provisions — Others)
(c) Shown under Schedule 2 (Reserves and Surplus — Revenue and Other Reserves)
(d) Shown off-balance-sheet under Schedule 12 (Contingent Liabilities)
6. A bank subscribes to ₹100 crore of listed non-convertible debentures issued by a manufacturing company, and also grants a ₹50 crore infrastructure loan secured solely by a charge over project licences and authorisations. In Form A, these appear respectively under:
(a) Schedule 9 Advances (Secured) and Schedule 9 Advances (Secured by tangible assets)
(b) Schedule 8 Investments and Schedule 9 Advances (Secured by tangible assets)
(c) Schedule 8 Investments and Schedule 9 Advances (Unsecured)
(d) Schedule 9 Advances (Unsecured) and Schedule 11 Other Assets
7. Which of the following statements about Reserves and Surplus (Schedule 2) for a commercial bank as at 31 December 2026 is CORRECT?
(a) Commercial banks need transfer only 20% of net profit to Statutory Reserve, and Investment Fluctuation Reserve is shown under Revenue and Other Reserves
(b) Commercial banks must transfer at least 25% of net profit before appropriations to Statutory Reserve, and Investment Fluctuation Reserve was discontinued w.e.f. 18 May 2026
(c) Commercial banks may freely draw down Statutory Reserve without prior RBI approval provided they report within 21 days
(d) Capital Reserves include revaluation surplus and may be freely distributed as cash dividends
8. Match the following Profit & Loss Account (Form B) items to their exact placement: (i) Deposit insurance premium paid to DICGC, (ii) Net profit on sale of investments, (iii) Provision for income tax.
(a) (i) Schedule 15, (ii) Schedule 13, (iii) Schedule 16
(b) (i) Schedule 16, (ii) Schedule 14, (iii) Provisions and Contingencies (outside Schedule 16)
(c) (i) Provisions and Contingencies, (ii) Schedule 14, (iii) Schedule 16
(d) (i) Schedule 16, (ii) Schedule 13, (iii) Schedule 16
9. Which of the following statements regarding Form B (Profit & Loss Account) is INCORRECT?
(a) Depreciation on leasehold properties and non-banking assets is booked under Schedule 16 (Operating Expenses)
(b) Printing charges for publicity brochures go under Schedule 16 (Advertisement and Publicity), not Printing and Stationery
(c) Provision for Non-Performing Investments (NPI) is netted inside Schedule 14 (Profit on revaluation of investments)
(d) Broken period interest paid on acquiring Government Securities is expensed in P&L rather than capitalised
10. Under RBI's Call, Notice and Term Money Markets Directions, unsecured interbank funds lent for 10 days fall under which category, and in which Form A schedule does the lending bank report them?
(a) Call Money — Schedule 6
(b) Notice Money — Schedule 7 (Money at Call and Short Notice)
(c) Term Money — Schedule 9 (Advances)
(d) Notice Money — Schedule 8 (Investments)
Answers (with one-line reasons)
Q#
	Key
	Rationale & Reference
	1
	b
	Under BR Act s. 29 and Third Schedule (S.O. 240(E)), Form A is the Balance Sheet (with no current/non-current split) and Form B is the Profit & Loss Account [F2, F7].
	2
	c
	Under BR Act s. 29(4), the Central Government may amend the Third Schedule by Official Gazette notification after giving not less than three months' notice [F3].
	3
	c
	Matured or overdue term deposits stay in Schedule 3 Deposits and are classified under Schedule 3(I) Demand Deposits, never Schedule 5 [F26].
	4
	a
	Refinance from RBI, NABARD, or EXIM Bank is shown under Schedule 4 Borrowings, and the underlying loans remain gross in Schedule 9 Advances [F28].
	5
	b
	Standard Asset provisions are never netted from Schedule 9 Advances; they are shown separately under Schedule 5(IV) Others [F17, F31].
	6
	c
	Corporate debentures sit in Schedule 8(I)(iv) Investments, while loans backed solely by rights, licences, or authorisations are classified as Unsecured in Schedule 9 B(iii) [F30, F32].
	7
	b
	RBI mandates at least 25% transfer of net profit to Statutory Reserve (above the 20% s. 17(1) floor), and IFR was discontinued w.e.f. 18 May 2026 and deleted from Schedule 2(IV) [F13, F14].
	8
	b
	DICGC premium sits in Schedule 16(XI) Insurance, net profit on sale of investments sits in Schedule 14(II) Other Income, and income tax provision sits in Provisions & Contingencies outside Schedule 16 [F34, F37, F38].
	9
	c
	Provision for NPI is NOT shown in Schedule 14; it is shown under Provisions and Contingencies outside Schedule 16 [F34, F38].
	10
	b
	Unsecured interbank lending for 2 to 14 days is Notice Money, reported by the lender under Schedule 7 Money at Call and Short Notice (original tenors up to and inclusive of 14 days) [F19, F20].
