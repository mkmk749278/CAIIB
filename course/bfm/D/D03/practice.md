# D03 · Practice (c1, 2026-11-24)
Our own questions, written from D03_fact_sheet (rules as at 31 December 2026). All figures illustrative. Includes 5 MCQs and 3 no-options keyed-in numericals (matching the D03 plan). Answers and one-line reasons citing [F#] are at the end.
Part A · Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs)
1. Under the RBI (Commercial Banks – IRACP) Directions, 2025, an exposure is defined as an "unsecured exposure" when the realisable value of tangible security assessed ab initio is:
   * (a) less than 25% of the outstanding exposure
   * (b) not more than 10% of the outstanding exposure
   * (c) nil, excluding State government guarantees
   * (d) less than 50% of the sanctioned limit
2. Which of the following standard-asset general provisioning rates is correctly matched as at 31 December 2026?
   * (a) Medium Enterprises — 0.25%
   * (b) Commercial Real Estate – Residential Housing (CRE-RH) — 1.00%
   * (c) Housing loans at teaser rates (before reset) — 2.00%
   * (d) Individual housing loans — 0.40%
3. A corporate borrower has an Unhedged Foreign Currency Exposure (UFCE) whose Potential Loss is 82% of its EBID. Over and above regular standard-asset provisioning and credit risk weights, the bank must apply:
   * (a) 60 bps incremental provision and no risk-weight change
   * (b) 80 bps incremental provision and a 25 percentage-point increase in risk weight
   * (c) 80 bps incremental provision and a 50 percentage-point increase in risk weight
   * (d) 100 bps incremental provision and a 25 percentage-point increase in risk weight
4. A bank detects a ₹12 crore loan fraud in Q3 (December 2026) and opts to spread the provisioning over four quarters under IRACP 2025. At financial year-end (31 March 2027), the unprovided balance of ₹6 crore must be:
   * (a) carried forward in P&L with no reserve adjustment
   * (b) debited to Statutory Reserve under Section 17(2) of the BR Act, 1949
   * (c) debited to 'other reserves' (excluding Section 17(2) reserve) by credit to provisions, and proportionately reversed to P&L in Q1–Q2 of next year
   * (d) netted directly against Gross Advances in Schedule 9
5. Which statement about the Provisioning Coverage Ratio (PCR) and Expected Credit Loss (ECL) is correct as at 31 December 2026?
   * (a) RBI mandates every commercial bank to maintain an ongoing minimum PCR of 70%
   * (b) Standard asset provisions are included in the PCR numerator
   * (c) RBI prescribes no ongoing minimum PCR today, and the ECL Directions 2026 take effect only from 1 April 2027
   * (d) Banks must use Loss Given Default (LGD) to compute IRACP provisions from 1 October 2026
Part B · Keyed-in Numericals (No Options)
6. An unsecured infrastructure loan of ₹45.00 lakh is classified as sub-standard. The bank maintains a proper cash-flow escrow mechanism and holds a clear, legal first claim on those cash flows. Calculate the minimum provision required (in ₹ lakh).
7. A consortium borrower with aggregate exposure of ₹1,800 crore is classified as sub-standard (secured) in a bank's books. No viable resolution plan is implemented within 180 days from the end of the 30-day Review Period (not yet past 365 days). Calculate the total minimum provision required (regular + additional specific provision, in ₹ crore).
8. A company has a Doubtful II (3rd year as NPA) term loan of ₹90.00 lakh, backed by realisable tangible security of ₹50.00 lakh. One of its whole-time directors appears twice on the List of Wilful Defaulters. Calculate the minimum provision required under accelerated provisioning (in ₹ lakh).
Answers & One-Line Reasons
1. (b) — IRACP25 defines "unsecured exposure" as one where realisable security value is not more than 10% ab-initio of outstanding [F19].
2. (c) — Teaser housing loans carry 2.00% (reverting to 0.40% one year after higher reset); Medium Enterprises are 0.40%, CRE-RH is 0.75%, and individual housing is 0.25% [F3, F4, F6, F7].
3. (b) — Potential Loss / EBID above 75% attracts 80 bps incremental provisioning plus a 25 percentage-point increase in risk weight [F15].
4. (c) — When 4-quarter fraud spreading crosses a financial year-end, the unprovided balance is debited to 'other reserves' (not s. 17(2) statutory reserve) and proportionately reversed to P&L in subsequent quarters [F28].
5. (c) — RBI prescribes no ongoing minimum PCR (70% was a one-time 30 Sep 2010 norm), standard provisions are excluded from PCR, and ECL Directions 2026 are effective from 1 April 2027 [F11, F38, F39, F41].
6. ₹9.00 lakh — Unsecured infrastructure sub-standard loans with cash-flow escrow and legal first claim attract 20% (45 × 20% = 9.00) [F22].
7. ₹630.00 crore — Regular sub-standard secured 15% (₹270 cr) + delayed-RP additional specific provision 20% (₹360 cr) = 35% (1800 × 35% = 630.00) [F20, F36].
8. ₹90.00 lakh — Under accelerated provisioning for repeat wilful defaulters, Doubtful II requires 100% on both secured and unsecured portions (90 × 100% = 90.00) [F34].
