# B02 practice (our own questions)

1. As per RBI's Operational Risk Guidance Note, "the variation around the prescribed risk appetite that the bank is
   willing to tolerate" is:
   a) risk capacity b) risk tolerance c) risk limit d) risk profile
   **Answer: b.** Definition (13). Capacity is the maximum the bank can assume before breaching its constraints (FSB 2013).

2. The Risk Management Committee of the Board (RMCB) of a bank must:
   a) consist mainly of whole-time directors b) meet at least once a quarter c) be chaired by the MD&CEO
   d) be chaired by the Chair of the Board
   **Answer: b.** Majority NEDs; at least once each quarter; chaired by an independent director who chairs neither the
   Board nor another committee (RBI Governance Directions 2025, para 21; para 58 for private banks).

3. Which of these is NOT allowed for a bank's Chief Risk Officer?
   a) reporting directly to the RMCB b) a fixed tenure approved by the Board
   c) a target for growth in advances d) acting as adviser to the credit sanction committee
   **Answer: c.** No business targets and no reporting line to business verticals (para 31).

4. In the three lines of defence, the compliance function typically sits in the:
   a) first line b) second line c) third line d) outside the three lines
   **Answer: b.** Second line: the independent risk function, which also typically includes compliance (RBI ORM
   Guidance Note, para 12).

5. Under COSO's 2004 ERM framework, which is one of the four categories of objectives?
   a) event identification b) reporting c) monitoring d) risk response
   **Answer: b.** Strategic, operations, reporting, compliance. The others are components.

6. A stress test that changes only the level of interest rates (a parallel shift) and nothing else is a:
   a) historical scenario b) hypothetical scenario c) sensitivity test d) reverse stress test
   **Answer: c.** One variable = sensitivity test (RBI stress testing guidelines 2007, Annex para 2).

7. Under risk-based supervision, the frequency and intensity of RBI's on-site inspection of a bank depend mainly on:
   a) the bank's size of business b) its probability of failure and the impact of failure
   c) the number of branches d) whether it is listed
   **Answer: b.** RBI HLSC report 2012.

8. A bank refuses to finance a sector it cannot assess. The risk response is:
   a) avoid b) reduce c) share d) accept
   **Answer: a.** COSO 2004: avoiding, accepting, reducing or sharing.
