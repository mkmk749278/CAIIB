# B02 map nodes

Module B map "Everything that can go wrong": B02 draws **branch 2 "Managing risk"**. The lesson map grows five nodes:

1. Process and the dial: identify · measure and assess · monitor and control · mitigate · report (RBI); RBI 1999
   parameters (organisation, policies, limits, MIS, independent risk function); credit building blocks (policy and
   strategy · organisation · operations and systems); responses avoid · reduce · share (transfer) · accept; capacity
   (what you can carry) ⊃ appetite (what you choose; Board) ± tolerance (drift); limits; appetite statement
2. The committee tree: Board → RMCB (majority NEDs; ≥ quarterly; ≥ half independent, one risk expert; independent
   chair who chairs nothing else; PSBs and private banks) → Risk Management Committee (integrated policy) → CRMC
   (Chairman / CEO / ED; CRMD independent) · ALCO (CEO / CMD / ED; ALM support group) · op risk; middle office; CRO:
   reports to MD&CEO / RMCB (RMCB meets CRO alone quarterly), no business line, no targets, no dual hatting, fixed
   tenure (Board), early exit reported to RBI DoS; in sanction: decision maker with vote (all liable) or adviser
   without vote
3. Three lines: business units own · independent risk function + compliance challenge · audit assures (staff not in
   risk processes)
4. All risks together: interdependent risks → ERM; centralise; challenges data · business pressure · culture; COSO
   2004 definition, 4 objectives (strategic · operations · reporting · compliance), 8 components; COSO 2017: 5
   components, 20 principles
5. What if, and RBS: measures sensitivity · volatility · downside (VaR); stress tests (unlikely but plausible) =
   sensitivity (one variable) + scenario (many; historical / hypothetical); results to the Board; RBI 2013 guidelines;
   RBS (HLSC 2012) vs compliance-based CAMELS: flow vs stock; intensity by probability and impact of failure, not size;
   inherent risk, controls, capital → mitigation plan; confidential
