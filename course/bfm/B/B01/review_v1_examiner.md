## Findings

1. **Medium · N064–N065 · market risk's "forms" missing.** The lesson gives Basel's definition and the four price
   variables but not RBI's line that market risk "takes the form of" liquidity, interest rate, forex, commodity and
   equity price risk (fact row 16; RMS99 7.1, re-read today). Because liquidity is drawn as its own door, a learner
   will answer Q5 wrong. Fix: one sentence after N065: "In R-B-I's older guidance, market risk takes five forms:
   liquidity, interest rate, foreign exchange, commodity and equity price risk. That's why liquidity and interest rate
   sit right next to this door." Add it to node 3 and one pause question.
2. **Medium · N064–N065, N082 · trap "market risk = price risk" from the plan not taught.** The plan lists it as a
   trap and Jun 2023 asked "Market Risk Other Name" (`items_tagged.csv`). The lesson teaches price risk only as IRR
   type five, so a learner meets two meanings with no warning. Fix: a fact row with a primary source for the alias
   (MAR10.1 "movements in market prices" supports the plain reading), then one contrast line at N082: "Don't mix
   this up with market risk as a whole, which some call price risk. R-B-I's price risk here is narrower: a bond sold
   before maturity." If no source supports the alias, record why in the script header.
3. **Medium · N043, N101 · transaction risk under operational risk (v3 addition 8; depth B2.5 "transaction risk;
   compliance (integrity) risk" under op risk).** The script uses "transaction risk" only as RBI's credit-risk sense
   (RMS99 3.1.2). A learner shown "transaction risk and compliance risk are parts of ___ risk" will pick credit.
   Fix: find the primary text for the op-risk sense (RBI operational-risk guidance) and add one line in Chapter 4:
   "Careful: 'transaction risk' has two uses. Under credit, it's default on one deal. Under operational risk, it's
   loss from failed processing of transactions." If no primary source carries it, record in the review reply why
   addition 8 is only partly done.
4. **Medium · B2.1 identification levels and "one loan, many risks" (v3 addition 3; fact row 8).** Fact row 8 (deal,
   portfolio, bank as a whole; RMS99 3.1.1 "also exposed to interest rate, forex and country risks") is not spoken.
   The morning story shows four events with several risks, but no single exposure is taken apart. Fix: in Chapter 4
   after N112, one line: "Risk is spotted at three levels: the single deal, the portfolio, the whole bank." Then one
   pause question: "One dollar loan to a borrower abroad. Name four risks." (credit, interest rate, foreign exchange,
   country). This also gives the mixed round a new form (Finding 7).
5. **Low · N107 · "acute" and "chronic" not said.** The board writes them (cue 361) but the voice only gives examples.
   Fix: "Physical risk, in two kinds. Acute, like floods, heatwaves and storms. Chronic, slow changes like rising seas."
6. **Low · Dec 2025 Q15 ("AAA-rated corporate bond … primarily subject to").** Not prepared. Fix: use it as a mixed
   round item: "Almost no chance of default, so the main risk is market risk, the price moving with rates."
7. **Low · N152–N157 · mixed round too easy and repetitive.** Replace Q3 (third basis ask) with the one-loan case
   (Finding 4) or Q6 above; keep one recall item first.
8. **Low · N020–N021 · split attributed to RBI.** RMS99's Introduction lists "financial and non-financial risks viz.,
   credit, … operational, etc." in one list; it does not assign each to a group. Fix: "R-B-I lists both financial
   and non-financial risks. Credit, interest rate … are financial. Legal, regulatory, reputational and operational
   are usually called non-financial."
9. **Low · N004 · basis-risk count.** `items_tagged.csv` shows 4 cycles, but the Dec 2025 full paper (Q60 "What is
   basis risk?") makes it 5, which is what the plan says. The fact sheet says 4. Say "four or five of the last seven
   cycles" or align both to 5 after checking.
10. **Low · N165 · scope statement.** It says the lesson covers "B two point one to two point five", but B2.4 is
    finished in B06 (CCFs) and B1.2 in B02. Fix: "B one point one and B two point one to two point five fully; B one
    point two and B two point four begin here and finish in B-zero-two and B-zero-six."
11. **Low · plan question kind "settlement risk is both credit and liquidity" not taught.** N049–N052 give only the
    principal-loss side. Add a fact row (BCBS 2013 FX settlement guidance names liquidity risk alongside principal
    risk) and half a line, or drop it from the plan's question kinds.
12. **Low · length.** 3,744 spoken words (about 26 min plus holds) against the plan's ~14.5 min; every chapter stays
    under 6 min and the owner accepts longer lessons, so no cut needed, but update the plan line. The plan's second
    misconception ("reputation risk isn't real because capital doesn't cover it") was replaced by the op-risk
    exclusion; record that in the script header.
