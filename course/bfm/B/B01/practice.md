# B01 practice (our own questions)

1. Losses above a bank's average expected level, whose timing and size cannot be known in advance, are covered
   mainly by:
   a) provisions b) pricing of loans c) capital d) the AFS-Reserve
   **Answer: c.** Unexpected loss is covered by capital; expected loss by pricing and provisions (BCBS IRB note 2005).

2. A large corporate fixed deposit is not renewed on maturity, and the bank must find the money elsewhere. This is:
   a) time risk b) funding risk c) call risk d) reinvestment risk
   **Answer: b.** Funding risk: replacing net outflows from withdrawal or non-renewal of deposits (RBI 1999, 8.2.3).

3. A bank's loans and deposits reprice on the same date, but the deposit rate rises by more than the loan rate. The
   risk is:
   a) gap risk b) basis risk c) yield curve risk d) embedded option risk
   **Answer: b.** Basis risk: rates of different items change by different magnitudes (RBI 1999, 9.3.2). Gap risk
   needs different repricing dates.

4. Under the Basel definition, operational risk excludes:
   a) legal risk b) risk from external events c) strategic and reputational risk d) risk from failed systems
   **Answer: c.** OPE10.1: includes legal risk, excludes strategic and reputational risk.

5. A borrower abroad has funds in his local currency but cannot remit them because his country restricts external
   payments. The bank faces:
   a) sovereign risk b) transfer risk c) settlement risk d) basis risk
   **Answer: b.** Transfer risk, the core of country risk (RBI Guidance Note 2002, 7.1).

6. As at 31 December 2026, which investments form a commercial bank's trading book for capital adequacy?
   a) HFT and AFS b) HFT only c) all FVTPL d) AFS and FVTPL
   **Answer: b.** Since 1 Apr 2024 the trading book is the HFT sub-category; HTM, AFS and FVTPL (non-HFT) are
   banking book (RBI circular 28 Feb 2024; same boundary in the Market Risk Directions 2026, from 1 Apr 2027).

7. Valuation gains and losses on AFS securities are taken to:
   a) the profit and loss account daily b) the AFS-Reserve c) no account; AFS is held at cost d) the IFR
   **Answer: b.** AFS is fair valued at least quarterly; net gains or losses go to the AFS-Reserve, not P&L (RBI
   Investment Directions 2025, paras 50–51). The IFR was discontinued from 18 May 2026 (para 105).

8. A bank pays away US dollars in an FX deal, but the counterparty bank fails before the euros are received. This is
   best described as:
   a) Herstatt (settlement) risk b) transfer risk c) price risk d) model risk
   **Answer: a.** Principal risk in FX settlement, "sometimes referred to as Herstatt risk" (BCBS 2013).
