# B01 map nodes

Module B map "Everything that can go wrong" (centre: the bank drawn as a house). B01 draws **branch 1 "Risk types"**
(1 o'clock). Inside the lesson, the lesson map grows six nodes in this order:

1. Risk and capital: risk = uncertainty of outcome; financial vs non-financial, all linked; expected loss → pricing
   and provisions; unexpected loss → capital; the triangle risk–capital–return (RAROC preview, D07); why manage
   risk; identify · measure · monitor · control
2. The credit door: inability or unwillingness; three levels (deal, portfolio, bank); transaction (default) vs
   portfolio (intrinsic, concentration); downgrade without default (RBI 2002: diminution in credit quality);
   counterparty (bilateral, while the deal is open); settlement / Herstatt (1974; credit and liquidity); country →
   transfer (core), sovereign, political (nationalisation), cross-border, currency; one loan, many risks
3. Market and liquidity: market = losses from price moves, on and off balance sheet; RBI 1999 forms of market risk
   (liquidity, IRR, FX, commodity, equity); liquidity = long assets, short liabilities: funding (deposits) · time
   (loans) · call (guarantees)
4. The interest-rate door: NII or market value of equity; gap (mismatch of amounts or dates) · basis (two
   rates, same maturity, different amounts) · embedded option (customer's choice; call / put on bonds) · yield curve (different
   maturities, non-parallel) · price (sell a bond early; ≠ market
   risk) · reinvestment · net interest position (float = natural hedge)
5. Operational risk and the weather: processes · people · systems · external events; legal (fines) in, strategic and
   reputational out; controls breaking → error, fraud, delay; compliance · reputational · strategic · systemic ·
   model (RBI draft) · climate (physical acute / chronic; transition)
6. Two books and the promises: intention decides; trading book = HFT (since 1 Apr 2024; same line from 1 Apr 2027,
   new capital method); HTM at cost · AFS quarterly, AFS-Reserve · FVTPL P&L · HFT daily, P&L; off-balance-sheet
   promises crystallise (LC devolves) → credit and call risk
