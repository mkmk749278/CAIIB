# B01 map nodes

Module B map "Everything that can go wrong" (centre: the bank drawn as a house). B01 draws **branch 1 "Risk types"**
(1 o'clock). Inside the lesson, the lesson map grows five nodes in this order:

1. Risk and capital: risk = uncertainty of outcome; financial vs non-financial, all linked; expected loss → pricing
   and provisions; unexpected loss → capital; the triangle risk–capital–return (RAROC preview, D07); why manage
   risk; identify · measure · monitor · control
2. The credit door: inability or unwillingness; transaction (default) vs portfolio (intrinsic, concentration);
   downgrade (migration) without default; counterparty (bilateral); settlement / Herstatt (1974); country → transfer
   (core) and sovereign
3. Market, liquidity, interest rate: market = losses from price moves, on and off balance sheet; liquidity = long
   assets, short liabilities: funding (deposits) · time (loans) · call (guarantees); IRR seven types: gap (when) ·
   basis (how much) · embedded option · yield curve · price · reinvestment · net interest position
4. Operational risk and the weather: processes · people · systems · external events; legal in, strategic and
   reputational out; compliance · reputational · strategic · systemic · model (RBI draft) · climate (physical acute /
   chronic; transition)
5. Two shelves and the promises: intention decides; trading book = HFT (since 1 Apr 2024; same line from 1 Apr 2027,
   new capital method); HTM at cost · AFS quarterly, AFS-Reserve · FVTPL P&L · HFT daily, P&L; off-balance-sheet
   promises crystallise → credit and call risk
