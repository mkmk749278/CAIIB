# B03 practice (our own questions; numericals keyed in, no options, as the plan asks)

1. Daily returns of a share over five days (%): 2, 0, −1, 3, 1. Find the standard deviation (population, ÷ n).
   **Answer: 1.4142%.** Mean 1; deviations 1, −1, −2, 2, 0; squares 1, 1, 4, 4, 0 = 10; ÷ 5 = 2; √2 = 1.4142.
2. A portfolio's annual volatility is 25%, with 250 trading days. Find its 10-day volatility.
   **Answer: 5%.** 10 ÷ 250 = 0.04; √0.04 = 0.2; 25 × 0.2 = 5.
3. The 64-day volatility of a bond index is 24%. Find its 1-day volatility.
   **Answer: 3%.** √64 = 8; 24 ÷ 8 = 3.
4. Daily volatility 1.5%, 250 trading days. Annual volatility (2 decimals)?
   **Answer: 23.72%.** √250 = 15.8114; × 1.5 = 23.7171.
5. Fund A: mean return 12%, SD 6%. Fund B: mean 9%, SD 3.6%. Which has less risk per unit of return?
   **Answer: B.** CV A = 6 ÷ 12 = 0.5; CV B = 3.6 ÷ 9 = 0.4.
6. Variance of a series of returns is 0.0004 (in decimal terms). What is the SD in percent?
   **Answer: 2%.** √0.0004 = 0.02 = 2%.
7. Mean daily cash need ₹50 lakh, SD ₹8 lakh, normal. Range covering about 95% of days?
   **Answer: ₹34 lakh to ₹66 lakh.** 50 ± 2 × 8.
