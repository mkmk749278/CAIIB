# Greetings and goodbyes used (PLAYBOOK §5b: a different one in every video)

Before writing a script, pick a greeting and a goodbye that are not on this list, then add them here.
Time-neutral by default; "good evening" / "good night" only for a lesson meant for an evening slot, never in two
videos running.

| Video | Greeting (first words) | Goodbye (last words) |
|---|---|---|
| 00 | "Hello everyone, good evening, and welcome. This is the very first class…" | "Good night everyone, see you in the next class." |
| A00 | "Hi everyone, good to see you. Welcome to the first real class of Module A." | "That's our class for today. Take care of yourselves, and I'll see you in the next one." |
| A01 | "Hello, and welcome back. Last class, I left you with one question…" | "Well done today. Rest a little, and keep that counter in your head. I'll see you soon." |
| A02 | "Good to have you here again. Settle in, and let's pick up where we stopped." | "That's it for today. Go well, and I'll see you in the next class." |
| A03 | "Welcome, everyone. Calculators ready? Today we practise." | "Good work today. Keep the calculator close, and see you next class." |
| A04 | "Hello again, everyone. Good to see you back, calculator in hand." | "That's all for today. Practise one cross rate tonight, and see you soon." |
| A05 | "Hi, everyone. Calculator out, please; today we look a little ahead." | "Thank you for staying till the end. Keep that calendar in mind, and we'll meet again soon." |
| A07 | "Hello everyone, good evening, and welcome to the class." | "Okay, good night everyone, go and rest. See you in the next class." |

Note: 00 and A07 are close to each other (both "good evening … good night"). They are not neighbours in the course
order, so they stand; change A07's lines if it is ever re-voiced.
