# A17 · Practice (c1, 2026-11-24)
Our own questions, written strictly from A17_fact_sheet.md (rules as at 31 December 2026) and A17_numericals.md. All figures illustrative. Options use real exam traps (old figures, confusable neighbouring accounts); keys spread across (a)–(d). Answers with one-line reasons at the end.
1. An Indian software engineer leaves Bengaluru on 10 February 2027 to take up a full-time job in Singapore. Under FEMA, 1999 s. 2(v), she becomes a person resident outside India: (a) on 1 April 2027, the start of the next financial year (b) immediately on leaving India for employment abroad (c) only after completing 182 days outside India (d) only after filing a declaration with RBI
2. Which of the following accounts may be held jointly by an NRI with a resident friend who is NOT a relative under Section 2(77) of the Companies Act, 2013? (a) NRE account on "former or survivor" basis (b) FCNR(B) account on "either or survivor" basis (c) NRO account on "former or survivor" basis (d) RFC account on "former or survivor" basis
3. On 15 December 2026, an NRI books a fresh 4-year FCNR(B) fixed deposit with a commercial bank in India. The applicable RBI interest rate ceiling is: (a) Overnight ARR / Swap + 500 bps (b) No ceiling, as RBI withdrew ceilings indefinitely in June 2026 (c) Comparable domestic rupee term deposit rate (d) Overnight ARR / Swap + 350 bps
4. Which statement about a Special Non-Resident Rupee (SNRR) account as on 31 December 2026 is correct? (a) It is non-interest-bearing, has no 7-year tenure cap, and may receive transfers from NRO within the USD 1 million/FY limit (b) It earns savings interest and has a strict maximum tenure of 7 years (c) Transfers from an NRO account to an SNRR account are strictly prohibited (d) Only non-residents who prove a business interest in India may open it
5. An NRI's resident brother holds a Power of Attorney (POA) on the NRI's NRE account. Which instruction must the bank refuse? (a) Paying the NRI's municipal property tax in India (b) Remitting USD 5,000 to the NRI's own bank account in Dubai (c) Transferring ₹3,00,000 as a wedding gift to the NRI's resident cousin (d) Debiting funds for an eligible Indian investment by the NRI
6. (Keyed-in numerical) An NRI places USD 25,000 in a 1-year (365-day) FCNR(B) deposit at 4.00% p.a. under RBI's prescribed convention (360-day year, compounded every 180 days plus remaining actual days). Calculate the total interest payable at maturity (in USD, to 2 decimal places).
7. (Keyed-in numerical) An NRI (no surcharge slab, no DTAA claim) earns ₹1,00,000 interest on an NRO fixed deposit in Tax Year 2026-27. How much TDS (in ₹) must the bank deduct under Section 393(2) Table Sl. No. 17 of the Income-tax Act, 2025?
8. An NRI returns permanently to India in May 2026 with a 3-year FCNR(B) deposit having 18 months left to maturity, and qualifies as RNOR under Section 6(13) of the Income-tax Act, 2025. Which treatment is correct? (a) The FCNR(B) must be broken immediately with a swap-cost penalty (b) The FCNR(B) may continue till maturity at the contracted rate, and its interest remains tax-exempt while he is RNOR (c) The FCNR(B) must be transferred to an RFC(D) non-interest-bearing current account (d) Both NRE and FCNR(B) interest remain tax-exempt for two years after return
________________
Answers (one-line reasons)
1. (b) — Leaving for employment, business, or an uncertain period makes a person non-resident immediately under FEMA s. 2(v)(i)(A) [F1].
2. (c) — NRO permits joint holding with any resident on former-or-survivor basis; NRE, FCNR(B), and RFC restrict resident joint holders to Companies Act s. 2(77) relatives [F9, F26].
3. (d) — The Dec 2024 +500 bps hike ended 31 Mar 2025 and the Jun 2026 ceiling withdrawal was cut short to 31 Aug 2026; normal ARR/Swap + 350 bps (3–5 yrs) applies on 31 Dec 2026 [F20, F21].
4. (a) — SNRR remains non-interest-bearing, but FEMA 5(R)(5)/2025-RB deleted the 7-year cap and FEMA 5(R)(6)/2026-RB (eff. 22 Jun 2026) deleted the business-interest clause and permitted NRO-to-SNRR transfers within USD 1M/FY [F8, F10, F11, F15].
5. (c) — A resident POA holder on NRE/NRO may make local payments and remit to the account holder himself, but cannot make gifts to residents or remit to third parties [F17].
6. USD 1,024.45 — First 180d: USD 500.00 (→ 25,500); next 180d: USD 510.00 (→ 26,010); remaining 5d: USD 14.45; total interest = USD 1,024.45 [F22; numericals HW].
7. ₹31,200 — NRO interest attracts 30% base tax (₹30,000) + 4% Health & Education Cess (₹1,200) = 31.2% effective TDS [F24, F33].
8. (b) — FCNR(B) may run to maturity at the contracted rate and stays tax-exempt under s. 11 Sch. IV Sl. 14 while RNOR; NRE exemption ends immediately on becoming FEMA resident [F25, F28].
