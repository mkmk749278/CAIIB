## Overall

A well-researched, tightly scoped lesson. Every core number I tested is right against the primary text: s. 4 bar, s. 6(2)
debt / s. 6(2A) non-debt since 15 Oct 2019, s. 6(4), s. 10(5) declaration and refusal in writing, s. 10(6)'s three limbs,
s. 11(3) ₹10,000 + ₹2,000 a day, s. 13(1) three times / ₹2 lakh / ₹5,000 a day after the first, s. 13(1C) up to five
years, s. 15 180 days from receipt of the application; the four capital uses and "no credit"; ODI / OPI test (unlisted,
≥ 10 % listed, control, once ODI always ODI); individual's ODI only in an operating, non-financial-services entity without
a controlled subsidiary; r. 19 real-estate activity / gambling bar and start-ups from own funds; evidence in six months
(else repatriate), 90 days for disinvestment proceeds, APR by 31 December with the under-10 %-without-control exemption;
FEMA 120/2004 superseded 22 Aug 2022; IFSC all LRS purposes since 10 Jul 2024 and the 15-day rule withdrawn 26 Apr 2023;
the rupee loan's seven conditions (minimum one year, NRO, India-only use, barred uses); R010 daily return next working
day with NIL reports; AD Cat-II / FFMC direct filing from 1 Jan 2026; employer-paid travel outside LRS; Schedule III
para 2 lines (incl. "whichever is more" for commission, ORF 4.2 over FAQ Q24); DIR 10 tie-ups without RBI approval,
AD solely responsible; AD Cat-II scope under APR26 reg 7; notes USD 3,000 / Iraq-Libya 5,000 / Iran-Russia-CIS full /
Haj; cash below ₹50,000 per journey; retention USD 2,000 notes + TCs, coins without limit; 180 days for resident
individuals and 90 / 180 for non-individuals. No High finding.

Three Medium items. (1) N019b says the five-year term is "the one prison term" under FEMA; s. 14 provides **civil
imprisonment** (up to three years where the demand exceeds ₹1 crore, six months otherwise) for not paying a penalty.
(2) N052 tells the learner "companies, firms and trusts get no jar", against its own card and the CAT Rules Schedule III
para 1 last proviso, and N053 gives the donation line without its three named purposes. (3) The homework extension,
practice 32 and fact row F61 have the ODI stake sold within the same financial year (F61: invested June, sold January),
which OI Rules r. 17(4)(ii) forbids (at least one year invested before any disinvestment); the lesson does not teach that
one-year lock-in at all.
