# A20 · Mind Map Nodes (Module A)
Nodes added to the Module A mind map ("International Banking") in exact order of appearance in A20_script_class.md, matching course/bfm/A/plans_A.md §A20:
1. Special zone: IFSC (introduced at N011, Ch 1)
2. SEZ Act, 2005 s. 18 (max 1 IFSC per SEZ); GIFT City, Gandhinagar (886 acres: 261 SEZ + 625 DTA; operational 2015).
3. Non-resident under FEMA (FEMA 339/2015-RB Reg 3), domestic laws apply unless exempted; testing ground for reforms; 6 business pillars; Income-tax Act, 2025 s. 147 (100% for 10/15 yrs), s. 70(1)(r), Schedule VI, 0% STT/CTT/GST/stamp duty.
4. IFSCA (introduced at N036, Ch 2)
5. Unified statutory regulator under IFSCA Act, 2019 (HQ Gandhinagar); replaced RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, and PFRDA inside IFSCs from 1 Oct 2020.
6. Dual compliance (IFSCA Banking Handbook + Home Regulator); IFSCA AML/CFT/KYC Guidelines 2022; NO RBI LOLR (Reg 9); NO DICGC cover in practice; no Banking Ombudsman; excluded from PSL.
7. IBU (introduced at N054, Ch 3)
8. Branch of Indian bank (non-co-op) or foreign bank licensed by IFSCA via SWIT (IBC = subsidiary, USD 50M).
9. USD 20M parent capital; Home Regulator NOC + Parent Letter of Comfort; no CRR/SLR on non-individual liabilities; 3% daily RDRR on individual deposits; LCR/NSFR at parent level with IFSCA approval; 5% single / 10% group exposure of parent Tier 1.
10. 15 specified FCYs (Jul 2024); no cash/cheques; individuals current/savings/term vs entities current/term only; separate Nostro + SNRRA for INR expenses; ring-fenced from domestic money/forex/payment systems; bulk deposit ≥ USD 500k.
11. FPIs at GIFT (introduced at N084, Ch 4)
12. Up to 23h 30m trading, twice-daily settlement, non-agri commodity derivatives, 0% STT/CTT/capital gains, SNAS & EFI due diligence.
13. SEBI 2024: up to 100% aggregate NRI/OCI/RI corpus (Option A / Option B) vs <50% standard.
14. 2024–2026 RBI updates: Jul 2024 expanded LRS to IFSC FCAs; Oct 2025 3-month exporter FCA window in IFSC; 12 Oct 2026 INR NDDCs by AD Cat-I with IBU restricted to hedging only.
