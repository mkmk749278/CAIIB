## Chapter 1 · What an IFSC Is, Why GIFT City, and Tax Reliefs

N011 · An International Financial Services Centre, or I-F-S-C, is a special financial zone carved inside a Special Economic Zone.
> 1 N: "IFSC = International Financial Services Centre (inside SEZ)" [F1] · 2 M: node "Special zone: IFSC"

N012 · Under Section eighteen of the S-E-Z Act, two thousand five, Government approves only one I-F-S-C per S-E-Z.
> 1 N: "SEZ Act 2005, s. 18: max 1 IFSC per SEZ" [F1]

N013 · Under Regulation three of FEMA three-thirty-nine, every permitted IFSC financial institution is treated as a person resident outside India.
> 1 N: "FEMA 339/2015 Reg 3: IFSC unit = person resident outside India" [F2] · B: box "resident outside India"

## Chapter 3 · Setting Up IBUs, Prudential Ratios, and What They Can and Cannot Do

N052 · Recall from Chapter two: does an I-B-U follow only I-F-S-C-A rules, or also its home regulator?
> 1 S: recall card "IBU prudential compliance?" · H: 3 s

N053 · Both: I-F-S-C-A norms plus its home regulator's directions unless I-F-S-C-A specifies otherwise.
> 1 N: "IFSCA + Home Regulator ✓" [F16]

N054 · Now let us set up Deccan Bank's I-B-U. Indian banks, excluding co-operative banks, and foreign banks apply via S-W-I-T.
> 1 N: "Eligible: Indian banks (no co-op) & Foreign banks via SWIT" [F21] · 2 M: node "IBU"

N055 · Since July twenty twenty-three, a bank may open an I-B-U branch or an I-B-C subsidiary company.
> 1 N: "IBU = branch · IBC = subsidiary (added Jul 2023)" [F22]

N056 · Write this down: minimum regulatory capital is twenty million dollars for an I-B-U at parent level, and fifty million for an I-B-C.
> 1 N: "Min capital: IBU = USD 20M (parent level) · IBC = USD 50M" [F24] · B: box "USD 20M"

N057 · Note that the old one-I-B-U-per-bank limit was omitted in July twenty twenty-three.
> 1 N: "Old 'one IBU per bank' cap omitted Jul 2023" [F23]

N058 · The parent bank must submit a home-regulator No Objection Letter and a Letter of Comfort promising liquidity support.
> 1 N: "Required: Home Regulator NOC + Parent Letter of Comfort" [F25]

N059 · Under Regulation eight, an I-B-U's non-individual liabilities are exempt from C-R-R and S-L-R.
> 1 N: "IBU non-individual liabilities: NO CRR, NO SLR" [F26]

N060 · By contrast, an I-B-C subsidiary must maintain statutory reserves under the Banking Regulation Act and R-B-I Act.
> 1 N: "IBC subsidiary: BR Act / RBI Act reserves apply" [F26]

N061 · For individual deposits, an I-B-U maintains a daily Retail Deposit Reserve Ratio, or R-D-R-R, at three percent of previous-day balances.
> 1 N: "RDRR = 3% daily on individual deposits (prev. working day)" [F27] · G: glow "3%"

N062 · L-C-R and N-S-F-R apply, and with I-F-S-C-A's prior approval, both may be maintained at parent level.
> 1 N: "LCR & NSFR: IBU level, or parent level with IFSCA approval" [F28]

N063 · Exposure ceilings are five percent of parent Tier one capital for a single borrower, and ten percent for a borrower group.
> 1 N: "Exposure: 5% single / 10% group of Parent Tier 1 capital" [F29]
