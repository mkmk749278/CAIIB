## Three things I'd remember
1. Owning or owing something abroad = capital; everything else = current. Schedule I bans, II needs a ministry, III is
   for individuals.
2. One jar: USD 250,000 per person per financial year, current and capital together; money brought back doesn't refill
   it; medical, studies and emigration can go above it on an estimate; PAN always; no bank loan for capital uses.
3. TCS is the tax law, not FEMA: nothing up to ₹10 lakh in the year; above it 2 % for education or medical, 20 % for
   the rest, nil on a bank education loan; it comes back as tax credit.
