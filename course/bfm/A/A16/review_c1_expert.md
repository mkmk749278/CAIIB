annex, and the FAQ is right: the s. 2(e)/2(j) test, the three schedules and their examples, rule 3 (Nepal / Bhutan),
who may use LRS, the USD 250,000 per FY limit for both lanes, sole proprietor, gift to any person abroad, the
resident-to-resident FC gift bar, "money back does not restore the limit", the barred list, the three above-limit cases
on estimates, card treatment (rule 7 is in RBI's Oct 2026 copy), PAN since 19 Jun 2018, Form A2 (any value, physical or
digital), designated branch, one-year relationship for capital account, no credit for capital account but allowed for
current, clubbing with the co-owner condition. TCS for FY 2026-27 (2 % / 20 % above ₹10 lakh, nil on loan-funded
education, tour 2 % from the first rupee, no-PAN 5 %) matches s. 394 as amended by Finance Bill 2026 cl. 73, and law vs
tax is kept strictly apart (TCS always "the tax law"). The sums are right and checked.

One spoken date is wrong and matters for dated questions (H1: the ₹7 lakh TCS line lasted until **31 March 2025**, not
"before October 2023"; IIBF's bank holds Jul 2024 / Dec 2024 TCS-threshold items whose answer is ₹7 lakh). Two Medium
items: the cue card says capital account is "RBI specifies (s. 6)", which is pre-2019 law (since s. 6(2A) the Central
Government rules non-debt capital account transactions; RBI keeps debt); and plan addition (14)'s "no TCS on trade or
company payments" trap is not in the lesson and no reason is recorded.
