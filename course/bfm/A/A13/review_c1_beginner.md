## Three things I'd remember
1. Import side = the bank sells: retire at bill selling (margin on margin) or the forward; crystallise at the same
   rate, no flip; interest at the bank's commercial rate.
2. Trade credit: supplier waits = suppliers' credit, bank lends = buyers' credit; up to 3 years it isn't an ECB;
   capital goods 3 years, others a year or the cycle, whichever is less; cost capped at benchmark + 300.
3. The old fixed numbers (6 months, USD 200,000 etc., 3 months' evidence) are gone; the contract, the bank's policy
   and IDPMS matching decide; no advance for gold or silver.
