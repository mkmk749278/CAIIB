N045 · Chapter three. First, from chapter two. Does an import bill flip its rate on crystallisation?

> 1 C: card "buyers' credit = an overseas bank / FI lends to the Indian importer; supplier paid at once" [F14] · 2 S: Frankfurt bank → supplier "pays now"; hospital → bank "repays later"

> 1 C: card "bill of entry = importer's declaration to Customs (home consumption or warehousing) = evidence of import" [F24]

N063 · I-D-P-M-S, the import twin of E-D-P-M-S, puts payments on one pan, bills of entry on the other.
> 1 S: two-pan scale "outward remittances | bills of entry" · 2 C: card "IDPMS = Import Data Processing and Monitoring System (2016) · twin of EDPMS (A11) · replaced the old BEF statement (after Dec 2017)" [F25]

N063a · From an E-D-I port, Customs sends the bill of entry in. From a non-E-D-I port, the bank enters it within five working days.
> 1 N: "EDI port → automatic · non-EDI → AD enters within 5 working days" [F24]

N064 · The bank debits the hospital only once it's sure the deal is genuine. The entry stays open until payment and bill of entry match.
> 1 C: card "debit only after genuineness check · update IDPMS at once · entry open till payment and bill of entry match; mark off after payment" [F25]

N065 · Small imports get an easy path. Bill of entry up to ten lakh rupees? The importer's own declaration closes it, or one declaration a quarter.
> 1 C: card "≤ ₹10 lakh → importer's declaration, or quarterly bulk declaration" [F26]

N066 · Imported services? An entry only for a business, not a resident individual, and only above ten lakh.
> 1 N: "services: IDPMS entry only if not a resident individual AND > ₹10 lakh (2nd Amendment)" [F24]
