## Overall
Accurate in almost every line. Every RBI point (Board policy and exit plans at ≥ 1 %, funded + non-funded from
domestic and foreign branches, indirect risk, own rating not better than an international agency's, yearly review,
ECGC's seven categories until then, quarterly updates on request, real-time monitoring, the 0.25–100 % table, 1 %
threshold, no provision on India, host country included, short-term "say 25 %") matches CRM 44–54 and IRP 113 word for
word. ECGC's history, ownership, three functions, risks covered and not covered, SCR terms (> ₹5 cr, whole turnover,
12 months, 90 %, 15th of the month, 30 days, 360/540 days, NCB 5 % to 50 %, from shipment, pre-shipment add-on 80 %
for manufacturer exporters under MBEP), SEP (≤ ₹5 cr, 60 days, wait halved), MEP (≤ ₹100 lakh, 90 %), ETP (≥ ₹20 lakh
premium, 90 %), SSP and BWP (80 %), MBEP (80 %, 90 % with extra premium, premium on the ALL) all match the product
pages and sales literature. ECGC terms are spoken as ECGC's, RBI rules as RBI's: the split is right throughout.
Two things need fixing before lock: the absolute "never 100 %" is contradicted by ECGC's own published SEP terms, and
the old-label trap card hides the fact that the **words "high" and "very high" moved to different grades**, which is
exactly how an exam option can catch the learner.
