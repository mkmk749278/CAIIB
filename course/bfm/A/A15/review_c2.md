RBI's old words (MC 2024 para 5.9.6; 2003 circular) against today's (IRP 113, ECGC legend):

| Letter | Old word | Today's word |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | moderate | moderately low |
| B2 | high | moderate |
| C1 | very high | moderately high |
| C2 | restricted | high |
| D | off-credit | very high |

"Moderate" moved one rung (B1 → B2), but "high" moved two (B2 → C2) and "very high" two (C1 → D), because RBI
inserted "moderately low" and "moderately high". "Lower" is also ambiguous on a ladder drawn A1 at the top. A learner
who keeps the voiced rule "one rung" and meets "high risk (older book)" works back one rung from today's C2 to C1 and
answers 25 % (the right old answer is B2, 20 %). The examples spoken next (B-two "high", C-one "very high") are right,
and the card shows both rows, so this is a framing fix, not a content fix. The N041a cue "the word tags slide one
rung" draws the same wrong picture.
