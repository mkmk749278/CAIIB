# A9 Incoterms · practice (our own questions)

1. Under Incoterms 2020, how many rules are there?
   (a) 13 (b) 11 (c) 9 (d) 12. **Answer: (b)**. 7 for any mode + 4 for sea and inland waterway. 13 was Incoterms 2000.
2. Goods sold "CIF Rotterdam Incoterms 2020" are lost at sea after loading at Chennai. Who bears the loss?
   (a) seller, who paid freight and insurance (b) buyer (c) carrier always (d) shared equally.
   **Answer: (b)**. Risk passes when the goods are on board at the port of shipment; the buyer claims on the
   insurance.
3. Which rule puts the maximum obligation on the seller? (a) EXW (b) DAP (c) DDP (d) CIP. **Answer: (c)**. The seller
   even clears import and pays duty.
4. Which Incoterms 2010 rule was renamed DPU in 2020? (a) DAF (b) DAT (c) DDU (d) DEQ. **Answer: (b)**.
5. Which of these can be used only for sea and inland waterway transport?
   (a) FCA (b) CPT (c) FOB (d) DAP. **Answer: (c)**. The four sea rules are FAS, FOB, CFR, CIF.
6. Under Incoterms 2020, the seller under CIP must, unless agreed otherwise, obtain insurance complying with
   (a) Institute Cargo Clauses (C) (b) Institute Cargo Clauses (A) (c) no insurance (d) Clauses (B).
   **Answer: (b)**. CIF stays at Clauses (C).
7. Under which rule is the seller NOT responsible for export clearance? (a) FCA (b) FAS (c) EXW (d) FOB.
   **Answer: (c)**.
8. (Keyed-in) A CIF invoice is USD 125,400 and the LC is silent on insurance. Minimum insurance cover in USD?
   **Answer: 137,940** (125400 × 1.1). UCP 600 Art. 28(f)(ii).
9. Under CPT, risk passes (a) at destination (b) when goods are handed to the first carrier (c) on unloading
   (d) on import clearance. **Answer: (b)**. A C rule: cost runs to destination, risk passes at the start.
