# A19 · Map Nodes (c1, 2026-11-24)
Module A Mind Map Branch: Borrowing from abroad Nodes added by Lesson A19 (in order of appearance in A19_script_class, matching course/bfm/A/plans_A.md §A19):
1. ECB (borrow, report, convert) — Feb 2026 Overhaul (FEMA 3(R)(5)/2026-RB, eff. 16 Feb 2026): any non-individual Indian entity under Central/State Act; any PROI / overseas RBI-regulated branch / IFSC FI lender; limit = higher of USD 1B outstanding ECB or 300% net worth (regulated FIs exempt); MAMP 3 yrs (mfg 1–3 yrs ≤ USD 150M); fixed AIC ceiling removed (≥3 yrs; <3 yrs mfg = TC ceiling +300/+250 bps); two-way currency conversion; Reg. 3A negative list (WC/GCP allowed); blanket RBI-RE guarantee bar; Form ECB 1 → LRN → Revised ECB 1 & event-based ECB 2 (7 calendar days); LSF (eff. 1 Apr 2026): ₹7,500 flat for ECB 1, ₹7,500 + 0.025%×A×n per delayed ECB 2; 70% hedging removed; parking ≤1 yr; untraceable = 4 quarters → RBI + ED; equity conversion MAMP-exempt at agreement-date FX rate.
2. ADR / GDR / IDR — ADR (US) & GDR (global): Issuer → Domestic Custodian → Foreign Depository → holder; DR Scheme 2014 (sponsored & unsponsored); limited two-way fungibility (Headroom); Form DRR in 30 days. IDR: foreign company in INR only; immediate repatriation; USD 5B cap; 1-yr lock-in; 25%/yr partial two-way fungibility; 30-day resident individual sale rule; prior sectoral regulator approval for foreign banks with Indian presence.
3. FCCB — Foreign Currency Convertible Bond: hybrid FCY debt + equity option (1993 Scheme); classified as ECB until conversion; outside FDI sectoral cap till conversion; MAMP exempt on conversion; NDI Rules pricing on conversion.
4. RDB — Rupee-Denominated Bonds overseas (Masala Bonds): INR ECB where foreign investor bears FX risk; Indian banks' 3 purposes (AT1 PDIs, Tier 2, long-term infra/affordable housing); eff. 1 Oct 2025: overseas FCY/RDB AT1 PDIs capped at 1.5% of RWAs (repealing 2021 49% cap).
