## Verdict: **ADOPT WITH FIXES**

The lesson is accurate and well aimed. Factoring has its legal definition, the three parties, the four services,
recourse with the exposure rule (incl. international → import factor, Board-approved limits), who may factor (banks
exempt; NBFC-Factor 50 / 50; NBFC-ICC), the two-factor model, TReDS and the 1988 Kalyanasundaram study group (the
Dec 2023 recalled item). Forfaiting has the aval, "without recourse", the four risks removed, the market-practice
features said as "usually", all three charges and the commitment-fee trap, URF 800, and the full comparison table
(plan v3 additions 1–4 all present). The Gold Card is one complete set of cards (eligibility, two exclusions, 3-year
limits, standby ≥ 20 %, 25 / 15 / 7 days, PCFC, waivers, rate cap). Status holders have the FTP 2023 thresholds, double
weightage and privileges. EEFC, the exporter's account abroad with the IFSC window, and the DDA are carded. The
Dec 2024 write-off item is a trap card with the exam rule on screen. Practice (30 items) keys are all correct.

What keeps it from ADOPT: (a) the **old-facility card N069** misstates the old cash-discount rule (it was
"proportionate interest on the unexpired usance", not "≤ 25 %"), and gives the old agency-commission, reduction
and refund conditions only as stubs; (b) the **DDA**, asked twice in recalled papers, gets one line, no check and no
case item, and its **old "2 years" track record** (changed 29 Apr 2025) is not shown; (c) the **EDF waiver /
free-of-cost limits** are stamped "superseded" on N069–N070 while N057 teaches the FTP's current free-of-cost limit,
and the pharma 2 % / health-programme 8 % figures are on no card; (d) the **answering rule for old figures** is on a
card (N060) but never voiced in A14; (e) the **factoring-committee line N024a is first on the cut list**, though it
carries the Dec 2023 recalled item and practice Q9.
