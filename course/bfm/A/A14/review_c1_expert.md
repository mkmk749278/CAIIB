## Overall
Rules, numbers and names are accurate. Every Gold Card figure (SAL, >10 %, 3 years, ≥ 20 %, 25 / 15 / 7 days, PCFC
preference, ≤ general export-credit rate, FCNR(B) priority) matches CF 365–383 word for word. Factoring parties,
recourse, exposure booking, bank exemption from FRA registration, NBFC-Factor 50 / 50 and NOF, TReDS and the EEFC,
exporter's FCA abroad and DDA points all match their primary texts. Status-holder thresholds, double weightage and
privileges match FTP 1.25–1.29. Market practice (80 % advance, forfaiting tenors, up to 100 %, commitment fee kept,
"short term") is spoken as "usually" and labelled on the cards: correct split between rule and practice. Old figures
(5 % / 10 % write-off, 25 % cap, agency commission, refund 3 months, A.12, A.13) are quoted correctly from the old MD
and fenced as trap cards with A13's exam rule. No superseded rule is taught as current, with one exception (finding 1),
which runs the other way: a **current** rule is taught as gone.
