## Chapter 1 · Pledge of shares: who, to whom, why, and invocation

N011 · Wrong. Under Rule nine-eight of the N-D-I Rules, four pledge routes run without R-B-I approval, verified by the A-D bank.
> 1 C: trap card right "Right: 4 routes allowed under Rule 9(8) / MD 7.11 via AD bank" [F1, F3, F6, F8]

> 1 C: card "Route 1 · ECB promoter pledge: borrower or associate resident co shares" [F1]

N014 · Now three routes for a non-resident holding Indian equity instruments or investment-vehicle units.
> 1 S: three lender cards ("Indian Bank · Overseas Bank · RBI-registered NBFC")

N015 · Route two: pledge to an Indian bank for bona-fide credit facilities to that Indian investee company.
> 1 C: card "Route 2 · Indian Bank (for Indian company's bona-fide credit)" [F3]

N016 · It needs a Statutory Auditor utilisation certificate, SEBI disclosures, and Banking Regulation Act section nineteen compliance.
> 1 N: "Indian bank: Statutory Auditor annual cert · SEBI norms · BR Act s. 19" [F4]

N017 · Section nineteen-two caps a bank's holding—even as pledgee—at thirty per cent of company paid-up capital or bank capital and reserves, whichever is less.
> 1 B: box "BR Act s. 19(2): ≤ 30% of company paid-up capital OR bank's capital & reserves (lower)" [F5]

N021 · Route four: pledge to an R-B-I-registered N-B-F-C for bona-fide credit to the Indian investee company.
