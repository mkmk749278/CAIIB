# A19c · Practice (c1, 2026-11-24)
Our own questions written from A19c_fact_sheet. All names and figures are illustrative. Answers and one-line reasons with [F#] citations are at the end.
1. A non-resident shareholder pledges equity shares of an Indian company in favour of an overseas bank. The overseas loan proceeds may be used for:
(a) subscribing to fresh equity in an Indian subsidiary
(b) genuine business purposes overseas with no direct or indirect investment in India
(c) repaying a rupee working-capital loan of the Indian investee company
(d) purchasing listed Indian shares under the FPI route
2. An RBI-registered NBFC invokes a pledge of Indian equity shares held by a non-resident and breaches its NBFC credit concentration norms. It must sell the shares and rectify the breach within:
(a) 15 days from invocation
(b) 30 days from invocation
(c) 60 days from invocation
(d) 90 days from invocation
3. When a permitted pledge of Indian equity instruments is invoked two years after creation, compliance with entry routes, sectoral caps, and pricing guidelines is tested as on the date of:
(a) invocation of the pledge
(b) creation of the pledge
(c) filing of Form FC-TRS
(d) original allotment of the shares
4. A resident individual buys repatriable equity shares of an unlisted Indian company from a Singapore investor. Who is responsible for filing Form FC-TRS, and within what time limit?
(a) The Indian investee company, within 30 days of transfer
(b) The Singapore seller, within 30 days of remittance
(c) The resident buyer, within 60 days of transfer or remittance of funds, whichever is earlier
(d) The AD Category-I bank, within 15 days of settlement
5. Following the 13 June 2026 amendment to FEMA 395/2019-RB, designated AD Category-I banks report stock-exchange purchases and transfers by individual persons resident outside India (including NRIs and OCIs) in:
(a) Form LEC (NRI)
(b) Form LEC (IFI)
(c) Form FC-TRS
(d) Form InVI
6. Keyed-in numerical: An Indian company allots equity shares worth ₹6,00,00,000 (₹6 crore) to a foreign investor and files Form FC-GPR 5 months and 5 days late. Compute the Late Submission Fee (LSF) in rupees (rounded upwards to the nearest ₹100).
7. An unlisted Indian company receives inward remittance of ₹3 crore for equity shares on 1 April, fails to allot shares within 60 days, and refunds the money on Day 85 along with delayed-refund interest under the Companies Act, 2013. Under FEMA NDI Rules, this transaction is:
(a) fully compliant because Companies Act interest was paid
(b) compliant if the AD bank issues a post-facto FIRC
(c) a contravention of NDI Rules notwithstanding payment of Companies Act interest
(d) regularised automatically by paying a flat ₹7,500 LSF
8. Which of the following entities is NOT required to file the annual FLA Return on RBI's FLAIR portal by 15 July?
(a) An unlisted Indian company with outstanding FDI as on 31 March whose accounts are still unaudited on 15 July
(b) An LLP that received foreign capital contribution in the previous financial year
(c) An Indian company that had received only foreign share application money as on 31 March with no outstanding FDI or ODI
(d) An Indian company whose non-resident shareholders exited during the current year, having held FDI at the end of the previous year
Answers and reasons
   1. (b) — Overseas-bank pledge loans must be used for genuine overseas business only, with no direct/indirect Indian investment and no capital inflow into India [F7].
   2. (b) — If invocation causes an NBFC to breach credit concentration norms, it must sell the shares and rectify the breach within 30 days [F10].
   3. (b) — Invocation transfers must follow entry routes, sectoral caps, pricing guidelines, and conditions in force at the time of creation of pledge [F12].
   4. (c) — For transfers between a repatriable PROI and a resident, the resident transferor/transferee files FC-TRS within 60 days of transfer or receipt/remittance of funds, whichever is earlier [F18].
   5. (b) — Notification FEMA 395(4)/2026-RB (13 Jun 2026) substituted Regulation 4(9) to Form LEC (IFI) for all individual PROIs including NRIs/OCIs [F27].
   6. ₹15,000 — Delay 5 months 5 days rounds upwards to 6 months (n = 6 ÷ 12 = 0.50); variable = 60000000 × 0.025% × 0.50 = ₹7,500; total = ₹7,500 + ₹7,500 = ₹15,000 (exact ₹100, below 100% of A) [F31, F32].
   7. (c) — Failure to allot within 60 days or refund within 15 days thereafter is a contravention of NDI Rules even if Companies Act interest is paid [F39, F40, F42].
   8. (c) — Entities with only share application money (and no outstanding FDI/ODI at end-March) are exempt from filing FLA; unaudited entities must still file provisional FLA by 15 July [F28, F29].
