N111 · Weaker. If the same basket of goods gets dearer faster in India than in the U-S, each rupee buys less. So over time, it's worth less against the dollar.

## Chapter 5 · Who sets the rate in India?

N126 · Before. Known early, go back. Good. Now, three regimes. A fixed rate is set officially, and the central bank holds it there. A floating rate is set by the market alone.

N128 · Two words to keep apart. An official cut in a fixed rate is a devaluation. A market fall in a floating rate is a depreciation.

N130 · Now India's road, in five posts. Post one. After the Second World War, India had a par value system, a fixed official rate, under the Bretton Woods system.
> 1 S: the road draws left to right · 2 S: post 1 "par value · Bretton Woods" [F23]

N131 · Post two. When that system broke down in nineteen seventy-one, the rupee was linked to the pound sterling.
