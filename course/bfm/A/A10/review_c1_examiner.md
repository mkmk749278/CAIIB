## Verdict: **ADOPT WITH FIXES**

The case is well built and what it teaches is right: independence and "documents, not goods" against the Jul 2024
recalled item (Arts. 4, 5), the fraud exception as the only door, five banking days counted from the day after the
documents reach the issuing bank (Art. 14(b)), insurance dated after shipment without a "cover from shipment"
clause (Art. 28(e)), the waiver that does not stop the clock and is the bank's decision (Art. 16(b)), one notice
(Art. 16(c)), complying → honour and reimburse (Arts. 15, 7(c)), transfer to two second beneficiaries only with
partial drawings allowed and only once (Art. 38(d)), the standby paid on a dated, signed statement without
investigating the default (ISP98 1.06, 4.17), and ISP98's 3/7 business days against UCP's 5. Both v3 variants are
present. All dates and weekdays are correct (Wed 12 May + 21 = Wed 2 Jun; Thu 3 Jun → day 5 Thu 10 Jun; Fri 14 May,
Tue 15 Jun, Mon 31 May, Thu 13 May all right).

The gaps are in the **"who bears / what next"** questions the plan names as the case's core: Q5's answer ("depends
on its own terms with her") gives the learner no exam-usable rule and never says the decisive point, that Chennai
need not reimburse a bank that paid against discrepant documents (Art. 7(c)); the issuing bank's own preclusion if it
misses day 5 is not applied; the obvious twist of this case (can she re-present corrected insurance? No: shipment +
21 ran out on 2 June) is missing. Two items handed to A10 by other lessons are not picked up: **legal risk** (A08's
risk card literally points "legal risk: local law vs UCP → A10") and **safeguards for banks** (A4.6, assigned
A09/A10/A13, taught nowhere). The plan's trap "Incoterm vs insurance document" is not voiced. ISP98's 3–7 line is
slightly misleading, and the ISP98 closure rule (F11, listed as a fact-sheet trap and a blueprint "compare") is not
taught. Practice P4's stem is self-contradictory.

Length: 1,208 spoken words (≈ 8.6 min at 140 wpm) + 126 s of holds ≈ **10.7 min**, inside the 10–14 band for a case;
the fixes below add about 250 words (≈ 12.5 min). No spoken sentence exceeds 25 words.

Answerable from A08–A10: **5 of 10 fully, 3 partly, 2 not.** (After fixes 1–6: 10 of 10.)
