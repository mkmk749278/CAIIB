## Verdict: **ADOPT WITH FIXES**

What the lesson teaches, it teaches correctly and in exam form: UCP binds only when the credit says so (Art. 1, FP
Q16 form), 39 vs 49 articles, "UCPDC" spelled out (Jun 2025 recalled item), the article map, five **banking** days
**following** the day of presentation with the UCP 500 "up to seven" contrast (Art. 14(b); FP Dec 2025 Q7), expiry
inside the five days does not shorten them, "need not be identical, must not conflict" (14(d)), invoice-only strict
description (14(e)/18(c)), non-documentary conditions (14(h)), documents dated before the credit (14(i)), invoice
above the credit amount (18(b)), the single complete refusal notice, waiver does not stop the clock, preclusion

practice with the 645 → 681 → 745 → 821 line, and the URR terms, independence of the authorisation, no compliance
certificate, the undertaking, three banking days, charges, and UCP 13 liability (Dec 2023 and Jun 2026 URR items).
The calendar arithmetic in the script is right (4 + 21 = 25 Mar, 11 + 21 = 1 Apr, 28 → 30 Mar; Thu 4, Thu 11, Thu

The gaps are of **range**, as in A08: (1) Art. 35, a planned v3 addition, is missing; (2) the credit's own
presentation period (the 21 days are only the default) is not said, so a common case variant is answered wrongly;

taught, and no other Module A lesson holds them; (7) when URR applies (only if the authorisation says so) is not
said; (8) practice P1's reason is wrong (its distractors are not banking-day counts). The script runs about 13.3
minutes (1,631 spoken words ≈ 11.6 min at 140 wpm + about 97 s of holds), inside the 12–15 band, with room for
