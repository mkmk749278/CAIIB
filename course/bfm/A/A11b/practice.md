**P1.** An exporter's order is CIF USD 80,000; freight and insurance USD 4,000. Taking ₹85 per dollar
(illustrative), domestic cost ₹70 lakh and a 10 % margin, and using the common bank practice of the lower of FOB value
and domestic cost less margin, the packing credit limit is:
(a) ₹61,20,000 (b) **₹58,14,000** (c) ₹63,00,000 (d) ₹68,00,000
*Reason: FOB = 76,000 × 85 = ₹64,60,000; lower than ₹70 lakh; less 10 % = ₹58,14,000. (a) uses CIF (80,000 × 85 ×
90 %); (c) uses cost. RBI itself only requires need-based limits [F24].*

**P2.** Packing credit not adjusted by export documents within 360 days from the date of advance:
(a) is automatically extended to 450 days (b) **ceases to be export credit from the date of advance** (c) becomes
post-shipment credit (d) must be reported to DGFT
*Reason: CF 235, 292 [F3]. The 450-day relief applied only to credit disbursed till 30 June 2026 [F25].*

**P3.** If the export does not take place at all, the bank charges on the packing credit:
(a) the export credit rate till repayment (b) **its domestic lending rate plus penal charges, if any, per its Board
policy** (c) no interest (d) the TT selling rate
*Reason: CF 293 [F4]. TT selling applies to PCFC not exported in 360 days [F22].*

**P4.** Which is NOT a form of post-shipment credit named in RBI's export credit directions?
(a) export bills purchased / discounted / negotiated (b) advance against bills for collection (c) advance against
duty drawback (d) **advance against a confirmed export order**
*Reason: CF 266 [F13]; (d) is packing credit [F2].*

**P5.** A usance export bill can be financed at the post-shipment stage for at most:
(a) 360 days from the advance (b) **365 days from the date of shipment, including NTP and grace** (c) 180 days
from the bill date (d) 25 days
*Reason: CF 270 [F14]. 25 days is the FEDAI NTP for a foreign-currency sight bill not under LC.*

**P6.** About PCFC, which is correct?
(a) available for deemed exports of all kinds (b) only for imported inputs (c) **for cash exports, domestic and
imported inputs, up to 360 days; if no export, adjusted at the TT selling rate** (d) must be in the invoice
currency
*Reason: CF 294, 295, 297, 304, 305 [F22].*

**P7.** A running-account packing credit facility:
(a) is open to all sub-suppliers (b) **needs a good track record (or EOU / SEZ status); bills are marked off on a
first-in, first-out basis** (c) needs a firm order before each drawal (d) has no time limit
*Reason: CF 243, 246 [F8].*
