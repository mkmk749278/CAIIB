# A18 · Practice (c1, 2026-11-24)
Our own questions, written strictly from A18_fact_sheet.md (rules as at 31 December 2026) and A18_numericals.md. All figures illustrative. Options use real exam traps (1961 Act numbers, FEMA vs IT confusion, NRE vs NRO loan differences). Answers with one-line reasons at the end.
1. A foreign national visits India on a tourist visa and stays continuously for 200 days in FY 2025-26. Under FEMA, 1999 s. 2(v), her residential status is: (a) Person resident in India, because she crossed 182 days (b) Person resident outside India, because a tourist visa lacks intention to stay for an uncertain period (c) Deemed resident, if her Indian income exceeds ₹15 lakh (d) Resident but Not Ordinarily Resident (RNOR)
2. Under the Income-tax Act, 2025 (in force 1 April 2026), an Indian citizen living in a tax-free jurisdiction with ₹18 lakh Indian-source income and 0 days in India during Tax Year 2026-27 is: (a) Non-Resident, because physical stay is 0 days (b) Resident and Ordinarily Resident (ROR) under s. 6(2) (c) Deemed Resident under s. 6(7), and automatically RNOR under s. 6(13)(c) (d) Deemed Resident under s. 6(1A), taxed on global income
3. Under Section 6(5) of the Income-tax Act, 2025, an Indian citizen or PIO visiting India whose non-foreign-source income exceeds ₹15 lakh becomes a resident (RNOR) if present in India for: (a) 60 days in the tax year + 365 days in 4 preceding tax years (b) 120 days in the tax year + 365 days in 4 preceding tax years (c) 182 days in the preceding financial year (d) 729 days in the 7 preceding tax years
4. Which of the following is expressly barred for a domestic Rupee loan in India against an NRE or FCNR(B) deposit? (a) Acquiring a flat in India for the NRI's own residential use (b) Direct investment in India on non-repatriation basis in an Indian company's capital (c) Premature withdrawal of the pledged deposit while the loan is outstanding (d) Repayment out of balances held in the borrower's NRO account
5. An NRI pledges his FCNR(B) fixed deposit in Mumbai so a resident friend's company can obtain a Rupee overdraft in India. Which pair of rules applies? (a) No direct/indirect FX consideration to the NRI, and MCLR/EBLR benchmark pricing applies (b) Free pricing exempt from MCLR/EBLR, and loan proceeds may be remitted abroad (c) Loan is capped at ₹1 crore, and the NRI may charge a 2% USD guarantee fee (d) Pledged deposit may be broken early if 25% margin is kept
6. Can an AD Category-I bank's London branch grant a GBP loan in London against an NRI's deposit held in India? (a) Yes, against NRE, FCNR(B), or NRO deposits (b) Yes, against NRE and FCNR(B) deposits, but NOT against NRO deposits (c) No, overseas branches can never lend against Indian deposits (d) Only if the borrower is an OCI cardholder
7. (Keyed-in numerical) An NRI buys a residential flat in Bengaluru costing ₹80,00,000, plus ₹5,60,000 in stamp duty and registration charges. Under RBI's resident-parity LTV ceiling (> ₹75 lakh slab = 75%), calculate the maximum permissible housing loan (in ₹).
8. Which statement regarding housing loans to NRIs/OCIs by AD banks is incorrect? (a) Quantum, margin, and repayment period are at par with resident Indians (b) The loan must be secured by equitable mortgage of the acquired property (c) EMIs may be repaid out of rental income derived from renting out that acquired property (d) The sanctioned housing loan amount may be credited directly to the borrower's NRE account
________________
Answers (one-line reasons)
1. (b) — Under FEMA s. 2(v)(i)(B) and RBI/MoF guidance, staying >182 days on a tourist/fixed-period visa lacks intention for an uncertain period, so status stays non-resident [F4].
2. (c) — Indian citizen with >₹15 lakh non-foreign income not liable to tax elsewhere is Deemed Resident u/s 6(7) (0 days needed) and automatically RNOR u/s 6(13)(c) [F9, F10].
3. (b) — s. 6(5) substitutes 120 days for 60 days in s. 6(2)(b) (120d + 365d in 4 prior years) for visiting citizens/PIOs with >₹15 lakh Indian income [F8].
4. (c) — Premature withdrawal of a pledged NRE/FCNR(B) deposit is not available while the loan runs (MD 14 Para 4.8.3) [F15].
5. (a) — Third-party loans require no direct/indirect FX consideration (MD 14 Para 4.8.1) and third-party Rupee loans against FCNR(B) are subject to MCLR/EBLR (Advances Directions 2025 Para 7) [F14, F17].
6. (b) — Overseas branches/correspondents may lend abroad against NRE/FCNR(B) deposits (MD 14 Para 4.8.2), but overseas loans against NRO are not permitted (RBI FAQ Id=52) [F16].
7. ₹60,00,000 — Stamp duty/registration are excluded from property cost above ₹10 lakh (Para 113); 80,00,000 × 75% = ₹60,00,000 [F18, F23].
8. (d) — FED MD 6 Para 4 expressly prohibits crediting the housing loan amount to the borrower's NRE/FCNR(B) account [F19].
