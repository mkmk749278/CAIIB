Many think "one-eighty-two days" settles FEMA residency. Wrong. Two intention limbs override the day count immediately, going out and coming in. Going abroad under section two-v-one-A: leave for employment, business, or an uncertain period, and you become non-resident on Day one. Chapter two: Door two, the Income-tax Act, twenty twenty-five. Recall first: under FEMA, who carries the onus of proving residential status? The individual. From one April twenty twenty-six, the Income-tax Act, twenty twenty-five replaced "previous year" with one clean term: "tax year", section three-one. Unlike FEMA, Income-tax ignores intention and counts current tax-year physical days. Under section six-two, you are resident if you meet either basic test. Test A: one-eighty-two days in that tax year. Test B: sixty days plus three-sixty-five days in four preceding tax years. Two exceptions drop the sixty-day test, leaving only one-eighty-two days: an Indian citizen leaving for employment or Indian ship crew, section six-three. And an Indian citizen or P-I-O visiting India, section six-four. Wait — there is a high-income twist under section six-five! If that visitor has non-foreign income above fifteen lakh, one-twenty days replaces sixty — plus three-sixty-five in four prior years. Meet Deemed Resident, section six-seven — formerly six-one-A: Indian citizen, Indian income above fifteen lakh, not liable to tax elsewhere. Zero days in India required! And remember the trap: never cite the repealed nineteen-sixty-one section numbers — cite six-two, six-five, six-seven, and six-thirteen. Next: R-N-O-R, section six-thirteen. Four ways in: non-resident nine of ten prior years, or up to seven-twenty-nine days in seven. Ways three and four: a high-income visitor staying one-twenty to one-eighty-one days under six-five, OR a Deemed Resident under six-seven. Both are automatically R-N-O-R! Why does R-N-O-R matter? Under section five, an R-O-R pays tax on global income; an N-R pays only on Indian income. An R-N-O-R pays on Indian income, plus foreign income only if from a business controlled in or profession set up in India. Count Vikram's days from one November to thirty-one March: thirty plus thirty-one plus thirty-one plus twenty-eight plus thirty-one equals one-fifty-one days. One-fifty-one is below one-eighty-two, and Vikram stayed only one-twenty days in four prior years. Under Income-tax he is Non-Resident — while FEMA resident! Pause check. One thing from Chapter two: Income-tax counts current tax-year days; Deemed Residents and high-income one-twenty-day visitors are R-N-O-R. Chapter three: loans to N-R-Is. Recall first: how many days in seven preceding years keeps a returning N-R-I in R-N-O-R? Seven-twenty-nine days or less. Loans against N-R-E and F-C-N-R-B in India carry no regulatory ceiling, covering fund and non-fund facilities with usual margin. Two deposit-lien bars: never mark a lien on N-R-E savings deposits, and never lend against term deposits held at another bank. Write this down — five hard rules on deposit loans. One: domestic loan proceeds can never be repatriated outside India. Two: barred end-uses — relending, real estate business, most farming and plantations. Since February twenty twenty-six, Regulation three-A adds T-D-R trading, chit funds, Nidhis, farmhouses. Three: on third-party loans in India, there must be no direct or indirect foreign exchange consideration to the non-resident depositor. Four: repay by adjusting the deposit, inward remittance, or N-R-O. Five: premature withdrawal of the pledged deposit is barred while the loan runs. What about loans outside India? Overseas branches may lend abroad against N-R-E or F-C-N-R-B deposits in India — never against N-R-O deposits! Pricing: own-deposit loans are exempt from M-C-L-R and E-B-L-R; third-party rupee loans against F-C-N-R-B must follow M-C-L-R or E-B-L-R. Resident borrower becomes N-R-I? The bank may continue the loan till maturity, repaid via inward remittance or N-R-E, F-C-N-R-B, or N-R-O. Housing loans to N-R-Is and O-C-Is. Many think N-R-Is can't borrow. Wrong — same quantum, margin, and tenor as residents. Resident-parity L-T-V slabs: up to thirty lakh, ninety percent; thirty to seventy-five, eighty percent; above seventy-five, seventy-five percent. Stamp duty excluded above ten lakh. Worked sum: eighty-lakh Pune flat, plus five point six lakh stamp duty. Above seventy-five lakh, L-T-V is seventy-five percent. Forty-eight lakh loan, twelve lakh margin. Disbursal rule: never credit the housing loan to an N-R-E or F-C-N-R-B account, and disburse linked to construction stages. Security is equitable mortgage of the property. Four repayment sources: inward remittance, N-R-E/F-C-N-R-B/N-R-O, rent from that acquired flat, or a close relative's Indian bank account. Employer staff housing loans to N-R-I staff credit to N-R-O and allow repayment only via inward remittance or N-R-E, N-R-O, or F-C-N-R-B. Pause check. One: can a pledged F-C-N-R-B deposit be broken early while the loan is active? No, premature withdrawal is barred. Two: can an N-R-I pay housing loan E-M-Is from rent earned on that flat? Yes, expressly permitted! Three, the twist: can a bank's London branch lend pounds against an N-R-O deposit in Mumbai? Never against N-R-O. Remember: deposit loans stay in India with no early break; housing loans match resident L-T-V and accept rent as repayment.
