## Chapter 2 · Door two: Income-tax Act, 2025 residency

## Chapter 3 · Loans to NRIs: against deposits and for housing

N040 · Chapter three: loans to N-R-Is. Recall first: how many days in seven preceding years keeps a returning N-R-I in R-N-O-R?
> 1 S: chapter title "3 · Loans to NRIs (A3.7 & A3.8)" · H: 3 s

N041 · Seven-twenty-nine days or less. Loans against N-R-E and F-C-N-R-B in India carry no regulatory ceiling, covering fund and non-fund facilities with usual margin.
> 1 N: "≤729d ✓" · 2 N: "Loans vs NRE/FCNR(B): no cap · usual margin · FB & NFB" [F12]

N042 · Two deposit-lien bars: never mark a lien on N-R-E savings deposits, and never lend against term deposits held at another bank.
> 1 N: "No lien on NRE SB · no loan vs other bank's TD" [F12]

N043 · Write this down — five hard rules on deposit loans. One: domestic loan proceeds can never be repatriated outside India.
> 1 B: box "Rule 1: NO repatriation of domestic loan" [F13]

N044 · Two: barred end-uses — relending, real estate business, most farming and plantations. Since February twenty twenty-six, Regulation three-A adds T-D-R trading, chit funds, Nidhis, farmhouses.
> 1 N: "Rule 2 Barred: relending, agri/plantation, real estate, TDR, farmhouses" [F13]

N045 · Three: on third-party loans in India, there must be no direct or indirect foreign exchange consideration to the non-resident depositor.
> 1 N: "Rule 3 (3rd party): NO direct/indirect FX benefit to depositor" [F14]

N046 · Four: repay by adjusting the deposit, inward remittance, or N-R-O. Five: premature withdrawal of the pledged deposit is barred while the loan runs.
> 1 N: "Rule 4 Repay: adjust deposit / inward / NRO · Rule 5: NO premature withdrawal" [F15]

N047 · What about loans outside India? Overseas branches may lend abroad against N-R-E or F-C-N-R-B deposits in India — never against N-R-O deposits!
> 1 C: trap card "Overseas loan vs NRE/FCNR(B): YES · vs NRO: NOT PERMITTED" [F16]

N048 · Pricing: own-deposit loans are exempt from M-C-L-R and E-B-L-R; third-party rupee loans against F-C-N-R-B must follow M-C-L-R or E-B-L-R.
> 1 N: "Own deposit = free pricing · 3rd-party vs FCNR(B) = MCLR/EBLR" [F17]

N049 · Resident borrower becomes N-R-I? The bank may continue the loan till maturity, repaid via inward remittance or N-R-E, F-C-N-R-B, or N-R-O.
> 1 N: "Resident loan on becoming NRI: may continue till maturity" [F17]

N050 · Housing loans to N-R-Is and O-C-Is. Many think N-R-Is can't borrow. Wrong — same quantum, margin, and tenor as residents.
> 1 B: strike "NRIs can't get housing loans?" · 2 N: "Housing loans: resident parity (ADs & HFCs)" [F18]

N051 · Resident-parity L-T-V slabs: up to thirty lakh, ninety percent; thirty to seventy-five, eighty percent; above seventy-five, seventy-five percent. Stamp duty excluded above ten lakh.
> 1 N: "LTV: ≤30L = 90% · 30–75L = 80% · >75L = 75% (no stamp duty >10L)" [F18]
