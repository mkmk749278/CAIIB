# Module A plans: video 00, A00–A22 (with A19b), AR (draft 2, v3, 2026-11-20)

One-page plan per video, in course order (`research/bfm/course_plan.md` v3, 62 videos). For the owner to review as a
module; after approval each plan moves into its video folder and the full script is written from it.

**What changed in draft 2 (2026-11-20).** Plans written for the three v3 videos (**A00**, **A19b**, **A22**). Every
Module A plan now records: its official sub-topic ids (`research/bfm/coverage.csv`); the question kinds it must
prepare for (CLAUDE.md layer 3); one line of JAIIB prerequisites (layer 6); and a numbered "**v3 additions (depth
checklist)**" line with the "Add to our plan" items from `research/bfm/depth/A.md` and the A-video rows of
`research/bfm/depth/C.md`, so each script's review can tick them off. Where a video can no longer fit 12–15 minutes,
a **Proposed split** is written inside its plan (A11, A15, A16, A19b) and lighter cases carry a "Watch" note. The
course plan is not renumbered here; the owner decides the splits.

**Rules for every plan below**
- **Rule version**: as at **31 Dec 2026** (May/June 2027 exam). Rules issued but effective later are said as
  "issued, coming". Each script states the version in its opening card.
- **Evidence**: "seen" = exam cycles (of 7, Jun 2023–Jun 2026) where the topic was recalled, from
  `research/bfm/items_tagged.csv`; "FP" = question numbers in the two full memory-based papers (`fullpaper_tagged.csv`).
- **Numbers in sums are illustrative** and are fixed in each video's `numericals.md`, solved twice, with calculator
  keys for the 8-function calculator. No answers are given here on purpose: none is final until checked.
- **Facts to verify** are checked against the primary source (RBI / FEMA / FEDAI / ICC / ECGC / IFSCA / SEBI) and
  recorded in `claims.csv` before the script is locked. Nothing below is a final fact until then. Figures quoted as
  "our file" come from `research/bfm/regulatory_updates.md` (already checked against RBI) and are re-checked at the
  fact freeze.
- **Depth checklists are not sources.** The v3 additions say *what* to cover; every rule, limit and date in them is
  taught only from the primary source named under "Facts to verify". Tags: "(reg.)" = from `regulatory_updates.md`;
  "(recall)" = from `items_tagged.csv`; "(C)" = from `depth/C.md`; untagged = `depth/A.md`.
- **Old rules taught elsewhere** (`depth/A.md`, last section: old TCS slabs, pre-2026 ECB, old AD categories, old
  export rules, RBI as IBU regulator…) appear only as trap cards ("what you may have read" vs "the rule at 31 Dec
  2026"), never as the taught rule.
- **Question kinds** (CLAUDE.md layer 3): define · purpose · who / when / how much · compare · exception · calculate ·
  apply to a case. Each plan lists the kinds its sub-topics allow, with an example; practice and the module exam test
  use them all.
- **JAIIB prerequisite**: the idea a 30-second refresher must give. The JAIIB paper named (IE&IFS, PPB, AFM, RBWM) is
  our mapping; confirm it against the JAIIB syllabus before the refresher is written.
- **Voice**: PLAYBOOK §5b (one AI teacher voice, online class, warm-up, check-ins, "some of you / others").
- Minutes are spoken length; chapters stay under 6 minutes.
