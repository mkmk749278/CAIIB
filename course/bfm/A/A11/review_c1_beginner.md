## Verdict: ADOPT WITH FIXES
High 1 · Medium 7 · Low 11. A clear, well-paced rules lesson. One story (the Tiruppur exporter, now with a cousin for
services), one picture (the export trail with a clock), a strong opening question ("nine or fifteen?") that is answered
with a light line in N057, a recall opener, a two-part pause check and a "one thing" line in every chapter, and a map
filled from memory. Most terms the brief lists are explained the moment they appear: AD bank (N011), EDF (N017),
STPI (N020), realisation, which is said as "received and brought into India" (N042), set-off, which the question

reason given for an answer (H1). **EDPMS is used and tested in chapter 2 before chapter 4 explains it** (M1). The
**old-versus-new history in N053–N055** stacks six figures by ear (M2). And **"rupees ten lakh" comes back as three
different short cuts** that I will mix up in the exam (M6).

## Three things I'd remember
1. Nine months from shipment to bring the money home; twelve if invoiced in rupees; her bank can extend.
2. DGFT gives the IEC (same as the PAN, updated every April–June); RBI wants the full value declared on the EDF.
3. Every export sits open in EDPMS until her bank closes it.
