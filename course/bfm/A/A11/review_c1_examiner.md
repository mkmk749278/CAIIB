## Verdict: **ADOPT WITH FIXES**

The lesson carries the big change correctly and clearly: the 2026 regulations from 1 Oct 2026 replace the Master
Direction, and every old figure a question bank may still hold (21 days, self write-off 5 % / status holder 10 %,
the exempt list, advance shipment 1 / 3 years, 15 months, RBI's caution list, "no set-off within a group") is shown
as a trap card beside the current rule, so a learner can answer either an old-key or a new-key item. The
realisation clock (9 months; 12 in rupees; warehouse from sale; project per contract; no SEZ period; AD extends) is
taught with a calendar and three cases, and the 9 → 15 → 9 history is exact. EDPMS (2014, XOS replaced, Customs
feed, 5 working days, ₹10 lakh closure by declaration or quarterly bulk), reduction, set-off with group companies,
and the self-acting one-year rule are all in. The recalled items (realisation of export bills, IEC, DGFT, status
holder self write-off) are all answerable.

Two old-rule traps that question banks certainly carry are missing: **SOFTEX** is never named (yet the plan,
the depth checklist and practice P2's distractor all use it), and the **old "six months at a time" extension cap**
is not shown beside "the AD extends". Manner of receipt stops at "through a bank" in a chosen currency; the
"which is a permitted mode" kind is only partly answerable. Practice P8's stem is unclear.

Length: **1,770 spoken words** (≈ 12.6 min at 140 wpm) + 119 s of holds ≈ **14.6 min**, at the top of the band.
The fixes below add about 55 words and the cuts listed remove about 45, so the lesson stays near 14.7 min. No spoken
sentence exceeds 25 words (checked by script).

Answerable from this lesson alone: **7 of 10 fully, 2 partly, 1 not.** (After fixes 1–3: 10 of 10.)
