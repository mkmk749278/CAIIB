## Verdict: **ADOPT WITH FIXES**

The lesson teaches the 2026 regime correctly and puts the old numbers on "before 1 Oct 2026" cards, which is the right
frame for the 31 Dec 2026 cut-off. No High finding. The gaps are about **questions framed on the old rules**, which a
May/June 2027 paper can still carry (the IIBF bank lags; the Dec 2024 item "Status holder exporter can self write-off"
is exactly such a question, and R26 began only three months before the cut-off). Where an old number is shown, it is
mostly on a card, not voiced in a way the learner can map to an option (write-off 5% vs 10%), and three plan v3 items
are absent or thin in voice: **SOFTEX** (never named), **exempt transactions** (only personal effects), and the
**caution-listing mechanism** (one line, no "who"). Two lines mislead slightly: N017 "Since November" (the Second
Amendment is dated 2 Nov 2026 but has effect **from 1 Oct 2026**) and "small service exporters" (resident individuals
are covered for **any** amount). N005's "export rule questions in every one of the seven cycles" overstates the
evidence (the 7/7 is the whole A-EXIMP cluster, mostly packing credit and DDA). Practice P1's reason calls the
15-month period "never-in-force", which contradicts the lesson's own trap card.

Length: 75 spoken lines, 1,480 words ≈ 10.6 min at 140 wpm + 96 s of holds ≈ **12.2 min**, at the bottom of the 12–15
band; the fixes below add about 50 s and still fit.

Evidence (A-EXIMP, 7/7 cycles, 22 mentions, rank 3; export-rule items within it): "Which is incorrect about
realization of export bill?" Dec 2023 (×2); "Directorate General Foreign Trade", "IEC Code" Jul 2024; "Status holder
exporter can self write-off export bill" Dec 2024; "Exporter" Jun 2025. No full-paper item on export rules (the FP
