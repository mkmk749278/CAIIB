# A11 · Practice (c2, 2026-11-21; c1 review fixes applied)

Our own items. Rules as at 31 Dec 2026: FEM (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026 ("R26").
Names, amounts and dates illustrative. Reasons cite `research/bfm/facts/A5.1-A5.5_A11.md` [F#].

**P1.** A Tiruppur garment exporter ships goods on **20 January 2027**, invoiced and settled in **Indian rupees**. The
export value must be realised by:
(a) 20 October 2027 (b) **20 January 2028** (c) 20 April 2028 (d) 20 July 2028
*Reason: INR-invoiced or INR-settled exports: 12 months from shipment (R26 reg 5(1), first proviso) [F25]. (a) is the
9-month rule for foreign-currency invoices; (c) is 15 months, the 2015 rules' period from 14 Nov 2025 to 4 Jun 2026; (d)
is R26's first 18-month INR text, changed before it came into force [F32].*

**P2.** Goods are shipped to a warehouse in Dubai on 10 February 2027 and sold from it on 5 May 2027. Realisation is
due within 9 months from:
(a) 10 February 2027, the shipment date
(b) **5 May 2027, the date of sale from the warehouse**
(c) 10 February 2027, with 15 months for warehouse exports
(d) the date the buyer pays
*Reason: R26 reg 5(1)(b) [F23]; the 15-month warehouse rule was the 2015 regime's [F32].*

**P3.** Under the rules at the cut-off, who may extend the export realisation period, and up to what limit?
(a) RBI only, up to 6 months
(b) the AD, up to 6 months at a time
(c) **the AD, on the exporter's reasoned request, with no cap set in the regulations**
(d) DGFT, up to one year
*Reason: R26 reg 5(1) second proviso; the AD's internal policy covers extensions [F27]. (b) is the pre-1 Oct 2026 rule
[F28].*

**P4.** A company exports IT services and receives **₹8 lakh** for one service. Which is correct?
(a) it must file an EDF with the AD within 30 days of the invoice month
(b) **it is deemed to have filed the EDF when it gives the AD the payment details**
(c) services never need an EDF
(d) it must file a SOFTEX form with STPI
*Reason: Second Amendment (2 Nov 2026, effective 1 Oct 2026): for persons other than resident individuals, deemed
filing where the inward remittance is ≤ ₹10 lakh [F9].*

**P5.** An exporter's proceeds remain unrealised **more than one year after the due date**, and the AD has not extended
the period. Under R26, the exporter:
(a) is automatically caution-listed by RBI
(b) **may make further exports only against full advance payment or an irrevocable LC**
(c) loses its IEC
(d) must write off the bill
*Reason: R26 reg 13 [F29]; automatic caution listing was withdrawn in Oct 2020, and the caution-list circulars are
superseded from 1 Oct 2026 [F40].*

**P6.** An exporter's EDPMS entry for a shipping bill of **₹6 lakh** is open though the money has come in. The AD may:
(a) close it only with RBI approval
(b) **close it on the exporter's declaration that payment has been realised, or in a quarterly bulk declaration**
(c) close it only after one year
(d) not close it, since EDPMS is closed by Customs
*Reason: R26 reg 4(2) provisos; first issued by A.P. (DIR) Circular No. 12 of 1 Oct 2025 [F38].*

**P7.** Which statement about the IEC is correct?
(a) issued by RBI, renewed every five years
(b) **issued by DGFT, the same as the entity's PAN, permanently valid but to be updated online every April–June**
(c) issued by Customs at the first shipment
(d) needed by every traveller carrying personal effects
*Reason: FTP 2.05; HBP 2.08–2.13 [F2, F3]; personal effects are not an export [F6].*

**P8.** An exporter sells garments to a Rotterdam buyer and buys dyes from the same buyer. Under R26 the AD may allow
set-off of the export receivable against the import payable:
(a) only if both are in the same calendar year and both are goods
(b) **with the same overseas buyer / supplier or its group company, within the realisation period**
(c) never; each leg must be settled gross
(d) only with RBI approval
*Reason: R26 reg 7 [F43]; the same-calendar-year and goods-vs-services conditions were the old Master Direction's [F44].*

**P9.** A freelance graphic designer, a **resident individual**, receives **₹25 lakh** from a client abroad for design
work. Which is correct at the cut-off?
(a) she must file an EDF with the AD, as the amount is above ₹10 lakh
(b) **she is deemed to have filed the EDF when she gives the AD the payment details, whatever the amount**
(c) she must file a SOFTEX form
(d) she needs RBI approval above ₹10 lakh
*Reason: Second Amendment (notified 2 Nov 2026, effective 1 Oct 2026): resident individuals, any amount [F9].*

**P10.** A status holder's export bill of USD 40,000 has been partly unrealised for a long time after a quality
dispute. Under the rules at 31 Dec 2026, the lower value can be settled by:
(a) self write-off up to 10% of last year's realisation
(b) AD write-off up to 10%, then RBI
(c) **a reduction in the export value allowed by the AD, on the exporter's reasoned request**
(d) only an RBI approval
*Reason: R26 reg 6 replaces the old write-off; (a) and (b) are the pre-1 Oct 2026 limits [F41, F42]. If every option
were an old figure, the question would be on the old rules: then self 5%, status holder 10%, AD 10%.*
