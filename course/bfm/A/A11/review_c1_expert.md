## Verdict: ADOPT WITH FIXES

The lesson teaches the right regime: at the 31 Dec 2026 cut-off for a May/June 2027 exam, the 2026 regulations
(in force 1 Oct 2026) and A.P. DIR 20 are current, and every main rule and number in the voice matches the primary
text. Treating the old MD figures as trap cards is right, and nothing called "superseded" survives elsewhere (checked
against RBI FEMA notifications to 3 Nov 2026). The fixes are about precision where an exam option would turn: "due
date" in the one-year rule, set-off only within the (extended) realisation period, two other live meanings of "15
months" in old question banks (warehouse exports, COVID 2020), a wrong description of XOS, and SOFTEX missing from the
voice. No High finding.

## Findings

### High
None.

### Medium

1. **N074, N075 card, N082, N088, P8: "a year past the due date" is ambiguous.** Reg 13 counts one year **from the due
   date of realisation** (or the extended date), not from the bill's due date. A learner who counts from a 90-day
   usance due date gets the wrong month. With N044's shipment (20 Jan 2027) the realisation due date is 20 Oct 2027,
   so the advance-or-LC rule bites only after 20 Oct 2028. Fix N074: "And if the money never comes? A full year past
   her realisation deadline, still unpaid. What happens to her next exports?" N075 card: "unrealised > 1 year after the
   **realisation due date** (or extended date) → further exports only against full advance or irrevocable LC".
   N082: "Unpaid a year past the realisation deadline." N088: "a year past the deadline, advance or L-C only". P8 stem:
   "unpaid 14 months after the end of its realisation period". (R26 reg 13.)

2. **N078–N079: "Her overdue bill … Set-off allowed? Yes" drops the condition of reg 7.** Set-off is allowed only
   "within the stipulated period for realisation … or extended period, if any, allowed by the AD". "Overdue" invites the
   reading "past the realisation period", where the answer is "not unless the AD extends first". N072 states the
   condition correctly; the pause check undoes it. Fix N078: "Two, the twist. Her bill is still inside the nine months,
   and she owes Hamburg's sister company for fabric. Set-off allowed?" N079: "Yes: group and associates are allowed,
   within the realisation period." Card N078/N079: "group ✓ · within the (extended) realisation period". P8 option (c):
   "…; set-off with the group company needs the period first extended by the AD" (or drop the set-off clause from (c)
   so the key is clean). (R26 reg 7.)

3. **N056 trap card "15 months ✗ (Nov 2025 – Jun 2026 only)" is incomplete; old banks carry two other 15-month rules.**
   (a) Old MD A.2(iii): **goods exported to a warehouse abroad: 15 months from the date of shipment** (the most likely
   old-bank item behind "15 months"); (b) A.2(ii) / A.P. DIR 27 of 1 Apr 2020: **15 months for exports made up to
   31 Jul 2020** (COVID). The 2026 rule for warehouses is 9 months from the **date of sale** (12 if INR). Fix N049 card:
   "warehouse abroad: 9 months from the date of sale ✓ · old MD: 15 months from shipment ✗". Fix N056 card: "15 months ✗
   (general rule only Nov 2025 – Jun 2026; also old warehouse rule and COVID 2020) · 9 months; INR 12 ✓ (as at 31 Dec
   2026)". Optionally one voiced clause in N049: "Older books say fifteen months from shipment. Not now." (OMD A.2(ii),
   (iii); DIR Annex A item 92; R26 reg 5(1)(b).)

4. **N036 / trap card "documents to AD in 21 days ✗" can be confused with UCP 600's 21 days.** The learner met the
   UCP 600 Art. 14(c) 21-day presentation rule in A09/A10, and that rule is fully alive for LC shipments. A bare
   "21 days ✗ superseded" invites the wrong exam answer on an LC question. Fix the card: "FEMA: documents to AD in 21
   days (old MD) ✗ · AD's own policy and SOP sets timelines ✓ · (LC presentation: UCP 600's 21 days still apply)" and
   add four words in N036: "…within twenty-one days, the old R-B-I rule." (OMD B.1(iv), B.2(ii); R26 reg 19(1)(a);
   UCP 600 Art. 14(c).)

5. **N066: XOS were not "paper statements".** XOS was the AD's statement of export bills outstanding; from 1 Oct 2013
   it was an **online bank-wide submission** (A.P. DIR 60, DIR Annex A item 68), and old XOS data was migrated into
   EDPMS in 2016 (A.P. DIR 74 of 26 May 2016, item 88). Fix N066: "Before E-D-P-M-S, banks sent R-B-I statements of
   outstanding export bills, called X-O-S. E-D-P-M-S replaced them." Card: "XOS statements of outstanding bills →
   replaced by EDPMS (2014; old XOS data moved in 2016)".

6. **SOFTEX is never said.** The plan's question kinds list "define … SOFTEX", the depth checklist A5.1 item 3 names it,
   P2 uses it as a distractor, and old banks ask "software exports are declared on the ___ form". Under the 2026 rules
   software is a service declared on the **EDF** to the AD or STPI (reg 2(1)(e), (f)(iii), Explanation); the SOFTEX
   procedure went with the MD (OMD B.5; DIR Annex A items 9, 54, 58, 60, 83). Fix N020: add "Older books call the
   software form SOFTEX; now it's the E-D-F." Card N020 adds "(old: SOFTEX form ✗)".

7. **N014: "Many of you think an I-E-C lasts forever. It doesn't."** FTP 2.05 sets no validity period and no renewal;
   an IEC is de-activated if not updated and **re-activated on updating** (2.05(e)). An exam option "IEC has no expiry /
   needs no renewal" is defensible, and the line teaches the learner to reject it. Fix N014: "Many of you think an
   I-E-C, once issued, needs nothing more. It never expires, but she must update it online every year, April to June,
   even with no change. Miss it, and it's deactivated." Card: "IEC: no renewal ✓ · update every year April–June ✓ ·
   not updated → deactivated (reactivated on updating)".

### Low

8. **N073 and fact sheet F15: group set-off is not new.** The old MD already allowed set-off with overseas group /
   associate companies from 4 Dec 2020 (OMD C.26.1, A.P. DIR 08). What the 2026 reg 7 dropped are the old MD's extra
   conditions (goods vs services not mixed; legs in the same calendar year). N073 "That's outdated now" is still true;
   make it "That's been outdated since twenty twenty." Fix F15's note: "'no group set-off' predates A.P. DIR 08 of
   4 Dec 2020; reg 7 keeps group set-off and drops the MD's goods/services and calendar-year conditions."

9. **N030: ACU wording.** MRP reg 3(2)(I)(a)(ii) (as amended Feb 2025): ACU mechanism for payments between residents of
   two participant countries, **"or as per the directions issued by the Reserve Bank"**; other transactions as for
   non-ACU countries. Card: "ACU members → ACU mechanism, or as RBI directs".

10. **N050 "no separate period for S-E-Z units any more"**: the period has been uniform for SEZ/EOU/STP/status holders
    since A.P. DIR 37 of 20 Nov 2014 (OMD A.2(i)), not since 2026. Say "There's no separate period for S-E-Z or E-O-U
    units." (drop "any more").

11. **N021 card**: add "notified 2 Nov 2026 · effective 1 Oct 2026" (Second Amendment applies back to commencement).

12. **N025, N081 "a service worth eight / six lakh"**: reg 3(2)(b) tests the **value of the inward remittance**, not the
    service value. Say "a remittance of rupees eight lakh for a service" (and in N081). P2 already says "remittance".

13. **N010 "R-B-I writes its rules"**: under FEMA the Central Government makes **rules**, RBI makes **regulations** and
    issues directions; a classic exam distinction. Say "R-B-I writes its regulations".

14. **N034 "Her bank closes the entry when she ships"**: the advance is recorded as an inward remittance in EDPMS and
    adjusted against the shipping bill when she ships (reg 18(1)(e), (g)); if no export happens and refund is not
    possible, the AD may close the advance entry on a reasoned request (18(1)(i)). Say "Her bank matches the advance to
    her shipping bill when she ships." Optional card line: "no export, no refund possible → AD may close on reasons".

15. **N032 card**: reg 4(2) says "close **or update**" (a part-payment updates). Card: "closes or updates the EDPMS entry
    at the same time".

16. **N070**: the old table also let the **AD write off 10 %** (OMD C.23.1), and the base was the previous calendar
    year's realisation. Add to the trap card: "AD 10 % · base: last calendar year's realisation".

17. **N012**: one clause for a frequent exam pair: IEC is mandatory for goods; for services it is needed only to claim
    FTP benefits (FTP 2.05(a)). Card: "services: IEC only for FTP benefits".

18. **Rule-version card**: the lesson teaches modes of receipt from the FEM (Manner of Receipt and Payment)
    Regulations, 2023; add it to the card.

19. **Outside this lesson (for the Module A session)**: `research/bfm/regulatory_updates.md` row 95 and `plans_A.md`
    §A11 ch. 3 say caution-listed exporters "stay under the 2015 rules". The text (reg 13 proviso) says they "continue to
    be governed by the **said order**" (RBI's caution-list order under 2015 reg 16) until removed. The script (N076) has
    it right; correct the two shared files so later lessons do not inherit it.

20. **Old-regime items and the exam (judgement asked for).** Teaching the 2026 regime as the rule is right: the
    regulations were in force before the 31 Dec 2026 cut-off, and IIBF's rule governs. IIBF's bank and courseware may
    still carry old figures for a cycle or two, so the learner must **recognise** each one. The lesson does this for 21
    days, 5 %/10 % write-off, exempt list, caution list, 15 months and 1 / 3 years; findings 3, 6 and 16 close the gaps
    (warehouse 15 months, COVID, SOFTEX, AD 10 %). Put the full old-vs-new table on the PDF traps page (21 days; SOFTEX;
    exempt list items; write-off 5/10/10; warehouse 15 months from shipment; advance 1 → 3 years; RBI caution list;
    group set-off conditions), and keep EDF waivers / DDA for A14 as planned. No change to the "old rule" framing.

21. **Practice**: P1, P3, P4, P5, P6, P7 keys and reasons correct (P3 cites MRP reg 3(2)(I)(a)(iii) correctly). P2 key
    correct; distractor (b) becomes teachable once finding 6 is fixed. P8: fix per findings 1 and 2.
