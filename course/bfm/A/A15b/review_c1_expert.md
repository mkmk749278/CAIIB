## Overall
Accurate, and well disciplined. Every ECIB percentage, premium rate, deadline and approval in chapters 1–2 matches
ECGC's sales literature, bonds and product pages, and is spoken as ECGC's ("E-C-G-C's cover pays…"), never as an RBI
rule; EXIM Bank's powers are cited as law (s. 10, s. 12) and its products from its own site. The confusable numbers
are each pinned to their product (66⅔ IN-PC/BI-PC; 75 IN-PS policyholder, EF, Surety, Buyer's Credit; 50–75 / 50–95 WT;
90 small-exporter WT), "whichever is earlier" is boxed and drilled with a worked date, recoveries 90:10 with the
"ECGC takes it all" trap, and the import buyer's credit vs EXIM buyer's credit direction trap is there. The fact sheet
is reliable (one small slip, L7). Nothing outdated is taught as current: old "guarantee" names, Overseas Lending and
Transfer Guarantees are history; NIRVIK and RELIEF are rightly left out. Two Medium items need a wording fix before
lock: the EXIM start date ("began in January eighty-two" vs EXIM Bank's own "commenced operations in March 1982") and
the muddled answer to the "which facility can't EXIM Bank give?" question. No High findings.
