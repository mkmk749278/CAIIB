# A1 practice (our own questions)

Rates for Q4–Q6 (illustrative): interbank USD/INR 85.40 / 85.42; TT buying 85.3573, bill buying 85.2719,
TT selling 85.5054, bill selling 85.6337.

1. In a direct quote for USD/INR given to a customer, which number does FEDAI say is quoted?
   a) dollars per rupee b) rupees per 1 or 100 units of foreign currency c) dollars per 100 rupees d) any, by bank policy
   **Answer: b.** FEDAI Rule 5.7.

2. A bank purchases an export bill and the bill is later not paid on the due date. The bank converts the foreign
   currency liability into rupees at its:
   a) TT buying rate b) bill buying rate c) TT selling rate d) bill selling rate
   **Answer: c.** Crystallisation uses TT selling (Rule 2.1(b)(iii)): reversing a purchase puts the bank on the
   selling side.

3. Proceeds of an export bill sent **on collection** are converted at:
   a) bill buying, on the date of lodging b) TT buying, after the amount is credited to the bank's Nostro
   c) TT selling d) the interbank bid
   **Answer: b.** Rule 2.5(a).

4. *(keyed-in)* A customer sends USD 6,000 for medical treatment abroad. How many rupees does the bank debit?
   **Answer: ₹5,13,032.40** (6,000 × 85.5054, TT selling, Rule 4.1). TCS, if any, is separate (lesson A16).

5. *(keyed-in)* An exporter's foreign-currency sight bill of USD 15,000 is purchased by the bank. Rupee value before
   interest?
   **Answer: ₹12,79,078.50** (15,000 × 85.2719, bill buying, Rule 2.1(a)).

6. Arrange from highest to lowest rupee rate: TT buying, bill selling, bill buying, TT selling.
   **Answer:** bill selling > TT selling > TT buying > bill buying.

7. A purchase forward contract is cancelled on its due date at the customer's request. Rate used?
   a) TT buying b) TT selling c) bill buying d) contracted rate
   **Answer: b.** Rule 6.4(ii)(a).
