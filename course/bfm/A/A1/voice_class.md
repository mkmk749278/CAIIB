T-T selling. It's a remittance going out, not an import bill. Two, the twist. A customer cancels a forward sale contract on its due date. Which rate? T-T buying. Undoing a sale, so the bank buys back. Three. An exporter has a forward contract at eighty-five point three. The bank buys his bill today. Which rate? His contract rate, eighty-five point three. A booked contract beats the board. [seriously] If you remember one thing from chapter three: undo a deal, flip sides on the T-T rung. An unpaid import bill doesn't flip.
