## The case (keyed-in answers, no options)
An exporter's bill for USD 30,000, 60 days after sight, foreign-currency, not under LC, is discounted. Bill buying
₹84.90; interest 8 % p.a. The bill is unpaid; the bank's policy crystallises 30 days after the due date at TT selling
₹85.70, with overdue interest at 10 % p.a. on the advance.

**P1.** Rupee advance and the amount credited to the exporter. *Answer: ₹25,47,000; interest for 85 days ₹47,450.96;
credited ₹24,99,549.04 [F1, F2; numericals V1].*

**P2.** Crystallised rupee liability. *Answer: 30,000 × 85.70 = ₹25,71,000 [F5; V2].*

**P3.** Overdue interest recovered on the crystallisation date. *Answer: ₹25,47,000 × 10 % × 30 ÷ 365 = ₹20,934.25
[F6; V2].*
