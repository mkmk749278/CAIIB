# CAIIB BFM: regulatory changes to teach (as of 2026-11-14)

**Which exam uses what** (IIBF rule): Nov/Dec 2026 exams use rules and developments up to **30 June 2026**;
May/June 2027 exams use them up to **31 December 2026**. Teach the rule **as it stood on the cut-off date**, and
mention rules already issued but effective later (e.g. ECL from 1 April 2027) as "issued, coming into force".

**Sources and status**: `IV` = IIBF Vision monthly newsletter (IIBF's own summary of developments; IIBF lists it as an
exam source), issue month in brackets. `RBI` = RBI Master Direction index or RBI page. `News` = secondary write-ups
(law firms, newspapers); verify against the RBI notification before a lesson uses the number. Every lesson must cite
the primary RBI/FEMA text, not this file.

## 0. The rulebook itself changed shape (affects every lesson)
- On 28 Nov 2025 RBI consolidated about 9,000 circulars into function-wise Master Directions (commercial banks: about
  32 MDs, e.g. *Commercial Banks – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy Directions, 2025*; *– Income Recognition,
  Asset Classification and Provisioning Directions, 2025*; *– Asset Liability Management Directions, 2025*; *– Cash
  Reserve Ratio and Statutory Liquidity Ratio Directions, 2025*). Instructions up to 9 Oct 2025 were carried over
  "as is". (News; RBI MD index lists them.) **Lessons cite the new MD names**, not old circular numbers.
- RBI MD index: https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx (fetched 2026-11-14; many MDs show
  "updated as on" dates after 30 June 2026, so the version as at the cut-off must be checked).
