## Module A: International Banking
- A1 Exchange Rates and Forex Business: markets, factors, exchange rate mechanism, dealing room, derivative products, RBI/FEDAI guidelines, forex arithmetic
- A2 Liberalised Remittance Scheme and other remittance facilities for residents: current vs capital account, FEMA sections, permissible/non-permissible, TCS, reporting
- A3 Correspondent Banking and NRI Accounts: Nostro/Vostro/Loro, SWIFT/CHIPS/CHAPS/RTGS, NRI rupee and foreign currency accounts, advances against NR deposits, housing loans to NRIs
- A4 Documentary Letters of Credit: types, operations, UCP 600, parties' rights, document scrutiny, crystallisation, risks, standby LC, URR 725, ISBP 745, Incoterms, case studies
- A5 Facilities for Exporters and Importers: exchange and trade control, export finance, Gold Card, EDPMS, factoring and forfaiting, import finance, IDPMS, supplier's and buyer's credit, case study on export finance
- A6 External Commercial Borrowings and Foreign Investments in India: ECB concepts and reporting, conversion to equity, eligible investors/entities/instruments, prohibited sectors, pledge of shares, NDI rules
- A7 Risks in Foreign Trade, Role of ECGC: country risk, export credit insurance, ECGC products, policies, claims
- A8 Role of EXIM Bank, RBI, Exchange Control (FEMA), FEDAI and others; short notes on ECB, ADR/GDR, FCCB
- A9 International Financial Service Centres (IFSC), GIFT City: IBUs, IFSCA, permissible activities, FPI relaxations
- A10 Technology in International Banking: digital platforms, fintech, blockchain in trade, challenges

## Module B: Risk Management
- B1 Risk and Basic Risk Management Framework
- B2 Risks in Banking Business: banking book, trading book, off-balance sheet, definitions
- B3 Risk Regulations in Banking Industry: Basel I, 1996 market risk amendment, Basel II, Basel III, capital charges (credit, market, operational), credit risk mitigation, Pillar 2, Pillar 3, CCB, leverage ratio, CCyB, SIFIs, RBS
- B4 Market Risk: framework, identification, measurement, monitoring, reporting, trading liquidity, mitigation
- B5 Credit Risk: framework, measurement, transaction and portfolio level controls, loan review, mitigation, securitisation, credit derivatives
- B6 Operational Risk and Integrated Risk Management: event types, practices, quantification, mitigation, scenario analysis
- B7 Liquidity Risk Management: drivers, types, principles, governance, ratios, stress testing, contingency funding, overseas operations, reporting to RBI
- B8 Basel III Framework on Liquidity Standards: LCR, monitoring tools, NSFR

## Module C: Treasury Management
- C1 Introduction to Treasury Management: integrated treasury, profit centre, organisation
- C2 Treasury Products: forex, money market, securities market products
- C3 International Equity and Debt Products: GDR, IDR, ECB, trade credits, rupee-denominated bonds
- C4 Funding and Regulatory Aspects: CRR, SLR, LAF, payment and settlement systems
- C5 Treasury Risk Management: supervision, market and credit risk, VaR and duration, derivatives in risk management
- C6 Derivative Products: OTC vs exchange traded, forwards, options, futures, swaps, RBI guidelines
- C7 Treasury and Asset-Liability Management: liquidity and interest rate risk, derivatives in ALM, credit derivatives, transfer pricing

## Module D: Balance Sheet Management
- D1 Components of Assets and Liabilities and their Management: ALM purpose and objectives
- D2 Capital Adequacy, Basel Norms: Pillar 1, 2, 3
- D3 Asset Classification and Provisioning Norms
- D4 Liquidity Management
- D5 Interest Rate Risk Management: sources, effects, measurement techniques, controls, IRRBB guidelines
- D6 RAROC and Profit Planning: risk aggregation, capital allocation, economic capital
