# FEDAI Rules as at 30 June 2026 (source notes for Module A forex lessons)

**Primary source**: FEDAI Rules, 10th edition, effective 1 April 2019, "updated up to 20 August 2026"
(https://fedai.org.in/UploadPopupPageFiles/FEDAI_Rules_10th_Edition_20Aug2026.pdf, fetched 2026-11-14).
Amendments listed in the document: AR 01.BC/2020 (23 Jan 2020), AR 04/2020 (2 Nov 2020), AR 01/2022 (8 Feb 2022),
AR 02/2024 (5 Apr 2024), **AR 03/2026 (7 Sep 2026)**. Only AR 03/2026 is after the 30 June 2026 cut-off; it changed
**Rule 2.5(b)** and **Rule 4.5**. Everything else below is the text in force at the cut-off. Our summary; short
quotes in quotation marks are FEDAI's words.

## The rate map (which rate for which transaction): the heart of lessons A1–A2
| Transaction | Rate FEDAI says to use | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Export bill purchased / discounted / negotiated | bill buying rate (or contracted rate); interest for normal transit or usance period recovered upfront | 2.1(a) |
| Unpaid export bill crystallised into rupee liability | **TT selling** rate; then interest for overdue period | 2.1(b)(iii)–(iv) |
| Proceeds arrive after crystallisation | **TT buying** (or contracted) rate | 2.1(c) |
| Export bill dishonoured before crystallisation | recover rupee equivalent of bill and foreign charges at **TT selling** | 2.1(d) |
| Export bill on collection / consignment proceeds | **TT buying** (or contract), converted only after credit to Nostro | 2.5(a) |
| Import bill retired | hedge-contract rate, else prevailing **bill selling** rate | 3.1(a) |
| Import bill under LC crystallised | same as retirement; per bank's stated policy | 3.1(b), 3.3 |
| Outward remittance | **TT selling** (or contract) | 4.1 |
| Inward remittance converted | **TT buying** (or contract) | 4.4 |
| Foreign currency notes, TCs, prepaid cards | buying or selling rate for that instrument at the time | 4.2 |
| Forward **purchase** contract cancelled | **TT selling** rate | 6.4(ii)(a) |
| Forward **sale** contract cancelled | **TT buying** rate | 6.4(ii)(b) |
| Cancelled before maturity | the appropriate **forward** TT rate | 6.4(ii)(c) |

Teaching picture: the bank's counter. Money the bank *receives* in foreign currency it *buys* (buying rates: TT
buying when the currency is already in its Nostro, bill buying when it pays before the money arrives). Money it
*pays out* in foreign currency it *sells* (TT selling for remittances, bill selling for import bills, which carry
extra handling). Reversing a deal is the opposite side of the counter (crystallisation and cancellation).

**Margins are not in the FEDAI Rules.** General guideline 1: banks set their own charges (not out of line with cost).
So the size of the margin is the bank's; exam sums state it. What the rules fix is *which* rate type applies.

## Quotes, dates and holidays
- Merchant quotes are **direct**: "so many Rupees and Paise for 1 unit or 100 units of foreign currency" (5.7).
  Rupee amounts may be rounded off or settled in actual paise, per bank policy (5.8).
- **Ready/cash** = same day; **value next day** = next working day; **spot** = second working day after the deal;
  **forward** = beyond spot, duration counted from the spot value date (5.2).
- Option period of delivery: at the customer's choice, **not beyond one month**; contract states first and last
  dates (5.2). In forward merchant contracts (except NDDC) the **merchant** has the option of delivery (5.5).
- Known holiday (known at least 3 working days before) → deliver the **preceding** working day; suddenly declared
  holiday → the **next** working day (1.4, 5.2). Same logic for option expiry (8.12).
- Market hours for onshore FCY/INR: 9.00 a.m. to 5.00 p.m. IST (1.1); deals beyond hours allowed with NOOP kept and
  spot rolling over at midnight (1.2). **Saturday is not a working day** for forex except individual transactions
  allowed under 1.2 (1.3).

## Export finance timing (lesson A5, A6)
- **Normal transit period (NTP)** (2.3): foreign-currency DP/sight bills not under LC **25 days**; rupee bills not under
  LC **20 days**; rupee bills under LC: reimbursement at the negotiating centre **3 days**, elsewhere in India
  **7 days**, by banks abroad **20 days**; exports to Russia with RBI state-credit reimbursement 20 days; TT
  reimbursement under LC **5 days** (plus any stated extra period); exports to countries under UN guidelines **up to
  120 days**. Fixed-due-date usance bills: NTP not applicable. A bank may vary NTP with documented reasons; finance
  beyond NTP **max 90 days from shipment**. No change in due date after purchase/discount/negotiation.
- Overdue interest if not paid within NTP (demand) or by due date (usance) (2.2(b)). Early realisation: refund
  interest for the unexpired period; recover or pay notional swap cost (2.2(c)).
- Change of tenor: interest per bank's internal guidelines plus notional swap difference (2.4).

## Early delivery, extension, cancellation (lesson A4)
- Early delivery and extension are at the bank's option (6.1). Early delivery: swap difference plus interest on
  outlay/inflow of funds (6.2, 6.5, 6.6); swap cost always recovered on early delivery, whether or not a swap is done.
- **Extension = cancel and rebook at the current rate** on or before maturity; the difference is settled (6.3).
- Cancellation loss/gain settled in lump sum or instalments, at least quarterly, not beyond maturity, under a Board-
  approved policy (6.4(i)).
- A matured contract with no instructions is cancelled by the bank **within three working days after maturity**;
  swap cost recovered; the customer **does not get** an exchange gain but **pays** any loss (unless the delay was beyond
  the customer's control and recorded) (6.4(iv)).

## Remittance service standards
- Inward remittances (4.5, **wording as amended 7 Sep 2026, after the cut-off**): inform the beneficiary on receipt,
  credit within two working days of credit advice; delay compensation **2% over the savings-bank rate** plus exchange
  loss per policy; crystallise if the beneficiary does not respond within five working days. **To do: find the pre-
  September 2026 wording of 4.5 and 2.5(b)** (AR 03/2026 or the earlier edition) before teaching exact timelines.
- Vostro-to-Vostro transfers: Form A3 within 5 working days; penalty Rs 1,000 per day beyond that; claims after 15
  days capped at Rs 10,000 (4.6).
