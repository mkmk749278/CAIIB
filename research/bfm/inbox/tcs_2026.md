# Tax Collected at Source (TCS) on LRS Remittances: FY 2026–27 (from 1 April 2026)

This document records the statutory thresholds, rates, and legal text for **Section 206C(1G) of the Income-tax Act, 1961** (as amended by Finance Act, 2025 and Finance Act, 2026 / Section 394 of the restructured direct tax code) governing **Tax Collected at Source (TCS)** on outward remittances under the Reserve Bank of India’s **Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS)** and the sale of **overseas tour programme packages**.

---

## 1. Summary Rate & Threshold Matrix (Applicable from 1 April 2026)

| Remittance Category / Purpose | Threshold (per Individual per FY across all AD Banks) | TCS Rate up to Threshold | TCS Rate Exceeding Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Education (Funded by Loan u/s 80E)** | No monetary ceiling (Exempt) | **Nil (0%)** | **Nil (0%)** |
| **Education (Self-Funded / Without Loan)** | **₹10 Lakh** | **Nil (0%)** | **2%** (on excess above ₹10 Lakh) |
| **Medical Treatment Abroad** | **₹10 Lakh** | **Nil (0%)** | **2%** (on excess above ₹10 Lakh) |
| **Overseas Tour Programme Package** | **No threshold** (Applies from first rupee) | **2%** | **2%** (Flat across entire amount) |
| **Other LRS Remittances** (Investments, gifts, maintenance, property/shares abroad) | **₹10 Lakh** | **Nil (0%)** | **20%** (on excess above ₹10 Lakh) |

*Note on Non-Furnishing of PAN*: If the remitter does not furnish a valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) / Aadhaar, TCS is collected at the higher rate specified under Section 206CC (typically double the applicable rate or 5%, whichever is higher; for 20% slab, up to 20%).

## 3. Exact Quoted Statutory Lines

### Charging Provision [Section 206C(1G)]:
> *"Every person,—*  
> *(a) being an authorised dealer, who receives an amount, for remittance out of India from a buyer, being a person remitting such amount out of India under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme of the Reserve Bank of India;*  
> *(b) being a seller of an overseas tour programme package, who receives any amount from a buyer, being the person who purchases such package,*  
> *shall, at the time of debiting the amount payable by the buyer or at the time of receipt of such amount from the said buyer, by any mode, whichever is earlier, collect from the buyer, a sum equal to the specified percentage of such amount as income-tax:"*

### Provisos on Rates & Thresholds:
> *"Provided that the authorised dealer shall not collect the sum where the amount or aggregate of the amounts being remitted by a buyer is less than ten lakh rupees in a financial year, where the amount is for the purpose of education or medical treatment, or for purposes other than overseas tour programme packages, and is not covered by the loan from any financial institution as defined in section 80E:*  
>  
> *Provided further that where the amount is remitted for the purpose of education and is out of a loan obtained from any financial institution defined in section 80E, the rate of collection of tax shall be Nil:*  
>  
> *Provided also that where the remittance is for the purpose of education (other than covered under the second proviso) or medical treatment, the rate of collection of tax shall be two percent of the amount or aggregate of the amounts in excess of ten lakh rupees in a financial year:*  
>  
> *Provided also that where the buyer purchases an overseas tour programme package, the rate of collection of tax shall be two percent of the amount paid for such package:*  
>  
> *Provided also that where the remittance is for any other purpose under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme, the rate of collection of tax shall be twenty percent of the amount or aggregate of amounts in excess of ten lakh rupees in a financial year."*

## 4. Key Rules for CAIIB BFM Teaching & Problem Solving

1. **Aggregate ₹10 Lakh Threshold**:
   - The threshold applies cumulatively across **all Authorised Dealer (AD) Category-I banks** in India in a financial year (1 April to 31 March).
   - AD banks verify prior remittances within the fiscal year through customer undertaking and the RBI's centralised LRS tracking monitoring portal.
2. **Education Loan Exemption**:
   - To claim the 0% (Nil) rate, the remittance must be made directly out of loan proceeds disbursed by an eligible financial institution under Section 80E (scheduled commercial banks, notified financial entities). If paid from personal savings, the self-funded education slab applies.
3. **Overseas Tour Programme Packages**:
   - Unlike remittances for general travel under LRS, an *overseas tour programme package* (defined as a package offering travel, stay, boarding, or other service outside India) has **no threshold**—the 2% TCS rate applies on the entire consideration from the first rupee.
4. **Nature of TCS**:
   - TCS is an **advance tax collection**, not an additional permanent levy or non-refundable fee. The remitter can claim 100% credit of the TCS against their total income tax liability or claim a refund while filing their Income Tax Return (ITR), reflected in Form 26AS / AIS.
