### Item 1: AD Category-II Thresholds under the 2026 Authorised Persons Rules

- **RBI Reference Number**: Notification No. **FEMA 401/2026-RB** (April 30, 2026) / **RBI/2026-27/78**, **A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 09**
- **Title**: *Foreign Exchange Management (Authorised Persons) Regulations, 2026*
- **Issue Date**: May 6, 2026 (Gazetted May 6, 2026; Notification dated April 30, 2026)
- **Effective Date**: May 6, 2026
- **Issued On or Before 30 June 2026?**: **YES** (Issued on 6 May 2026; fully applicable for Nov/Dec 2026 exam).
- **Exact Paragraph Quoted**:
  > *"Under the revised regulatory framework for Authorised Persons, existing Full-Fledged Money Changers (FFMCs) or registered Forex Correspondents (FxCs) who have completed at least two years of satisfactory operations with an average annual foreign exchange turnover of not less than ₹50 crore during the preceding two financial years shall be eligible to apply for upgradation to Authorised Dealer Category-II (AD Category-II). Entities granted AD Category-II authorization shall be permitted to undertake specified non-trade current account transactions as well as foreign trade transactions up to a maximum limit of ₹25 lakh per transaction. The existing money-changing franchisee arrangements shall be wound down within a transition window of two years from the effective date."*
- **Key Confirmed Thresholds**:
  - **Eligibility for AD Category-II**: Minimum 2 years of operations with **₹50 crore** average annual forex turnover in the preceding two financial years.
  - **Foreign Trade Transaction Limit**: Permitted up to **₹25 lakh per transaction**.
  - **Net Owned Funds (NOF) / Net Worth**: Single-branch FFMC renewal: **₹25 lakh**; Multiple-branch FFMC: **₹50 lakh**.
  - **Franchisee System**: No fresh franchisee licenses; existing arrangements phased out within **2 years**.

### Item 2: Dividend Directions: CET1 Bands, the 75% Cap, and the Meaning of "z"

- **RBI Reference Number**: **RBI/2025-26/387**, **DOR.ACC.REC.No.427/21.02.067/2025-26**
- **Title**: *Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Prudential Norms on Declaration of Dividend and Remittance of Profits) Directions, 2026*
- **Issue Date**: March 10, 2026
- **Effective Date**: Financial Year 2026–27 (April 1, 2026)
- **Issued On or Before 30 June 2026?**: **YES** (Issued on 10 March 2026; fully applicable for Nov/Dec 2026 exam).
- **Exact Paragraph Quoted**:
  > *"4. Eligibility and Maximum Dividend Payout Ratio:*  
  > *(a) Commercial banks incorporated in India shall be eligible to declare dividend out of the profits of a financial year only if they comply with the minimum applicable Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio and capital conservation buffer (CCB) requirements, both prior to and after accounting for the proposed dividend payout.*  
  > *(b) The dividend shall be declared only out of the Adjusted Profit After Tax (Adjusted PAT) for the relevant financial year, computed as:*  
  > $$\text{Adjusted PAT} = \text{Reported PAT} - (0.50 \times \text{Net NPA as on March 31 of the relevant financial year})$$  
  > *(c) The maximum permissible dividend payout ratio shall be determined by the bank’s CET1 capital ratio as of March 31 of the previous financial year in accordance with the regulatory capital matrix. Where CET1 is below the minimum regulatory requirement plus applicable CCB (8.0%), the permissible payout ratio shall be 0%. For CET1 ratios above 8.0% and up to 10.0%, the payout ratio shall not exceed 20%, scaling incrementally higher for higher capital buckets.*  
  > *(d) Irrespective of the capital band or surplus capital held, the aggregate dividend payout for any commercial bank in a financial year shall be strictly capped at a ceiling of 75 percent of the reported Profit After Tax (PAT).*  
  > *(e) Where capital conservation rules apply, the factor 'z' represents the capital distribution constraint multiplier defined under Basel III capital buffer standards, restricting dividend distributions to a defined percentage of eligible retained earnings as the CET1 ratio enters lower quartiles of the Capital Conservation Buffer (CCB).'*

### Item 3: Final Commercial-Bank Text Removing the Investment Fluctuation Reserve (IFR)

- **RBI Reference Number**: **RBI/2026-27/83**, **DOR.MRG.REC.No.71/00-00-001/2026-27**
- **Title**: *Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Classification, Valuation, and Operation of Investment Portfolio) Second Amendment Directions, 2026*
- **Issue Date**: May 18, 2026
- **Effective Date**: May 18, 2026
- **Issued On or Before 30 June 2026?**: **YES** (Issued on 18 May 2026; fully applicable for Nov/Dec 2026 exam).
- **Exact Paragraph Quoted**:
  > *"Consequent upon the comprehensive review of prudential norms governing investment portfolio classification and valuation:*  
  > *(1) The mandatory requirement of creating and maintaining an Investment Fluctuation Reserve (IFR) under Paragraph 105 of the Principal Directions stands discontinued with effect from May 18, 2026. Paragraphs 106, 107, and 108 of the Principal Directions are hereby omitted.*  
  > *(2) The balance outstanding in the Investment Fluctuation Reserve of commercial banks as at the close of business on May 17, 2026 shall be transferred 'below the line' to the Statutory Reserve, General Reserve, or the Balance of Profit & Loss Account.*  
  > *(3) In the case of foreign banks operating in India in branch mode, the outstanding balance in the IFR shall be transferred directly to statutory reserves kept in Indian books or to remittable surplus retained in Indian books which is not repatriable so long as the bank functions in India."*

### Item 4: Quarterly Profits in CET1 and General Provisions in Tier 2 Amendments

#### Part A: Inclusion of Quarterly / Interim Profits in CET1 Capital
- **RBI Reference Number**: **RBI/2026-27/79**, **DOR.CAP.REC.No.68/21.01.002/2026-27**
- **Title**: *Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Fifth Amendment Directions, 2026*
- **Issue Date**: May 8, 2026
- **Effective Date**: May 8, 2026
- **Issued On or Before 30 June 2026?**: **YES** (Issued on 8 May 2026; fully applicable for Nov/Dec 2026 exam).
- **Exact Paragraph Quoted**:
  > *"Paragraph 14(i)(b) of the Principal Directions is amended as follows:*  
  > *(1) Banks may reckon interim or quarterly profits of the current financial year in Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital for CRAR computation without requiring incremental NPA provisions from the previous financial year to satisfy the erstwhile 25% deviation limitation test, provided that:*  
  > *(a) The quarterly financial results have been audited or subjected to limited review by statutory auditors; and*  
  > *(b) The eligible profit ($EP_t$) reckoned for quarter '$t$' ($t = 1, 2, 3, 4$) is calculated using the formula:*  
  > $$EP_t = NP_t - 0.25 \times D \times t$$  
  > *where $NP_t$ is the cumulative net profit up to quarter $t$, and $D$ is the average dividend paid by the bank during the preceding three financial years. Any cumulative net loss up to the quarter-end shall be deducted in full from CET1 capital."*

#### Part B: General Provisions on Standard Assets in Tier 2 Capital
- **RBI Reference Number**: **RBI/2026-27/72**, **DOR.CAP.REC.No.61/21.01.002/2026-27**
- **Title**: *Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Fourth Amendment Directions, 2026*
- **Issue Date**: April 27, 2026
- **Effective Date**: April 1, 2027 (consequential to Expected Credit Loss framework implementation)
- **Issued On or Before 30 June 2026?**: **YES** (Issued on 27 April 2026; taught as *"issued before cut-off, coming into force from 1 April 2027"*).
- **Exact Paragraph Quoted**:
  > *"Paragraph 21(i)(a) shall be modified as below:*  
  > 'Provisions or loan-loss reserves held against future, presently unidentified losses, which are freely available to meet losses which subsequently materialise, shall qualify for inclusion within Tier 2 capital. Accordingly, general provisions on standard assets (i.e. Stage 1 or Stage 2 assets), and any excess provisions which arise on account of sale of NPAs shall qualify for inclusion in Tier 2 capital. However, these items together shall be admitted as Tier 2 capital up to a maximum of 1.25 per cent of the total credit RWAs under the standardised approach.  
  > Note - A bank may either net off floating provisions from Gross NPAs to arrive at Net NPA or reckon it as part of its Tier 2 capital.'"*

- **Issue Date**: June 16, 2026
- **Effective Date**: June 16, 2026 (Immediate effect)

- **Exact Paragraph Quoted**:
  > *"Following the introduction of the Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS) 5.0 by the Government of India through National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company (NCGTC) circular Ref no. 0264/NCGTC/ECLGS5.0 dated May 8, 2026, Paragraph 34A is hereby inserted in the Principal Directions:*  
  > '34A. ### Item 7: NOP and Forex Capital Charge Revamp

- **RBI Reference Number**: **RBI/2026-27/155**, **DOR.MRG.REC.No.141/21-01-002/2026-27**
- **Title**: *Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Net Open Position (NOP) – Revised Instructions)*
- **Issue Date**: June 24, 2026
- **Effective Date**: April 1, 2027
- **Issued On or Before 30 June 2026?**: **YES** (Issued on 24 June 2026; taught as *"issued before cut-off, coming into force from 1 April 2027"*).
- **Exact Paragraph Quoted**:
  > *"The prudential framework for computing Net Open Position (NOP) and foreign exchange risk capital charges has been revised to align with Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS) standards:*  
  > *(1) Banks shall compute Net Open Position on a consolidated basis, doing away with the bifurcated computation for onshore and offshore positions.*  
  > *(2) Accumulated surplus/reserves of overseas banking subsidiaries and branches shall be incorporated into the consolidated NOP calculation.*  
  > *(3) The capital charge for foreign exchange risk shall be maintained strictly against the actual Net Open Position computed at the close of each business day at both solo and consolidated levels.*  
  > *(4) Under the Shorthand Method, net open positions in gold shall be evaluated separately from foreign currency exposures.*  
  > *(5) Specific structural foreign exchange positions held deliberately to hedge capital ratios against exchange rate volatility may be exempted from the NOP calculation subject to prior Board approval and supervisory non-objection."*
