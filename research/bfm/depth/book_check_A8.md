# Book check: unit A8 (EXIM Bank, RBI, FEMA, FEDAI and others)

Done 2026-11-26. We compared the book's Unit 8 (sections 8.1–8.5, plus an annex summarising FEDAI Rules 1–8; about 30
pages) with A15b (EXIM Bank), A16 / A16b (FEMA, LRS), A19 (ADR / GDR / FCCB / FCEB) and A21 (draft: RBI, FEMA, AP
categories, FEDAI).

## Verdict
- **Scope:** covered, with one gap: the **RBI returns banks file** (8.2.1–8.2.10).
- **Depth:**
  - **EXIM Bank:** A15b teaches the Act, s. 10 business, the 12-month deposit floor and lines of credit. The book's
    product catalogue (EMF, product liability insurance, EVDLP …) is trivia, apart from lines of credit.
  - **FEMA, LRS, ODI:** current in A16 / A16b. The book's ODI paragraphs (FEMA 120/2004, 400 % of net worth, USD 1 bn)
    are superseded by the OI Rules 2022, and A16b has the trap.
  - **ADR / GDR / FCCB / FCEB:** in A19.
  - **FEDAI:** in A05, A06, A12 and A21.
- **Real gaps:** 3. **Trap cards:** 2.
