# Book check: unit A3 (Correspondent banking and NRI accounts)

Done 2026-11-26. We compared the book's Unit 3 (sections 3.1–3.9, about 24 pages, 3 short cases, 3 "Check Your
Progress" sets, 7 terminal questions) with A02, A17, A18 and their fact sheets. Written in our own words.

## Verdict
- **Scope:** most of it is covered. One sub-area, **NRI investment and property facilities (book 3.7)**, is thin in
  our lessons.
- **Depth:** ours is current where the book is old. Examples: T2 has replaced TARGET2; CHAPS settles gross; RBI (not
  IDRBT) runs RTGS; SNRR's 7-year cap is gone; NRI listed-equity caps were raised on 12 Jun 2026; residence is under the
  Income-tax Act 2025.
- **Real gaps:** 4. **New trap cards:** 2.
