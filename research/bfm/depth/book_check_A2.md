# Book check: unit A2 (LRS and other remittance facilities for residents)

Done 2026-11-26. We compared the book's Unit 2 (sections 2.1–2.9, about 13 pages: one worked TCS table, 5 practice
questions, 4 terminal questions) with A16, A16b, A17 and their fact sheets. Written in our own words.

## Verdict
- **Scope:** nothing is missing. Every section is taught in A16 or A16b.
- **Depth:** ours is deeper and current. The book still has TCS at 5 % above ₹7 lakh, ODI under FEMA 120/2004, the
  IFSC 15-day idle-funds rule, Form ODI and 60-day repatriation. We teach the Income-tax Act 2025 s. 394 TCS (₹10 lakh,
  2 % / 20 %, nil on an education loan), the OI Rules 2022, Form FC, 90 days, and the withdrawal of the 15-day rule.
  Each old figure is already a dated trap card.
- **Real gaps:** 2 small ones. **New trap cards:** 2.

## Gaps

| G1 | 2.6.4, 2.6.10 | A bank may not issue guarantees under LRS in favour of a foreign university or for emigration | A16 c4, one line on the studies/emigration card, **only if the current LRS MD still says so** | LRS MD (6 Sep 2024) A.7 / FAQ |
| G2 | 2.5.1 | Due diligence before an LRS remittance: a new customer's account opening, source of funds (bank statement for the past year, or the tax return) | A16 c4: one card line ("the bank checks the source of funds"); detail is bank policy, so no figures | LRS MD (KYC / source of funds para); PMLA rules |

**Leave out:** the Schedule II list of ministries (A16 names Schedule II as "needs the government's nod", which is
enough for the exam); the book's list of relatives under the Companies Act 1956 (we teach the 2013 Act definition); the
older ODI valuation rule (USD 5 million: merchant banker; otherwise a CA), which no longer applies.
