# Book check: unit A4 (Documentary letters of credit)

Done 2026-11-26. We compared the book's Unit 4 (sections 4.1–4.13, about 36 pages, 9 cases, 4 "Check Your Progress"
sets, 7 terminal questions) with A07 (Incoterms), A08, A09, A10 (case), A13 and their fact sheets. Written in our own
words.

## Verdict
- **Scope:** nothing is missing.
- **Depth:** ours matches or beats the book on nearly every point. We cover:
  - all the LC types, the parties and amendments;
  - UCP Arts. 14(d), 14(h), 14(i), 14(k), 16, 17, 29, 31, 35 and 36;
  - URR 725 (with 3 banking days);
  - **ISBP 821**, which replaced 745 in July 2023 (the book stops at 745);
  - Incoterms 2020 with DPU;
  - SWIFT MT 700 / 707 / 202 (in A02);
  - evidence of import under the 2026 regulations.
- **Real gaps:** 3 small ones. **New trap cards:** 2. The book's 9 cases are all question kinds we already drill
  (A09, A10). Our A10 uses its own cases.
