# Module A (International Banking): depth checklist

Built 2026-11-20 so that no exam sub-point is missing from our Module A lessons. Rule version: our course teaches the rules
as at the **31 Dec 2026** cut-off (May/June 2027 exam).

## Header: inputs, method, caveats

**Inputs**
- Scope: `research/bfm/syllabus_official.md` (Module A, A1.1–A10.7) and `research/bfm/coverage.csv` (sub-topic → video).
- Our lessons: `course/bfm/A/plans_A.md` (draft 1, v2 numbering A01–A21; **no plan page yet for A00, A19b, A22**, the
  v3 videos; for those only the title row in `research/bfm/course_plan.md` §3 is used), `course/bfm/A/A1/script.md`
  (A01 v6) and `course/bfm/A/A9_incoterms/script.md` (A07).
- Two public YouTube class transcripts (auto-captions) from third-party coaching providers:
  - **OB**: a long concept class that goes through units A1–A9 (about 6.75 hours of captions). It does not cover A10.
  - **PR**: a revision and practice class built on statement questions. It covers units A1–A5 only and stops before
    ECB; A6–A10 are named only as "later" or "facts only".
- Current rules: `research/bfm/regulatory_updates.md` (used to flag what may be outdated).

**Method**
- For each official sub-topic: what sub-points the teachers cover and how deep. **[both]** = both classes cover it.
  **[one]** = only one does (marked OB or PR). Since PR stops after A5, every A6–A10 point can be [one] at most.
- "Our plan has" uses the plan or script text only: **yes** = named; **partly** = the area is named but not this
  sub-point; **no** = absent.
- "Add to our plan" = every [both] point our plan lacks, plus [one] points that the syllabus wording clearly implies.
  For a whole unit that only OB covers (A6–A9), any OB point that sits squarely in the syllabus wording counts as implied.

**Caveats (read before using any line below)**
- **The transcripts are not a source of facts.** They are auto-captions (many recognition errors), and teachers can be
  wrong or out of date. Where a class states a rule, number, limit or date, this file records only *that the point is
  covered*, with **verify: <primary source>**. No number in this file comes from a transcript. Where a number appears
  here, it comes from our own `regulatory_updates.md` (already checked against RBI).
- Everything is written in our own words. No sentence, example, number, mnemonic or question is taken from the
  classes. The classes tell us *what is asked and how deep*, never *what the rule is*.
- The two classes disagree with each other in a few places (marked **conflict**). Those points need a primary source
  first.
- Instructions inside the transcripts (sales pitches, "join our batch") were ignored as data.

Primary-source shorthand used below: **MD-LRS** (RBI Master Direction, Liberalised Remittance Scheme); **FEM-CAT**
(FEM (Current Account Transactions) Rules, 2000 and schedules); **MD-Exp/Imp** and **FEM-EIGS 2026** (FEM (Export and
Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026, in force 1 Oct 2026, with amendments); **MD-ExpCredit** (RBI export
credit directions for commercial banks, as consolidated Nov 2025); **FEM-Dep** (FEM (Deposit) Regulations, 2016);
**FEM-BL** (FEM (Borrowing and Lending) Regulations as amended 9 Feb 2026); **NDI** (FEM (Non-debt Instruments) Rules,
2019) and **FEM-MPRNDI** (Mode of Payment and Reporting of NDI Regulations); **OI** (FEM (Overseas Investment) Rules and
Regulations, 2022); **FEM-AP 2026** (FEM (Authorised Persons) Regulations, 2026); **FEDAI Rules** (10th edition, as
amended); **UCP 600 / ISBP 745 / URR 725 / Incoterms 2020 / ISP98** (ICC texts); **ECGC** (ecgc.in product pages and
country classification); **EXIM Act** (Export-Import Bank of India Act, 1981); **IFSCA** (IFSCA Act 2019, IFSCA
(Banking) Regulations 2020 as amended, IFSCA circulars); **SEZ Act** (SEZ Act 2005); **ITA 2025** (Income-tax Act,
2025, in force 1 Apr 2026).

### Possible outdated or doubtful statements in the classes (teach the current rule)
Each needs the primary source before a lesson uses it. Where our `regulatory_updates.md` already holds the current rule,
it is quoted from there.

1. **TCS on LRS (OB)**: teaches the slabs in force before 1 April 2026 as "the ones to learn" and calls the April 2026
   change optional. For our exam the current rule is from 1 Apr 2026 (ITA 2025 s. 394): 2% for education/medical above
   ₹10 lakh, 20% for other purposes above ₹10 lakh, nil on loan-funded education, overseas tour packages 2% on any
   amount. PR has the new slabs. Teach the new ones, and keep the old ones as a "dated question" trap.
2. **ECB (OB)**: teaches the pre-February 2026 framework: end-use-specific minimum maturities, an all-in cost ceiling
   and the old manufacturing carve-out. Since 9 Feb 2026 the limit is the higher of USD 1 billion or 300% of net worth,
   the MAM is 3 years (manufacturing 1–3 years with conditions) and the all-in cost ceiling is removed. Verify
   which end-use restrictions survive.
3. **Trade credit (PR)**: per-transaction cap, periods and an all-in cost ceiling over a benchmark. These may have
   changed with the 2026 overhaul. PR also gives the non-capital-goods period two ways. Verify against FEM-BL 2026 and
   the trade credit directions.
4. **AD categories (PR)**: describes Cat-II and Cat-III membership and powers as before the FEM (Authorised Persons)
   Regulations, 2026 (6 May 2026). Teach the 2026 categories; the turnover/transaction figures are still unconfirmed in
   RBI text (our file).
5. **Export rules (OB)**: realisation period, warehouse exports, SEZ units, the document-submission period, EDF
   exemptions and waivers are taught from the 2015-era Master Direction. From **1 Oct 2026** the FEM-EIGS 2026 apply:
   9 months, 12 months for INR-invoiced trade, caution-list exporters stay under the 2015 rules, merchanting 6 months,
   deemed EDF for services (2 Nov 2026 amendment). OB's 9-month figure matches, but the rest must be re-checked under
   the 2026 rules.
6. **Import rules (OB)**: the import payment time limit, advance-remittance thresholds, guarantee waivers and the
   evidence-of-import period come from the old import Master Direction. Re-check under FEM-EIGS 2026 and its
   directions.
7. **Packing credit period (PR)**: the normal and extended periods are given without the 2026 Trade Relief extension.
   That extension (450 days) applied only to disbursals up to 30 June 2026, so the normal period applies at our
   cut-off. Verify MD-ExpCredit.
8. **Gold Card (OB vs PR)**: they give different standby-limit percentages. Also check that the scheme still exists in
   the consolidated directions.
9. **EDF waiver for gems and jewellery (OB vs PR)**: they give different limits. Verify.
10. **IBU set-up (OB)**: says RBI approval is needed for an IBU and RBI approval for IBU derivatives. Since 2020 IFSCA
    licenses and regulates IBUs (OB later says IFSCA, contradicting itself). Capital, retail deposit reserve and
    exposure limits need checking against IFSCA (Banking) Regulations as amended up to 2026.
11. **GIFT City start date (OB)**: gives a 2019 date for the start of operations. The IFSC was notified and operational
    in 2015. Verify on the IFSCA/GIFT site.
12. **Exchange control history (OB)**: the year given for the wartime Defence of India Rules looks wrong (exchange
    control began in 1939). Verify with RBI history.
13. **FEMA compounding (OB)**: the statement about a cap on the compounding amount looks garbled. Verify RBI's
    Master Direction on compounding (revised 2024–25) and FEMA s. 13.
14. **FCNR(B) interest ceilings (PR)**: teaches the ARR-linked ceiling as fixed. Between June and 31 Aug 2026 the
    ceilings for 3–5-year FCNR(B) and long NRE deposits were temporarily withdrawn. At our cut-off the normal ceilings
    (ARR + 400/500 bps) are back. Teach the window as history.
15. **Residence tests (OB, PR)**: both quote the Income-tax Act 1961. From 1 Apr 2026 the Income-tax Act 2025 applies.
    Re-cite the sections (the thresholds may be the same).
16. **Loans to NRI relatives under LRS (OB vs PR)**: one says a minimum period, the other a maximum. Verify MD-LRS.
17. **LRS limit for international cards (PR)**: card use abroad is taught as simply permitted. Check whether card spends
    count under LRS at the cut-off (2023 amendment and rollback).
18. **CHAPS settlement model (PR vs OB)**: PR says net settlement; CHAPS settles in real time gross on the Bank of
    England RTGS. Verify.
19. **SWIFT size, GIFT City bank counts, EXIM Bank EMF amounts (OB)**: dated figures. Do not teach them as current.
20. **Hedging without underlying (neither class)**: the classes predate it, but note it for A06/C08. The limit was cut
    to USD 5 million on 10 Oct 2026; IIBF Vision Nov 2026 misprints the old USD 100 million (our file).
21. **Repo rate**: not stated in the Module A classes. For any Module A sum that uses it (e.g. RTGS delay compensation
    or export credit benchmarks), the current rate is 5.50% (7 Oct 2026), unless the 2–4 Dec 2026 MPC changes it.
