## Points
| # | Testable point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | UCP 600 binds a credit only when the credit **expressly** says it is subject to UCP (A08 F1). 39 articles; in force 1 Jul 2007 (UCP 500 had 49 articles). | UCP 1; ICC (**verify** "49" for UCP 500) |
| 2 | Examination: the nominated bank (acting on its nomination), confirming bank and issuing bank check **on the documents alone**, whether they **appear on their face** to comply. | UCP 14(a) |
| 3 | Each has a **maximum of five banking days following the day of presentation** to decide. An expiry date falling inside that period does **not** shorten it. (UCP 500: "reasonable time, not exceeding seven banking days".) | UCP 14(b); UCP 500 Art. 13(b) (**verify** wording) |
| 4 | Presentation with an **original transport document** (Arts. 19–25): not later than **21 calendar days after shipment**, and never after the **expiry date**. | UCP 14(c) |
| 5 | Data need **not be identical**, but must **not conflict** with the same document, other documents or the credit. | UCP 14(d) |
| 6 | Goods description: **in the invoice it must correspond** with the credit; in other documents it may be in **general terms** not conflicting. | UCP 14(e), 18(c) |
| 7 | A document presented but **not required** is disregarded and may be returned. | UCP 14(g) |
| 8 | A **non-documentary condition** (no document named to show compliance) is **deemed not stated** and disregarded. | UCP 14(h) |
| 9 | A document **may be dated before** the credit's issue date, but **not later than** its presentation date. | UCP 14(i) |
| 10 | Shipper / consignor need not be the beneficiary; a transport document may be issued by a party other than the carrier if it meets Arts. 19–24. | UCP 14(k), (l) |
| 11 | **Refusal**: a **single notice** stating refusal, **each** discrepancy, and what happens to the documents (held, held pending waiver, returned, or per prior instructions); sent by telecommunication **no later than the close of the fifth banking day** after presentation. The issuing bank may approach the applicant for a **waiver**, but that does **not** extend the five days. A bank that fails these steps is **precluded** from claiming non-compliance. | UCP 16(b)–(f) |
| 12 | **Commercial invoice**: issued by the beneficiary, in the applicant's name, in the credit's currency, **need not be signed**; a bank **may** accept an invoice above the credit amount (binding on all) if it does not pay more than the credit allows. | UCP 18 |
| 13 | **Bill of lading**: carrier named, signed by carrier / master or a named agent; **shipped on board** a named vessel (on-board notation date = shipment date, else issue date); ports as in the credit; **full set** of originals; no charter-party indication. Transhipment allowed if one B/L covers the whole carriage. | UCP 20 |
| 14 | **Clean** transport document: no clause declaring defective goods or packing; the word "clean" need not appear. **On deck**: not acceptable (a "may be loaded on deck" clause is). "Shipper's load and count" acceptable. | UCP 26, 27 |
| 15 | **Insurance**: issued and signed by an **insurance company / underwriter** or their agents (not a broker); **cover notes not accepted**; dated **no later than shipment** (unless cover effective by then); same currency; if the credit is silent, **at least 110% of the CIF / CIP value**. | UCP 28 |
| 16 | **Expiry or last presentation day on a closed day** (for reasons other than force majeure): extended to the **first following banking day**; the nominated bank states this on its schedule. The **latest shipment date is not extended**. | UCP 29 |
| 17 | **Force majeure** (Acts of God, riots, wars, strikes, etc.): no liability; a credit that **expires during the interruption is not honoured** after business resumes. | UCP 36 |
| 18 | Tolerances: "about" / "approximately" = ±10% of the amount, quantity or unit price it refers to; ±5% on quantity (not for packing units / items; total not above the credit); 5% less on amount when quantity shipped in full. Partial drawings/shipments **allowed** unless the credit says otherwise. Instalment missed → the credit ceases for that and later instalments. | UCP 30, 31, 32 |
| 19 | **Reimbursement (UCP 13)**: the credit states whether URR applies; if not: the issuing bank gives a reimbursement authorisation (no expiry date); the claiming bank need **not** certify compliance; the issuing bank bears interest and costs if not reimbursed on first demand and stays liable. | UCP 13 |
| 20 | **URR 725**: the reimbursing bank acts on the issuing bank's **reimbursement authorisation**, not on the credit; it does **not** examine documents and is not given the credit; it is not obliged to pay unless it has issued a **reimbursement undertaking**; it has **three banking days** following receipt of a claim to process it; its charges are for the issuing bank unless stated. Four terms: issuing bank, claiming bank, reimbursing bank, reimbursement authorisation (and undertaking). | URR 725 (**verify** article numbers and the 3-day rule against ICC text) |
| 21 | **ISBP 745**: a statement of **international standard banking practice** for examining documents under UCP 600; it is **practice that supports UCP, not a rule**; approved 2013. Earlier: ISBP 645 (2002), ISBP 681 (2007, for UCP 600). | ICC (**verify** years) |

## Traps
- Five **calendar** days (no: five **banking** days following the day of presentation).
- A holiday always extends expiry (no: Art. 29 extends for a closure, but not for force majeure, Art. 36; and never the latest shipment date).
- ISBP is a rule (no: practice).
- The reimbursing bank checks the documents (no: it acts only on the authorisation).
- Insurance from a broker, or a cover note (not accepted).
- A B/L dated before the LC is a discrepancy (no: may be dated before issue, Art. 14(i)).
- 21 days counted in banking days (no: calendar days).
