What this lesson must carry. A Forward Rate Agreement (FRA) is a cash-settled OTC interest rate derivative covering one future period on a notional principal (never exchanged), settled at the start of the forward period by discounting the rate difference at the reference rate. An Interest Rate Swap (IRS) exchanges a stream of interest payments over multiple periods on a notional principal that is not exchanged, settled net in arrears. Under the Master Direction – RBI (Rupee Interest Rate Derivatives) Directions, 2025 (8 Dec 2025, effective 1 March 2026, updated 22 Sep 2026), Scheduled Banks, SPDs, NBFC-ULs and five AIFIs (EXIM Bank, NABARD, NHB, SIDBI, NaBFID) act as market-makers who need no underlying risk and may warehouse swaps. Any resident (including corporates—refuting the class claim that MIBOR is interbank-only) may use FRAs and IRSs referencing benchmarks published by an authorised FBA (FBIL): retail users for hedging only, non-retail users (minimum net worth ₹500 crore or turnover ₹1,000 crore, or regulated financial entities) without purpose restriction, except MMIFOR swaps which are hedging-only for all users. Key swap types in India include plain-vanilla fixed-for-floating IRS, basis (floating-for-floating) swaps, and Overnight Indexed Swaps (OIS) on FBIL Overnight MIBOR (since 4 August 2025, first 3 hours 9:00 AM–12:00 Noon of NDS-CALL trades, published 12:45 PM) and FBIL SORR (published since 7 July 2025; FIMMDA SORR-OIS conventions 18 Dec 2025, Actual/365 Fixed, annual settlement ≤ 1 year, semi-annual > 1 year). For ALM and risk management under the Commercial Banks – ALM Directions, 2025 (28 Nov 2025), banks bucket rate-sensitive assets, liabilities, and off-balance-sheet swaps/FRAs in Traditional Gap Analysis (TGA) and Duration Gap Analysis (DGA): pay fixed / receive floating to protect a floating-rate liability, negative repricing gap, or fixed-rate bond book when rates rise; receive fixed / pay floating to protect a floating-rate asset or positive gap when rates fall. Currency swaps (Master Direction – Risk Management and Inter-Bank Dealings, updated 1 Oct 2026) come in three forms—principal-only (POS), coupon-only (COS), and principal + interest (full cross-currency swap)—and usually exchange principal (unlike an IRS). A short-term USD/INR FX swap (actual exchange of principal only on a near leg and reverse exchange on a far leg) lets a bank raise rupees against surplus dollars at an effective cost equal to dollar funding cost plus the annualised forward premium.
