# Fact sheet · D3.2 Provisioning norms (video D03)
Layer 2 (PLAYBOOK §4c). Written 2026-11-24. Rules as at the 31 Dec 2026 cut-off (exam May/June 2027). Rows cited as [F1]…[F42]. Our own words; every row cites its primary text, paragraph number, and full URL. Sister sheets: D02 (asset classification: SMA, NPA, income recognition) and D04 (NPA & provisioning case study with ECGC/CGTMSE cover, IBC, NARCL).


What this lesson must carry. On 31 December 2026, bank provisioning is governed by the RBI (Commercial Banks – Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning) Directions, 2025 (updated as on 1 Oct 2026), not yet by Expected Credit Loss (ECL). The ECL Directions, 2026 (issued 27 Apr 2026) take effect only from 1 April 2027 ("issued, coming"). Under IRACP 2025, even standard assets carry general provisions on global funded outstanding: 0.25% for farm credit, individual housing loans, and Small & Micro Enterprises; 0.40% for Medium Enterprises and all other advances; 0.75% for CRE-RH; 1.00% for CRE; 2.00% for teaser housing loans (reverting to 0.40% one year after rate reset if standard); and project finance (post-1 Oct 2025) at 1.25% CRE / 1.00% CRE-RH & others during construction (reverting to 1.00% / 0.75% / 0.40% in operation). Standard provisions sit in Schedule 5 (not netted from advances or Net NPA) and count in Tier 2 capital up to 1.25% of total credit RWAs. Separately, Unhedged Foreign Currency Exposure (UFCE) on standard borrowers triggers incremental provisioning (0 / 20 / 40 / 60 / 80 bps by Potential Loss ÷ EBID) plus a 25 percentage-point risk-weight increase above 75% (flat 10 bps option for smaller entities ≤ ₹50 crore). For NPAs, an "unsecured exposure" means realisable tangible security is not more than 10% ab-initio (guarantees are not tangible security). Sub-standard takes 15% (secured, no allowance for ECGC/security), 25% (unsecured ab-initio), or 20% (unsecured infrastructure loans with cash-flow escrow and legal first claim). Doubtful splits into 100% on the unsecured portion plus 25% (D1: up to 1 yr), 40% (D2: 1–3 yrs), or 100% (D3: >3 yrs) on the secured portion. Loss assets are written off or 100% provided. Fraud requires 100% provision immediately, with an option to spread over up to 4 quarters (debiting 'other reserves' across a FY-end, reversing to P&L next year; ECL 2026 removes this 4-quarter spread from 1 Apr 2027). Wilful defaulters (≥ ₹25 lakh; 6-month declaration timeline updated for ECL from 1 Apr 2027): existing loans to companies with a director (other than govt/FI distress nominee) listed more than once in the LWD attract 5% when standard and accelerated provisioning when NPA (up to 25%/40% sub-standard, 40% D1 secured, 100% on both secured and unsecured in D2). Delayed Resolution Plans (aggregate exposure ≥ ₹1,500 cr) attract 20% additional provision after 180 days from end of the 30-day Review Period and +15% (35% total) after 365 days. PCR = Provisions ÷ Gross NPAs; RBI prescribes no ongoing minimum (70% was a one-time 30 Sep 2010 norm creating the countercyclical provisioning buffer).
