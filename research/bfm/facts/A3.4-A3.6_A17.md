# Fact sheet · A3.4 NRI banking · A3.5 NRI accounts · A3.6 Facilities to NRIs (video A17)
Layer 2 (PLAYBOOK §4c). Written 2026-11-24. Rules as at the 31 Dec 2026 cut-off (exam May/June 2027). Rows cited as [F1]. Our own words; every row cites its primary text. Sister sheets: A2.2-A2.7-A2.9_A16b.md (A16b: LRS capital uses, travel forex); A18 (loans to NRIs, full residence day-count tests).
What this lesson must carry. Under FEMA 1999 s. 2(v), a person resident in India stayed more than 182 days in the preceding financial year, excluding anyone leaving for employment, business, or an uncertain period (including students abroad); everyone else is a person resident outside India (s. 2(w)). Under FEM (Deposit) Regulations, 2016 (amended up to 29 June 2026), an NRI is a non-resident Indian citizen, while a PIO is a non-resident foreign citizen (not Pakistan/Bangladesh) of Indian lineage or foreign-origin spouse and explicitly includes an OCI cardholder under Citizenship Act s. 7A (PIO cards merged into OCI on 9 January 2015). OCBs (≥60% NRI-owned) were derecognised on 16 September 2003 and cannot open fresh accounts. Four accounts: NRE (INR, savings/current/RD/FD, min 1-yr TD, fully repatriable, tax-free under Income-tax Act, 2025 s. 11 Sch. IV Sl. 1, depositor bears exchange risk); NRO (INR, all forms, min 7-day TD, Indian income, 30% + surcharge + 4% cess TDS under s. 393(2) Table Sl. 17, current income freely repatriable, balances repatriable up to USD 1 million/FY with Form 145/146 under Income-tax Rules, 2026 Rule 220, formerly 15CA/15CB; resident account redesignated NRO on leaving); FCNR(B) (freely convertible currency, term deposit only, 1–5 years, fully repatriable, tax-free for NR/RNOR under s. 11 Sch. IV Sl. 14, bank bears exchange risk, 360-day year, compounded every 180 days + remaining actual days, 6-month floating reset, normal ceiling Overnight ARR/Swap + 250 bps for 1 to <3 yrs and + 350 bps for 3–5 yrs after the June–Aug 2026 temporary window ended 31 Aug 2026); SNRR (INR, non-interest-bearing, freely repatriable; 7-year cap deleted 14 Jan 2025, business-interest clause deleted and NRO-to-SNRR transfers within USD 1M/FY permitted from 22 Jun 2026). POA holders on NRE/NRO may make local payments and remit to the account holder, never gift or remit to third parties. On permanent return, NRE/NRO redesignate as resident (or NRE to RFC); FCNR(B) may run to maturity at contracted rate; RFC (returning NRI, any form, freely repatriable, free rate, tax-free while RNOR u/s 6(13)) vs RFC(D) (any resident, non-interest-bearing current account, monthly rupee conversion). Under NDI Rules 2019, Sch. III listed equity caps rose 12 Jun 2026 to <10% individual / ≤24% aggregate (5%/10%/24% for private banks), Sch. IV is deemed domestic, and Ch. IX allows buying residential/commercial property (no agricultural land, farm house, or plantation except by inheritance).
