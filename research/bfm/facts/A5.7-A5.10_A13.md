# Fact sheet · A5.7 import guidelines · A5.8 import finance · A5.9 IDPMS · A5.10 trade credit (video A13)

Layer 2 (PLAYBOOK §4c). Draft 1, written 2026-11-22. Rules as at the **31 Dec 2026** cut-off. Rows cited as [F3]. Our
own words; RBI, FEDAI and DGFT texts cited by paragraph.

**As in A11:** from **1 Oct 2026** imports are governed by the FEM (Export and Import of Goods and Services)
Regulations, 2026 (R26) and A.P. (DIR) 20 (DIR); the **Import Master Direction (updated to 12 Jan 2026) is
superseded** [A11 F1]. Its fixed figures (6 months to pay, USD 200,000 advance without guarantee, USD 5 million board
waiver, MoF waiver above USD 100,000 for PSUs, 3 months for evidence of import) are trap cards only. **Trade credit is
not part of that change**: it sits in the FEM (Borrowing and Lending) Regulations 2018 and the **Master Direction –
ECB, Trade Credits and Structured Obligations (updated 8 Sep 2026)**, Part II, which is current.

## Points
| # | Testable point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **IEC and FTP**: no import of goods without an IEC; goods must be importable under the FTP (free, or restricted with an authorisation / licence; prohibited items not at all). Mandatory import documents: transport document, commercial invoice cum packing list, **bill of entry**. | FTP 2.05(a), 2.06(b); DIR para 2 |
| 2 | **Payment time**: the AD monitors IDPMS entries and follows up so the importer pays **within the period in the underlying contract**; the AD may extend on a reasoned request. (Old MD B.5.1: within **6 months from shipment**: superseded.) | R26 reg 9; OMI B.5.1 |
| 3 | **Advance for imports**: the AD may permit an advance after satisfying itself of the genuine need; the **AD may set its own thresholds** above which a **standby LC or guarantee** is needed. Advance and later payments through the **same AD** (switch after telling both). **No advance for gold or silver imports.** (Old MD C.1.1: above **USD 200,000** an SBLC / guarantee of an international bank; waivable up to **USD 5 million** under a Board policy; PSU / Government above **USD 100,000** needed a Ministry of Finance waiver: superseded.) | R26 reg 10(2), (3), 11; OMI C.1.1 |
| 4 | **Import not made**: the importer must bring the advance back. If not repatriated within the contract (or extended) period, or the IDPMS entry is not closed, **every future advance** needs an unconditional, irrevocable **SBLC or guarantee from an international bank of repute** (or an AD's guarantee backed by such a counter-guarantee). | R26 reg 12 |
| 5 | **Interest on delayed import payment** (and on export advances) must not exceed the **all-in-cost ceiling for trade credit**. | R26 reg 10(4) |
| 6 | **Manner of payment**: through an authorised bank; Nepal / Bhutan in INR; **ACU members via the ACU mechanism** or as RBI directs; others INR or any foreign currency. Third-party payments allowed by the AD if bona fide. | MRP reg 3; R26 regs 4(1), 8 |
| 7 | **IDPMS** (Import Data Processing and Monitoring System; or any system RBI specifies): bills of entry from EDI ports reach it; the AD enters **non-EDI imports within 5 working days** of receiving documents, and **import of services** where the importer is not a resident individual and the invoice / contract is **above ₹10 lakh**, within 5 working days; it records all outward remittances, follows up, and **marks off** an entry once payment is made. At payment the AD **closes or updates the entry at the same time**. **Up to ₹10 lakh** per bill of entry / invoice, closure on the importer's declaration (or a quarterly bulk declaration). The AD may close an advance entry where no import happened and repatriation is impossible, or a reduced-value settlement, on reasons. IDPMS began in **2016** (A.P. DIR 65 of 28 Apr 2016, superseded with the MD) and replaced the BEF (bill of entry follow-up) statement (XBRL returns, superseded). | R26 regs 4(2) and proviso, 18(1)(c), (d), (e), (f), (h), (j), (k); DIR Annex B items 64, 65 |
| 8 | **Evidence of import**: the bill of entry (its number, port code and date, marked in IDPMS); by post: postal appraisal form / customs assessment certificate; courier: courier bill of entry. (Old MD C.7: for advance remittances, evidence within **3 months** of remittance, extendable: superseded; the 2026 rules leave timelines to the AD's SOP.) | R26 reg 18(1)(h); R26 reg 19; OMI C.7.1 |
| 9 | **Retiring an import bill** (FEDAI): at the **hedge (forward) contract rate**, or, without one, the **prevailing bill selling rate**. | FEDAI 3.1(a) |
| 10 | **Import bill under an LC not paid by the importer**: the bank (which has paid abroad) **crystallises** it as per its **stated policy**, at the same rate as retirement (contract, else bill selling). Bills negotiated under import LCs carry the **commercial rate** applicable to domestic advances. | FEDAI 3.1(b), 3.2(a), 3.3 |
| 11 | **Trade credit (TC)**: credit for imports of capital / non-capital goods permissible under the FTP: **suppliers' credit** (the overseas supplier gives time) and **buyers' credit** (a bank / FI / foreign equity holder abroad, or a financial institution in an IFSC, lends to the importer to pay the supplier). In foreign currency **or INR**. Borrower: a person resident in India acting as importer. | TC 13, 14(i), (ii), (iv) |
| 12 | **TC amount (automatic route)**: up to **USD 150 million** per import transaction for oil / gas refining and marketing, airline and shipping companies; **USD 50 million** for others. | TC 14(iii) |
| 13 | **TC period** (from the date of shipment): **capital goods up to 3 years**; **non-capital goods up to 1 year or the operating cycle, whichever is less** (shipyards / shipbuilders: up to 3 years). TC beyond 3 years is an **ECB**. | TC 14(v), 18; ECB table (TC beyond 3 years listed as ECB) |
| 14 | **TC all-in-cost ceiling**: FCY TC: **benchmark rate + 300 bps** (new TCs; 350 bps for older LIBOR-linked TCs moved to an ARR); INR TC: **benchmark + 250 bps**. | TC 14(vi) |
| 15 | **TC currency switch**: FCY → another FCY or INR freely; **INR → FCY not permitted**. Lenders' recognised list: foreign branches / subsidiaries of Indian banks only for FCY TC. | TC 14(iv) note, (ix) |
| 16 | **TC security**: AD guarantees in favour of the overseas lender up to the TC amount and period; **Letters of Undertaking / Comfort not permitted**; importer may offer movable / immovable assets (not SEZ land), corporate / personal guarantee. | TC 16.1, 16.2 |
| 17 | **TC reporting**: ADs report drawal, utilisation, repayment monthly in **Form TC** (by the 10th of the following month); unique ID per TC. | TC 17.1 |
| 18 | **"Buyer's credit" for exports (EXIM Bank)** is a different product: EXIM Bank lends to an **overseas** buyer to buy Indian exports. Not a TC. | (concept; EXIM Bank: A15) |
| 19 | **Import finance by banks**: working-capital loans against imported goods by **pledge** (goods in the bank's possession / control) or **hypothecation** (goods with the borrower, charge only); usance (DA) import bills under LC give the importer credit. | JAIIB prerequisite (PPB); no RBI text needed (concept) |
| 20 | **Merchanting**: both legs within **6 months** (AD may extend); outward only to the overseas seller, inward only from the buyer (AD may allow a third party). | R26 reg 16 |

## For the script
- Trap cards: 6 months, USD 200,000 / 5 million / 100,000, 3 months evidence (old MD); supplier's vs buyer's credit;
  TC vs ECB (beyond 3 years = ECB); import buyer's credit vs EXIM Bank buyer's credit; non-capital "1 year or
  operating cycle, whichever less".
- Numericals: retirement at bill selling (FEDAI 3.1), and at a forward contract rate.
