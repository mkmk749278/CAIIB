# Fact sheet · A5.1, A5.5 (and A5.2 reduction / set-off): export rules: IEC, EDF, realisation, EDPMS (video A11)

Layer 2 (PLAYBOOK §4c). Written 2026-11-21. Rules as at the 31 Dec 2026 cut-off (May/June 2027 exam). Rows are cited as [F3].
Our own words; we cite regulation and paragraph numbers. A11b owns packing credit and post-shipment finance; A14 owns
agency commission, refund, EDF waiver, EEFC/DDA, Gold Card.

**The big fact for this lesson:** from **1 Oct 2026** exports are governed by the **FEM (Export and Import of Goods and
Services) Regulations, 2026** (FEMA 23(R)/2026-RB) and the short directions in **A.P. (DIR) Circular No. 20 (16 Jan 2026)**.
That circular **supersedes the Master Direction – Export of Goods and Services** and about 96 export circulars from the
same date (incl. the EDPMS caution-listing circulars, write-off, SOFTEX, set-off, EDPMS ₹10 lakh circular). The 2026
text is principles-based: many old fixed numbers (21 days, write-off 5%/10%, extension six months at a time, warehouse
15 months, advance-payment shipment within 3 years, SOFTEX, the exemption list) **are not in it**. They are taught only
as history ("before 1 Oct 2026"), and the 2015 regime still matters for transactions before 1 Oct 2026 and for
caution-listed exporters.

## Points
### Chapter 1 · Two regulators, IEC, declaring an export
| # | Testable point | Source |
|---|---|---|

| 9 | **Deemed EDF for services (Second Amendment, 2 Nov 2026, effective 1 Oct 2026)**: a services exporter is **deemed to have filed the EDF** when it gives the AD the payment information for crediting its account, if it is (a) a **resident individual** (a natural person resident in India): **any amount**; or (b) **anyone else**: when the inward remittance for that service is **not more than ₹10 lakh** (or equivalent). Mirror on imports: the AD enters import of services in IDPMS only for importers other than resident individuals and invoice/contract value **above ₹10 lakh**. | R26 reg 2(1)(da), 3(2)(a)–(b), 18(1)(d); R26A2 |

EY Jan 2026 says the consolidated EDF "replaces SOFTEX" (lead; **verify** against the Annex form) |

| 12 | Services with no prescribed form (old rule): could be exported without a declaration, but the value still had to be realised and repatriated. | R15 reg 3(3); MDX B.7 (history) |

### Chapter 3 · The realisation clock
| # | Testable point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | **Realisation rule at the cut-off (R26 reg 5)**: the full export value (or reduced value allowed under reg 6), including by **set-off** under reg 7, must be **realised and repatriated within 9 months**: **goods** from the **date of shipment**; **services** (incl. software) from the **date of invoice**. | R26 reg 5(1)(a); R26A1 |
| 23 | **Goods exported to a warehouse outside India**: **9 months from the date of sale** of the goods from the warehouse (not from shipment). | R26 reg 5(1)(b); R26A1 |
| 24 | **Project exports**: as per the **payment terms of the contract**. | R26 reg 5(1)(c) |
| 25 | **INR-invoiced and/or INR-settled exports**: **12 months** (from shipment for goods, invoice for services, sale for warehouse goods). | R26 reg 5(1) first proviso; R26A1 |
| 26 | **SEZ, EOU, EHTP, STP, BTP, status holders**: R26 has **no separate period**: the same reg 5 applies to all exporters (SEZ only changes the specified authority: the DC). Old rule said the same 9 months for these units (R15 reg 9(2), MDX A.2(i)). | R26 reg 2(1)(f), 5; R15 reg 9(2) |
| 27 | **Extension**: the **AD** may extend the realisation period on the exporter's request citing reasons, if satisfied; **no cap and no RBI reference** in R26; the AD's internal policy must cover extensions; the AD must have systems to monitor and follow up realisation. | R26 reg 5(1) second proviso, 5(2), 19(1)(b) |
| 28 | Old extension rule (history): AD could extend **up to 6 months at a time**, irrespective of value, if not under ED/CBI investigation, reasons beyond control, exporter's declaration; beyond **one year** from export only if the exporter's total outstanding ≤ **USD 1 million or 10%** of average realisation of the past 3 years (whichever higher); no limit where the exporter has sued the buyer abroad; other cases to RBI. | MDX C.20 (superseded 1 Oct 2026) |
| 29 | **Unrealised beyond one year**: if proceeds stay unrealised **more than one year after the due date** (or the AD's extended date), the exporter may make further exports **only against full advance or an irrevocable LC**. | R26 reg 13 |
| 30 | **Caution-listed exporters**: exporters **on RBI's caution list on 30 Sep 2026** (orders under reg 16 of R15) **continue to be governed by that order until removed** from the list. | R26 reg 13 proviso; R26A1 |
| 31 | **Transactions before 1 Oct 2026** that needed RBI approval under R15 / the MDs are now handled by the **AD**. | R26 reg 20; R26A1 |
| 32 | **History, 9 → 15 → 9 (the trap card)**: R15 reg 9: **9 months** (COVID: 15 months for exports up to 31 Jul 2020) → **15 months** from **14 Nov 2025** (FEMA 23(R)/(7)/2025-RB dated 13 Nov 2025, in force on Gazette publication 14 Nov 2025) → **9 months** from **5 Jun 2026** (FEMA 23(R)/(8)/2026-RB, in force on publication). ## Traps
- "Realisation is 15 months" (no: 9 months at the cut-off; 15 was 13 Nov 2025 – 4 Jun 2026 under R15, and R26's first text before it started).
- "INR invoice: 18 months" (no: 12; 18 was R26's first text, never in force).
- "Warehouse exports: 15 months from shipment" (old R15 rule; now **9 months from the date of sale**).
- "Services: from date of shipment" (no: from the **invoice** date).
- "Shipment against advance within 3 years" (old R15 rule; R26 sets **no** period; only same-AD routing and the trade-credit interest ceiling).
- "Documents within 21 days" (old R15 reg 10; R26 leaves timelines to the AD's SOP).
- "AD can extend only 6 months at a time / RBI beyond that" (old MD; R26: AD extends, no cap).
- "Write-off 5% / 10%" (old MD; R26: AD-allowed **reduction**, ₹10 lakh on declaration).
- "Caution listing is automatic after 2 years" (withdrawn Oct 2020; and new caution listing is not in R26: reg 13's "beyond one year after due date → advance or LC" rule replaces it).
- "Deemed EDF for all services up to ₹10 lakh" (resident **individuals**: any amount; **others**: up to ₹10 lakh).
- "IEC is issued by RBI / Customs" (DGFT); "IEC needs annual renewal" (no: permanent; but annual **update** April–June or it is de-activated).
- "EDPMS entry is created when the money comes" (no: from Customs at shipment; the money closes it).
- "₹10 lakh closure: Nov 2025" (the RBI circular is 1 Oct 2025).
