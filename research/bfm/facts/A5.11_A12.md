## Points
| # | Testable point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Discounting / purchase / negotiation of an export bill**: at the AD's **bill buying rate** (or the forward contract rate, if she has one); **interest for the normal transit period and/or usance period is recovered upfront, at the same time**. | FEDAI 2.1(a); A05 F13 |
| 2 | **Due date**: usance bill with a **fixed due date** (e.g. so many days after shipment / bill date) → actual due date known, **no NTP**. Sight bill, or a usance bill **after sight** → the bank adds the NTP (foreign-currency bill not under LC: **25 days**) to reach the **notional due date**. The due date cannot change after the bill is purchased / discounted / negotiated. | FEDAI 2.3(a), (b); A05 F15 |
| 3 | **Overdue interest**: charged if payment is not received within the NTP (demand bills) or on / before the notional or actual due date (usance bills). | FEDAI 2.2(b) |
| 4 | **Crystallisation**: if the bill is not paid on the due date, the bank converts the foreign-currency liability into a **rupee liability** under **its own policy**, which must be **transparently available to customers** (no fixed day in the current rules). | FEDAI 2.1(b)(i), (ii) |
| 5 | **Crystallisation rate: the AD's TT selling rate**. The amount recoverable is the crystallised rupee amount plus interest and charges. (Why selling: the bank paid her in rupees against dollars that never came; it now "sells" her the dollars to close the dollar advance.) | FEDAI 2.1(b)(iii) |
| 6 | **Interest on crystallisation**: recovered **on the date of crystallisation for the overdue period** at the appropriate rate; and thereafter till the crystallised amount is recovered. For EBR bills, interest from the due date to the date of crystallisation per the bank's interest policy. | FEDAI 2.1(b)(iv); CF 359 |
| 7 | **Countries with externalisation problems** (buyer pays locally but the money can't leave): crystallise per bank policy even if local-currency payment is advised. | FEDAI 2.1(b)(v) |
| 8 | **Proceeds arrive after crystallisation**: converted at the **TT buying rate** (or the contracted rate). | FEDAI 2.1(c) |
| 9 | **Dishonour before crystallisation** (bill returned unpaid): recover the rupee equivalent of the bill and foreign charges at **TT selling**, plus interest and rupee charges. | FEDAI 2.1(d) |
| 10 | **Early realisation**: refund interest for the unexpired period; pay or recover the notional swap cost (as for early delivery of a forward contract). | FEDAI 2.2(c) |
| 11 | **Collection bills** (not financed): converted at TT buying (or contract) only after the money is credited to the Nostro; credit to the exporter within the time in 2.5(b), else compensation. | FEDAI 2.5(a)–(c) |
| 12 | **Interest rates**: rupee export credit at the bank's Board-approved policy (subject to the Interest Rates on Advances Directions); FEDAI: rate on export transactions as per RBI guidelines. **Exam sums state the rate** (and the base and day count: we use actual days / 365 on the rupee advance, labelled "case convention"). | CF 291; FEDAI 2.2(a) |
| 13 | **Overdue bill repaid from rupees**: overdue post-shipment rupee credit may be extinguished from rupee resources; the EDF / EDPMS entry stays open till realised. | CF 268; A11b F15 |
| 14 | **Usance post-shipment period**: max 365 days from shipment incl. NTP and grace; sight bills: NTP. | CF 269, 270; A11b F14 |

## Leads checked
- "Crystallise on the **30th day** after the notional due date": an older FEDAI rule (pre-2019 editions), not in the
  10th edition text as updated to Aug 2026, which leaves the day to the bank's published policy [F4]. Taught as a trap
  card ("older books"); the case uses "her bank's policy: 30 days after the due date (illustrative)".
- "Crystallise at bill buying again": wrong; TT selling [F5].
