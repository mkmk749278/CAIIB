# Fact sheet · C4.1 Reserve assets: CRR, SLR, NDTL and monetary policy (video C04)

Sister sheets: C02b (LAF liquidity framework, VRR/VRRR, OMO), C04b (Payment and settlement systems: RTGS, NEFT, CCIL, NDS-OM, PSS Act), C05 (G-sec valuation & HTM/AFS/FVTPL), B14/B14b (LCR/NSFR).
What this lesson must carry. Every Scheduled Commercial Bank (SCB) in India must set aside two statutory reserves against its Net Demand and Time Liabilities (NDTL) as on the last day of the second preceding fortnight: the Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) under Section 42(1) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, and the Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) under Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. Both reserve requirements are governed by the consolidated RBI (Commercial Banks – Cash Reserve Ratio and Statutory Liquidity Ratio) Directions, 2025 (updated as on 9 October 2026). NDTL aggregates demand liabilities, time liabilities, other demand and time liabilities (ODTL), and net liabilities to the banking system (floored at zero; excess inter-bank assets cannot reduce liabilities to others, and net inter-bank liability is exempt from CRR). Thirteen liability items are excluded from both CRR and SLR under Paragraph 19 (including paid-up capital, reserves, Tier 1/AT1 borrowings, P&L credit balance, refinance from RBI/EXIM/NABARD/NHB/SIDBI/NaBFID and other DFIs, net income-tax provision, pending DICGC/ECGC/NCGTC claims, ad-hoc insurance/Court Receiver receipts, BAF, specified subsidies, net unrealised trading-derivative gains/losses, non-refundable advance income, and eligible rediscounted bills), while nine categories enjoy exemptions under Paragraph 20 — notably ACU (US$) balances, OBUs, IBUs, market repo against government securities (corporate-bond repo is not exempt), and Long-Term Bonds (minimum 7 years, INR, plain vanilla) financing infrastructure and affordable housing (DTL = I − Min(EC, LB)), confirmed still in force under the November 2025 Resource Raising Norms Directions. As at 24 November 2026, CRR stands at 3.0% of NDTL (reduced from 4.0% in four 25 bps tranches completed 29 November 2025), held solely as cash balances with RBI earning zero interest (branch cash does not count for CRR of SCBs), with no statutory floor or ceiling since the RBI (Amendment) Act, 2006. Effective 15 December 2025 under the Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025, a fortnight runs from the 1st to the 15th and from the 16th to the last day of each calendar month, and SCBs submit a single electronic Form A on CIMS within 5 days (plus monthly Form VIII before the 20th). Effective 16 October 2026 (RBI/2026-27/290), the daily minimum CRR rose from 90% to 99% of the required CRR on every day of the fortnight. SLR stands at 18.0% of NDTL daily (40% statutory ceiling; 25% floor removed in 2007), held in cash/deemed cash (including branch cash, excess CRR above requirement, net current-account balances with SCBs, and Standing Deposit Facility balances — SDF counts for SLR, never CRR), gold valued at or below current market price, and unencumbered SLR securities (GoI dated securities, T-Bills including Cash Management Bills, and State Government Securities — development-bank bonds are not SLR assets). Shortfalls in CRR or SLR attract penal interest at Bank Rate + 3 percentage points on the first day/fortnight (8.75% p.a.) and Bank Rate + 5 percentage points (10.75% p.a.) on continuing defaults, anchored to the 5.75% Bank Rate / MSF rate set after the 7 October 2026 Monetary Policy Committee meeting (policy Repo Rate 5.50%, SDF 5.25%, stance "calibrated tightening"). Finally, the 6-member MPC (RBI Act s. 45ZB) targets 4% CPI inflation (±2%, retained through 31 March 2031), and CRR changes transmit to broad money (M3) via reserve money (M0) and the money multiplier (m = M3 ÷ M0), with a CRR cut injecting primary liquidity into the banking system.

| Inter-bank netting rule: Liabilities to the banking system (SBI, nationalised banks, RRBs, banking companies, co-operative banks, notified FIs) are reduced by assets with the banking system (current account balances, call/notice lending ≤14 days, loans to banks). Excess inter-bank assets (when assets exceed liabilities) cannot be subtracted from liabilities to others.
