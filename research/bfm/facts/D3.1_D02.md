# Fact sheet · D3.1 Asset classification (with income recognition under IRACP) (video D02)
Layer 2 (PLAYBOOK §4c). Written 2026-11-24. Rules as at the 31 Dec 2026 cut-off (exam May/June 2027). Rows cited as [F1]–[F38]. Our own words; every row cites its primary RBI text and full URL.
Executive Summary: What this lesson must carry
Under the RBI (Commercial Banks – Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning) Directions, 2025 (updated 1 October 2026) and the RBI (Commercial Banks – Resolution of Stressed Assets) Directions, 2025 (updated 1 October 2026), asset classification follows objective record-of-recovery norms introduced on the Narasimham Committee recommendations and applies borrower-wise, not facility-wise. A loan turns NPA when interest or principal is overdue for more than 90 days (term loans, bills), an OD/CC is out of order (excess over limit/drawing power continuously for 90 days, no credits for 90 days, or credits below interest debited during the previous 90 days inclusive of the day-end date), irregular drawings in working capital (including from stock statements older than 3 months) continue for 90 continuous days, regular or ad hoc limits remain unreviewed for 180 days, a credit card's minimum amount due stays unpaid for 90 days from the payment due date, or an eligible farm loan is overdue for two crop seasons (short-duration, <= 1 year) or one crop season (long-duration, > 1 year). Early stress is tagged at day-end as SMA-0 (up to 30 days, non-revolving only), SMA-1 (31–60 days), and SMA-2 (61–90 days), with CRILC reporting at ₹5 crore and above (monthly main report, weekly Friday default report). NPAs age from sub-standard (<= 12 months) to doubtful (after 12 months in sub-standard; D1 <= 1 yr, D2 1–3 yrs, D3 > 3 yrs) to loss, except that security erosion (< 50% of last assessed value -> straightaway Doubtful; < 10% of outstanding -> straightaway Loss) skips stages. Upgrade requires repayment of entire arrears across all facilities. Income on NPAs is booked only on cash basis, unrealised past interest is reversed, and from 1 October 2026 unrealised prior interest cannot be recognised on acquiring an SNFA (legacy booked reversed by 30 September 2027). The ECL Directions, 2026 (issued 27 April 2026, effective 1 April 2027) are issued, coming.
