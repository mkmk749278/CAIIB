## Points
| # | Testable point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A quote "A/B = x" means 1 unit of A (the **base** currency) costs x units of B (the **quote** currency). | Concept |
| 2 | **Direct** quote: home currency per unit (or 100 units) of foreign currency (USD/INR 85.60). **Indirect**: foreign currency per unit (or 100 units) of home currency (e.g. USD 1.1682 per ₹100). India's merchant quotes are direct. | FEDAI 5.7; concept |
| 3 | In a two-way quote, bid (lower) = the quoting bank buys the **base** currency; ask (higher) = it sells the base currency. | Concept (A01 F7) |
| 4 | **Cross rate**: the rate between two currencies fixed through a third currency (usually USD), when no direct market quote exists. | Concept; FBIL (EUR, GBP, JPY reference rates are crossed through USD/INR) |
| 5 | FBIL publishes USD/INR, EUR/INR, GBP/INR and JPY/INR reference rates (since 10 Jul 2018) around 1.30 p.m.; the EUR, GBP and JPY rates are **crossed**: EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/JPY market rates combined with the USD/INR reference rate. JPY/INR is per **100** yen. FBIL publishes JPY/INR to **2** decimals, the others to 4 (methodology para 10), around 13.30 hrs (para 11); our 4-decimal crosses are merchant/exam style. | FBIL (methodology v2, 15 Jan 2019, paras 8–11) |
| 6 | Market convention: EUR, GBP (and AUD, NZD) are quoted with USD as the quote currency (EUR/USD 1.0850 = USD per euro); most others with USD as the base (USD/JPY 149.50 = yen per dollar). | Concept / market convention (FBIL uses EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY) |
| 7 | **Rule 1: the USD sits on opposite sides** (EUR/USD and USD/INR): **multiply**. EUR/INR bid = EUR/USD bid × USD/INR bid; ask = ask × ask. | Arithmetic |
| 8 | **Rule 2: the USD is the base in both** (USD/INR and USD/JPY): **divide**, crossing the sides. JPY/INR bid = USD/INR bid ÷ USD/JPY **ask**; ask = USD/INR ask ÷ USD/JPY **bid**. (Bank buys yen for rupees: it gives 1 USD for 149.60 yen (its USD/JPY ask) and pays 85.60 rupees for that USD (its USD/INR bid).) **Same side generally**: the USD as the quote in both (EUR/USD, GBP/USD → EUR/GBP) also divides, crossing: EUR/GBP bid = EUR/USD bid ÷ GBP/USD ask. | Arithmetic |
| 9 | Check: the cross bid must be below the cross ask; if not, a side was crossed wrongly. | Arithmetic |
| 10 | **Chain rule**: a chain of equivalences, each starting with the currency the last one ended in; the answer = product of the right-hand numbers ÷ product of the left-hand numbers. | Arithmetic |
| 11 | JPY per 100: rupees = rate per 100 × (yen ÷ 100). | FEDAI 5.7; FBIL |
| 12 | **Two-way direct → indirect**: flip and swap. Indirect bid = 100 ÷ direct ask; indirect ask = 100 ÷ direct bid (85.60 / 85.62 → USD 1.1680 / 1.1682 per ₹100). In an indirect quote the bank buys high and sells low. | Arithmetic |
| 13 | The % spread of a cross ≈ the sum of the legs' % spreads; a leg backed out of a cross comes out wider than the original. | Arithmetic |

## Traps
- Bid × ask (or mixing sides) in Rule 1 (EUR/INR bid 92.8977 instead of 92.8760).
- In Rule 2, dividing bid by bid (JPY/INR bid 57.2575 instead of 57.2193).
- Forgetting ×100 (or ÷100) for yen.
- Multiplying when the USD is the base in both quotes (should divide).
