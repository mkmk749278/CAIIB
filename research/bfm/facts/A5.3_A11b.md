# Fact sheet · A5.3 Export finance: packing credit to post-shipment (video A11b)

## Points
| # | Testable point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The scheme**: RBI introduced export financing in **1967**; aim: short-term working capital to exporters at internationally comparable rates; in **rupees and in foreign currency**. | CF ch. XV background |
| 2 | **Packing credit (pre-shipment) defined**: any loan or credit to an exporter to finance **purchase, processing, manufacturing or packing of goods before shipment** (or working-capital expenses for rendering services), against an **LC**, a **confirmed and irrevocable export order**, or other evidence of an export order, unless lodgement of the order / LC is waived. | CF 2(1)(xxix) |
| 3 | **Period**: fixed by the bank case by case (time to procure, make and ship); must let the exporter ship in reasonable time. **Not adjusted by export documents within 360 days from the date of advance → ceases to be export credit from the start (ab initio).** | CF 234, 235, 292 |
| 4 | **Exports never happen**: the bank charges the **domestic lending rate plus penal charges, if any**, under a transparent **Board-approved** policy. | CF 293 |
| 5 | **Disbursal**: generally a **separate account for each packing credit**; released **in one lump sum or in stages**; separate accounts by stage (hypothecation, pledge), adjusted finally by the export documents; bank monitors progress. | CF 236–239 |
| 6 | **Liquidation**: from the **proceeds of the export bills** (on purchase, discount, etc.), which turns pre-shipment credit into **post-shipment credit**; by mutual agreement also from **EEFC balances** or the exporter's **rupee resources, to the extent exports have actually taken place**. | CF 240 |
| 7 | **Good track record relaxations**: liquidate with documents of **another order** (same or other commodity) if commercially necessary and unavoidable ("substitution of contract"); mark off with documents on which **no packing credit was drawn** (check no PC from another bank); **not for sister / associate / group concerns**. | CF 242 |
| 8 | **Running account**: PC **without prior LC / firm order**, any commodity, at the bank's judgement; only for exporters with a **good track record** and **EOUs / EPZ / SEZ units**; LC / orders produced within a reasonable time; bills marked off **FIFO**; each drawal limited to the **sanction period or 360 days, whichever earlier**; withdrawn on misuse; **not for sub-suppliers**. | CF 243–246 |
| 9 | **Waiver of order / LC**: banks waive submission ab initio for good-track-record exporters, with periodical statements of orders / LCs on hand (in the sanction, told to ECGC). | CF 390 |
| 10 | **Excess over export value** (agro): wastage → extinguish with **by-product** bills (cashew shell oil); non-exportable portion sold at home → **domestic rate from the date of advance**; HPS groundnut / deoiled cakes: PC up to raw-material value, excess adjusted within **30 days**. | CF 241 |
| 11 | **Sharing**: manufacturer-suppliers exporting through STC / MMTC / export houses may get PC; **sub-supplier** scheme via an **inland LC** opened by the export order holder's bank, first stage of production only, no running account, ECGC cover sought. | CF 248, 249 |
| 12 | **Other eligible exporters**: construction contractors (adjust within **365 days**); **services** covered by GATS earning free foreign exchange (all rules apply mutatis mutandis; service exporter registered with ESC / SEPC / FIEO, contract, invoice, inward remittance); agro / floriculture (not for investment items); **DTA → SEZ supplies count as exports**; **deemed exports** to multilateral-funded projects (post-supply credit max **30 days** or till payment, if earlier). | CF 250–264, 287–290 |
| 13 | **Post-shipment forms**: (1) export bills **purchased / discounted / negotiated**; (2) **advances against bills for collection**; (3) **advances against duty drawback** receivable. Also: undrawn balances, retention money (supply portion), consignment and exhibition exports. | CF 266, 271–284 |
| 14 | **Post-shipment period**: **demand bills → the Normal Transit Period (NTP) set by FEDAI**; **usance bills → max 365 days from shipment, including NTP and grace period**; banks to persuade earlier realisation. | CF 269, 270 |
| 15 | **Post-shipment liquidation**: from the export bill proceeds; by agreement from EEFC or proceeds of other unfinanced (collection) bills; the bills still stay open in **EDPMS** till realised. Overdue post-shipment rupee credit may be **extinguished from rupee resources**, but the **EDF stays outstanding** and the exporter must still realise. | CF 267, 268 |
| 16 | **Undrawn balance**: where bills are drawn for **90–98 % of FOB**, the balance is payable after the buyer checks quality / quantity; contingent; advance at the bank's judgement. | CF 271, 272 |
| 17 | **Retention money**: advance only against the **supply portion**, **never the services portion**; treated as a deferred liability; prefer guarantees. | CF 273–279 |
| 18 | **Consignment exports**: interest at par with cash-term sales; precious / semi-precious stones: PC moved on export to a **special post-shipment account**, adjusted by proceeds **within 365 days from export**. | CF 280, 281 |
| 19 | **Duty drawback advance**: against entitlements **provisionally certified by Customs** and covered by ECGC; lien with the designated bank. | CF 283, 284 |
| 20 | **ECGC (ECIB)**: banks to consider export credit insurance for banks; premium for the post-shipment guarantee may be borne by the bank, not passed on. | CF 285, 286 |
| 21 | **Interest**: rupee export credit at the bank's **Board-approved** policy, subject to the Interest Rates on Advances Directions 2025; foreign-currency export credit priced on an **Alternative Reference Rate (ARR)** of the currency. | CF 291, 303 |
| 22 | **PCFC** (pre-shipment credit in foreign currency): for **domestic and imported inputs**; **cash exports only**; any convertible currency (may differ from the invoice currency, exporter bears the cross-currency risk); ACU exports allowed; funded from EEFC, RFC(D), FCNR(B), lines of credit abroad, swaps; if supplier's credit covers imported inputs, PCFC only for **domestic** inputs. **Max 360 days**; no export within 360 days → adjusted at the **TT selling rate**. Liquidated by discounting the export bill under **EBR** or a foreign-currency loan (DP bills), or EEFC / rupee resources to the extent exported; on cancellation the exporter buys FX to repay. Running account allowed for good-track-record exporters (FIFO). | CF 294–337 |
| 23 | **EBR** (rediscounting of export bills abroad): usance bills **up to 180 days** from shipment (incl. NTP and grace); with or without recourse (usually with); Bankers' Acceptance Facility; exporter limits and EEFC credit restored only on realisation if "with recourse". | CF 340–364 |
| 24 | **Export credit target**: banks to reach export credit equal to **10 % of net credit**; need-based limits not denied only for lack of collateral; pre- and post-shipment limits **interchangeable**. | CF 388, 389, 394(3) |
| 25 | **Trade relief (sunset)**: TRM 2025 (14 Nov 2025): for eligible borrowers (listed export sectors; export credit outstanding on 31 Aug 2025; standard on that date) banks **may** allow export credit up to **450 days** (pre- and post-shipment) for credit **disbursed till 31 Mar 2026**; PC availed by 31 Aug 2025 where goods could not be dispatched may be liquidated from **legitimate alternate sources** (domestic sale, substituted order). TRM 2026 (31 Mar 2026, West Asia crisis): 450 days for export credit **disbursed till 30 June 2026**; alternate-source liquidation for PC availed on or before 31 Mar 2026. At the cut-off: **expired for new disbursals**; the normal limits (360 days PC; 365 days usance) apply. Teach as "came and went". | TRM 2025 paras 7, 10, 11; TRM 2026 paras 5–7; CF 396 |

## For A14 (Gold Card, read in the same chapter)
Gold Card: all creditworthy exporters with good track record (incl. MSME); **not** for exporters on ECGC's **Specific
Approval List** or with **overdue bills over 10 % of the previous year's turnover**; in-principle limits for **3
years** with automatic renewal; **stand-by limit of not less than 20 %** of the assessed limit; decisions within **25
days (fresh) / 15 days (renewal) / 7 days (ad hoc)**; preference in PCFC; collateral and ECIB waiver possible; rate
**not more than** the bank's general export-credit rate [CF 365–384]. This settles the depth checklist's "standby %"
conflict (A5.4).

## Leads NOT found in the current text (check before any trap card)
- "Packing credit amount = **lower of FOB value and domestic cost**": not in CF Chapter XV. Check the old Master
  Circular (2015) before teaching it as an old rule; in the current rule the amount is need-based (CF 389).
- "**Margin** on non-LC usance bills", "PC **eligibility checklist** (not on RBI caution list / ECGC SAL)": not in CF
  Chapter XV (SAL appears only for the Gold Card). Same check.
- DDA (CF 338–339): **turnover ₹3 crore** average of the preceding three licensing years, track record **at least two
  years** here, but RBI's FCA amendment (29 Apr 2025, per a lead) says three years: verify in FEMA 10(R) for A14.
