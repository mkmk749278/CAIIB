# Fact sheet · A3.4 Residency (FEMA & Income-tax Act 2025) · A3.7 Loans against NRI deposits · A3.8 Housing loans to NRIs (video A18)
Layer 2 (PLAYBOOK §4c). Written 2026-11-24. Rules as at the 31 Dec 2026 cut-off (exam May/June 2027). Rows cited as [F1]–[F25]. Our own words; every row cites its primary text. Sister sheet: A17_fact_sheet.md (A17: NRE, NRO, FCNR(B), SNRR, RFC, RFC(D), investments, property).
What this lesson must carry. FEMA, 1999 and the Income-tax Act, 2025 (w.e.f. 1 April 2026) apply two independent residency tests. Under FEMA s. 2(v), residency looks at > 182 days in the preceding financial year modified immediately by intention/purpose: leaving for employment, business, or an uncertain period (s. 2(v)(i)(A)) makes you non-resident on Day 1; returning for those purposes (s. 2(v)(i)(B)) makes you resident on Day 1, while a temporary/tourist visit stays non-resident even beyond 182 days. Under Income-tax Act, 2025, "tax year" (s. 3(1)) replaces previous/assessment year, and residency uses current tax-year physical days: ≥ 182 days (s. 6(2)(a)) OR ≥ 60 days + ≥ 365 days in 4 preceding tax years (s. 6(2)(b)). The 60-day limb is disapplied for Indian citizens leaving for employment/Indian ship crew (s. 6(3)) and visiting Indian citizens/PIOs (s. 6(4)), except visiting citizens/PIOs with > ₹15 lakh non-foreign income face 120 days + 365 days (s. 6(5)). A Deemed Resident (s. 6(7), carve-out s. 6(8)) is an Indian citizen with > ₹15 lakh non-foreign income not liable to tax in any other country (0 days stay required). RNOR (s. 6(13)) covers: NR in 9 of 10 preceding tax years, ≤ 729 days in 7 preceding tax years, 120–181 day high-income visitors, and deemed residents; under s. 5, ROR taxes global income, RNOR taxes Indian income plus Indian-controlled foreign business, and NR taxes Indian income only. Under RBI MD – Deposits & Accounts (updated 2 Sep 2026) and FEMA 3(R)/2018-RB (amended 16 Feb 2026, new Reg. 3A), ADs may grant fund/non-fund loans against NRE/FCNR(B) deposits in India to account holders or third parties without regulatory limit (usual margin; no lien on NRE savings; no loan against other banks' TDs). Loan proceeds cannot be repatriated, barred end-uses include relending, agriculture/plantation, real estate business, farmhouses, and TDR trading, third-party loans permit no direct/indirect FX consideration, account-holder repayment uses deposit adjustment, inward remittance, or NRO, and premature withdrawal of pledged deposits is barred. Overseas branches may lend abroad against NRE/FCNR(B) (not NRO). Own-deposit loans are exempt from MCLR/EBLR; third-party Rupee loans against FCNR(B) follow MCLR/EBLR (Interest Rates on Advances Directions, 2025). Housing loans to NRIs/OCIs (FEMA 3(R) Reg. 7; FED MD 6 updated 8 Sep 2026; Credit Facilities Directions, 2025 updated 1 Oct 2026) match resident terms (LTV ≤ 90% up to ₹30L, ≤ 80% >₹30L–₹75L, ≤ 75% >₹75L, stamp duty excluded >₹10L), cannot be credited to NRE/FCNR(B), disburse stage-linked, are secured by equitable mortgage, and are repaid via inward remittance, NRE/FCNR(B)/NRO, rent from that property, or close relatives (Companies Act s. 2(77)).
