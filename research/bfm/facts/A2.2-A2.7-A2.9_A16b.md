# Fact sheet · A2.2 Key sections under FEMA vis-à-vis LRS · A2.7 LRS vis-à-vis capital account transactions · A2.8 Reporting requirements under LRS · A2.9 Other remittance facilities for residents, with travel cash (video A16b)

Layer 2 (PLAYBOOK §4c). Written 2026-11-22. Rules as at the **31 Dec 2026** cut-off (exam May/June 2027). Rows cited
as [F3]. Our own words; every row cites its primary text. Sister sheet: `A2.1-A2.6_A16.md` (A16: current vs capital,
the three schedules, who may use LRS, PAN, Form A2, TCS); its rows are cited here as [A16 F13] etc. and not repeated.

**What this lesson must carry.** The LRS rests on a few FEMA sections. **s. 4** forbids a resident to hold foreign
exchange, foreign securities or property abroad unless the Act allows it; **s. 6** lets him draw foreign exchange for
capital account transactions that RBI (debt) or the Government (non-debt, s. 6(2A)) permit; **s. 10(5)** makes the
bank take a **declaration** before every forex deal, and **s. 10(6)** makes it a contravention to **use the money for
another purpose or not surrender it in time**; **s. 11** lets RBI direct banks and fine them (up to **₹10,000**, then
**₹2,000 a day**); **s. 13** fines anyone up to **three times the sum** (or **₹2 lakh** if not quantifiable, plus
**₹5,000 a day**), and **s. 14** allows civil prison for an unpaid penalty; **s. 15** lets the contravention be **compounded within 180 days**. Under LRS an individual may take
four capital routes: a **foreign currency account abroad** (or in **GIFT IFSC**), **immovable property abroad**,
**ODI/OPI** under the **Overseas Investment Rules 2022** (ODI only in an **operating** foreign company **not in
financial services**, no real estate or gambling, **no lending to it**; share proof in **6 months**, sale money home in
**90 days**, **APR by 31 December**), and an **interest-free rupee loan to an NRI/PIO relative** (minimum **one year**,
credited to **NRO**, used **in India**). Banks report every LRS remittance in the **LRS daily return on CIMS by close
of the next working day** (nil report if none); from **1 Jan 2026** AD Category-II and FFMCs file it themselves and can
see a person's **PAN-wise total** for the year. Outside LRS: employer-paid business travel (no limit), non-individuals'
Schedule III para 2 thresholds, and (from **13 May 2026**) bank tie-ups with **fintech remittance apps without RBI
approval**. At the travel counter: **cash only below ₹50,000**, **notes USD 3,000 per visit** (Iraq/Libya **5,000**;
Iran/Russia/CIS **full**; Haj/Umrah as the Haj Committee says), unspent forex **surrendered within 180 days**, but up to
**USD 2,000** in notes/TCs may be **kept for the next trip**, coins without limit. The **FEM (Authorised Persons)
Regulations 2026** (in force 6 May 2026) stop new FFMC licences and new franchisees.

## Points: the FEMA sections behind LRS (A2.2)
| # | Testable point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **s. 4 (the starting bar)**: save as the Act provides, **no person resident in India shall acquire, hold, own, possess or transfer any foreign exchange, foreign security or immovable property outside India**. LRS, the OI Rules and the retention limits are the "as provided" exceptions. | FEMA s. 4 |
| 2 | **s. 5 vs s. 6**: current account drawal is free (Government may restrict by rules: the CAT Rules) [A16 F3]. **s. 6(1)**: any person may draw foreign exchange for a **capital account transaction**, subject to s. 6(2). **s. 6(2)**: **RBI**, in consultation with the Government, specifies capital transactions **involving debt instruments**, the limits and conditions. **s. 6(2A)**: the **Central Government**, in consultation with RBI, prescribes those **not involving debt instruments** (hence the OI **Rules** are a Government notification and the OI **Regulations** RBI's). Both inserted/substituted by the Finance Act 2015, **in force 15 Oct 2019**. | FEMA ss. 5, 6(1), 6(2), 6(2A) and footnotes |
| 3 | **s. 6(4) (assets from non-resident days)**: a resident may **hold, own, transfer or invest** foreign currency, foreign security or property abroad **acquired when he was resident outside India, or inherited from a person resident outside India**. (s. 6(5) is the mirror for non-residents' Indian assets.) | FEMA s. 6(4), 6(5) |
| 4 | **s. 8 and s. 9**: a resident to whom foreign exchange is due must **realise and repatriate** it within the time RBI specifies (s. 8). s. 9 exempts from ss. 4 and 8, among others, **foreign currency/coins up to RBI's limit** (the USD 2,000 retention, F55) and **foreign currency accounts** RBI permits. | FEMA ss. 8, 9(a), (b), (e) |
| 5 | **s. 10(1)–(4)**: RBI **authorises** "authorised persons" (authorised dealer, money changer, offshore banking unit, or otherwise), **in writing**, and may **revoke** (public interest, or breach after a hearing); an AP must follow RBI's directions and deal only within its authorisation. | FEMA s. 10(1)–(4) |
| 6 | **s. 10(5) (the declaration)**: **before any forex transaction** the AP **must require a declaration and information** that reasonably satisfy it the deal is **not designed to contravene or evade** FEMA; if the customer refuses or complies poorly, the AP **refuses in writing** and, if it suspects a contravention, **reports to RBI**. Form A2 is this declaration in practice; RBI does not prescribe a document list. | FEMA s. 10(5); LRS B.2–B.4; ORF 6.1–6.3; APR26 reg 8(8) |
| 7 | **s. 10(6) (end-use)**: a person (not an AP) who bought foreign exchange for the **purpose declared under s. 10(5)** and **(a) does not use it for that purpose, or (b) does not surrender it within the specified period, or (c) uses it for a purpose not permissible** is **deemed to have contravened the Act**. The "specified period" is the R9 regulation (individuals 180 days, F54). Purchased for one permitted purpose and used for **another permitted** purpose is fine (ORF 3.1.3(i)). | FEMA s. 10(6); R9 preamble (made under s. 10(6)), regs 6–7; ORF 3.1.3 |
| 8 | **s. 11 (RBI over banks)**: RBI may give **directions** to APs (s. 11(1); LRS and ORF are issued under it) and call for **information/returns** (s. 11(2); the LRS daily return). **s. 11(3)**: an AP that breaks a direction or **fails to file a return** may be fined, after a hearing, **up to ₹10,000**, plus **up to ₹2,000 per day** while it continues. | FEMA s. 11(1)–(3); ORF covering letter para 2 |
| 9 | **s. 13(1) (penalty on anyone)**: for contravening the Act, rules, regulations, directions or authorisation conditions, on **adjudication**: **up to three times the sum involved** where quantifiable, **up to ₹2 lakh** where not; continuing contravention **up to ₹5,000 for every day after the first day**. s. 13(2): the adjudicator may also **confiscate** the currency/property and direct holdings abroad to be **brought back**. Penalties come by **adjudication**, not criminal trial. **s. 14 (unpaid penalty)**: if a s. 13 penalty is not paid within **90 days** of the demand notice, **civil imprisonment** up to **three years** where the demand exceeds **₹1 crore**, else up to **six months** (s. 14(1), (11)); recovery as for income tax (s. 14A). So prison arises only under s. 13(1C) (row 10) and s. 14 (c2, expert review M1). | FEMA s. 13(1), (2), s. 14, s. 14A |
| 10 | **s. 13(1A)–(1D) (Finance Act 2015, w.e.f. 9 Sep 2015)**: holding foreign exchange, foreign security or property **abroad** in breach of s. 4 above the value prescribed under **s. 37A**: penalty **up to three times** plus **confiscation of the equivalent value in India**; the adjudicator may recommend **prosecution**: **imprisonment up to five years and fine** (s. 13(1C)), on complaint by an officer **not below Assistant Director** (ED). s. 37A lets ED **seize equivalent assets in India**. | FEMA s. 13(1A)–(1D), s. 37A(1) |
| 11 | **s. 15 (compounding)**: any **s. 13 contravention** may, **on the contravener's application**, be **compounded within 180 days from receipt of the application** by ED officers or **RBI officers** authorised by the Government; once compounded, **no further proceedings** for it. RBI compounds all but **s. 3(a)** cases (dealing with an unauthorised person). Not compounded by RBI: amount **not quantifiable**, **s. 37A** cases, cases **already adjudicated**, suspected **money laundering / terror finance / sovereignty**; a **similar contravention within three years** of an earlier compounding. Fee **₹10,000 + GST**; the compounding amount is **paid within 15 days** of the order and **never exceeds 300 % of the sum involved** (that is the "cap" a class garbled). Since **23 Oct 2026** more categories go to **Regional Offices** (A.P. DIR 28). Detail: A21. | FEMA s. 15; CMD preamble, 1.2, 3.2, 4.1, 4.4, 5.4 II(i), 7.1 |
| 12 | **The section card (our summary for the board)**: s. 4 = bar on holding abroad · s. 6 = capital account (RBI debt, Govt non-debt) · s. 10(5) = declaration before the deal · s. 10(6) = misuse or non-surrender is a contravention · s. 11 = RBI directs and fines banks (₹10,000 / ₹2,000 a day) · s. 13 = penalty on anyone (3× / ₹2 lakh / ₹5,000 a day) · s. 15 = compounding in 180 days. | rows 1–11 |

## Points: LRS for capital account transactions (A2.7)
| # | Testable point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | **The four capital uses under LRS** (all inside the same USD 250,000): (i) **foreign currency account with a bank abroad**; (ii) **immovable property abroad**, **ODI** and **OPI** under the OI Rules, Regulations and Directions 2022; (iii) **loans, including rupee loans, to NRI relatives** (Companies Act relative). No bank credit for any of these [A16 F28]; capital remittances barred to FATF non-co-operative countries [A16 F12]; one-year banking relationship [A16 F26]. | LRS A.6, A.12, A.14, B.7 |
| 14 | **Account abroad**: open, hold and maintain **without RBI approval**, and use it for all LRS-linked transactions. Clubbing for an account or investment **only with co-owners**. A resident **cannot gift foreign currency to another resident's account abroad**. | LRS A.4, A.10; FAQ Q5, Q23 |
| 15 | **Immovable property abroad (OI Rules r. 21)**: no resident may buy or transfer property abroad without permission, **except** as listed; an individual may buy **from a non-resident out of LRS remittances** (resident **relatives may consolidate** their LRS for it), out of **RFC account** funds, **jointly with a non-resident relative**, out of **income or sale proceeds of overseas assets (other than ODI)**, or by **inheritance**; from a **resident** by inheritance, gift or purchase if that resident held it lawfully. A **lease up to five years** is outside the rule. He may later **gift it to an eligible resident or sell it**. | OIR r. 21(1)–(3); LRS A.4; FAQ Q5 |
| 16 | **ODI vs OPI (the test)**: **ODI** = buying **unlisted** equity capital, or subscribing to the MoA, of a foreign entity; or **10 % or more** of a **listed** foreign entity's paid-up equity; or **less than 10 % but with control**. **Control** = right to appoint a majority of directors or control management/policy, including through **10 % or more of voting rights**. Once ODI, **always ODI** even if the stake falls below 10 %. **OPI** = any other investment in foreign securities, but **not** unlisted debt, not securities of Indian residents outside IFSC, not derivatives (unless RBI permits), not commodities/BDRs. **Units of a regulated overseas fund = OPI**. | OIR r. 2(c), (q), (s); OIMD Part I 1(iii), (vi), (ix)(a), (e) |
| 17 | **ODI by a resident individual (Schedule III)**: only in an **operating foreign entity** that is **not engaged in financial services** (an activity that would need a financial regulator's licence in India) and **has no subsidiary or step-down subsidiary where the individual has control**; all **within the LRS limit** unless the Schedule says otherwise. ODI by **inheritance, sweat equity, qualification shares or ESOP** may be in any entity. Sweat equity / qualification shares / ESOP **below 10 % without control count as OPI**. If he holds ODI **without control** and the company later sets up a subsidiary, he **may not then take control**. | OIR Sch. III para 1(1)–(2) and provisos; OIMD 22(1)–(2) |
| 18 | **Barred for every resident's ODI**: a foreign entity in **real estate activity** (buying/selling real estate or **TDR trading**; **not** townships, buildings, roads, bridges built to sell or lease), **gambling in any form**, or **INR-linked financial products** without RBI approval. ODI in a **start-up abroad**: individuals only from **own funds**. No OI in a country the Central Government notifies. | OIR r. 19(1)–(2), r. 9(2) proviso |
| 19 | **Individuals cannot lend to their foreign company**: a resident individual **shall not make financial commitment by way of debt** (loans, debt instruments, guarantees are for Indian entities with ODI and control). So the "loan" LRS allows is only the loan to an NRI relative (F27). | OIMD 21(3); OIR r. 2(f) |
| 20 | **NOC first**: a resident who has an **NPA account**, is a **wilful defaulter**, or is **under investigation** (a financial regulator, CBI, ED, SFIO) must get a **No Objection Certificate** from the lender/regulator/agency **before** any financial commitment or disinvestment; **no reply in 60 days = no objection presumed**. | OIR r. 10 |
| 21 | **After investing (ODI)**: get a **UIN** through the **designated AD bank** **before** the first remittance (Form **FC** with **Form A2**); route all transactions for that UIN through that AD; **evidence of investment** (share certificate etc.) to the AD **within six months** of remittance, **else bring the money back** within the same six months; **realise and repatriate** dues, **disinvestment proceeds** and liquidation proceeds **within 90 days**. Pricing at **arm's length**. | OIRg regs 9(1)–(4); OIMD 16(2)–(3), 27(1); OIR r. 16 |
| 22 | **Selling the ODI**: the investor must have **stayed invested at least one year** (except certain group mergers); on full disinvestment no dues may remain outstanding; report the **disinvestment within 30 days** of receiving the proceeds. Resident individuals **cannot gift** overseas investments **to a non-resident**. A swap that lands non-conforming equity (e.g. financial services, or a subsidiary under control) must be **disinvested within six months**. | OIR r. 17(4); OIRg reg 10(2); OIMD 22(3)–(4) |
| 23 | **Annual Performance Report (APR)**: for each ODI entity **by 31 December** every year (if the entity's year ends 31 Dec, by 31 Dec of the next year), on audited accounts; for an individual, **certified by a chartered accountant**. **No APR** if he holds **under 10 % without control** and has no other commitment, or the entity is in liquidation. Late filings: **Late Submission Fee**, at most **three years** from the due date. | OIRg reg 10(4), 11(1); OIMD 17(3) |
| 24 | **OPI reporting (trap)**: the half-yearly **OPI form** is for persons **other than resident individuals** (reg 10(3)); an individual's OPI is reported **under LRS** (the AD's LRS daily return). ESOP shares that count as OPI are reported by the **Indian employer**. Inherited or gifted foreign securities are **not counted** against LRS and not reported under LRS. | OIRg reg 10(3); OIMD 17(4), 22(5) |
| 25 | **Gifts and ESOP**: foreign securities by **inheritance** (any source) or by **gift from a resident relative** without limit; gift from a non-resident only under **FCRA 2010**. ESOP / sweat equity: no limit on amount, but the remittance **counts toward LRS**; **pre-incorporation expenses** of a foreign entity: up to **USD 100,000** per entity, also inside LRS. | OIR Sch. III paras 2–3; OIMD 22(7), 27(6) |
| 26 | **Trap: the old ODI regime**: books citing **FEMA 120/2004** ("JV/WOS", "Indian party") or the **2015 property regulations** are out of date: both **superseded on 22 Aug 2022** by the OI Rules and Regulations. The LRS MD's old line "setting up JV/WOS from 5 Aug 2013 under FEMA 263" and "no need to repatriate income" (before 24 Aug 2022) are history. Teach "foreign entity", "Indian entity", ODI/OPI. | OIMD covering para 2 (n12381), Part I 1(i), (iv); LRS fn 8, 16, 17 |
| 27 | **Rupee loan to an NRI/PIO relative**: by **crossed cheque or electronic transfer**; **(i) interest-free, minimum maturity one year** (settles the class conflict: it is a **minimum**, not a maximum); (ii) **within the lender's USD 250,000**, the lender's responsibility; (iii) used for the borrower's **personal needs or own business in India**; (iv) **not** for **chit fund, Nidhi company, agriculture/plantation, real estate business, farm houses, TDR trading** (townships, buildings, roads, bridges are not "real estate business"); (v) **credited to the borrower's NRO account** (an eligible credit); (vi) **not remitted outside India**; (vii) repaid by **inward remittance** or **debit to NRO/NRE/FCNR**, or from sale proceeds of the shares/securities/property it was given against. | LRS A.18; FAQ Q25 |
| 28 | **Why it is a capital transaction**: the loan creates a **claim on a non-resident** (an asset of the resident on a person outside India): FEMA s. 2(e). Contrast: a **rupee gift** to an NRI relative's NRO account is a **current** transaction (LRS A.19) [A16 F35]; both sit inside LRS. | FEMA s. 2(e); LRS A.6, A.18, A.19 |
| 29 | **LRS to GIFT IFSC (today)**: an AP may send LRS money to an IFSC for **all permissible LRS purposes**: (i) **financial services or products** under the IFSCA Act 2019 **within IFSC**; (ii) **any current or capital account transaction in another foreign jurisdiction through a foreign currency account (FCA) held in IFSC**. Resident individuals may **open an FCA in IFSC** for these. **No domestic transactions with other residents** may be settled through that FCA. IFSC counts as "abroad" for LRS. | LRS A.11 and fn 11–13; IFSC24 paras 2–3 |
| 30 | **IFSC idle funds (trap)**: the **Feb 2021** rule made idle funds in the IFSC account come back **within 15 days**; **withdrawn on 26 Apr 2023**: idle money now follows the general LRS rule, i.e. **repatriate and surrender within 180 days** unless reinvested (LRS A.17). Before **10 Jul 2024** LRS to IFSC was only for **securities (not of Indian residents outside IFSC)** and **fees to foreign universities in IFSC**; a "15 days" or "only for securities" option is the old rule. | IFSC23 para 2; IFSC24 para 2; LRS A.17 |
| 31 | **ODI in IFSC by an individual**: the "operating, non-financial-services" limits **do not apply** in IFSC (but **no banking or insurance** entity); such an entity may have subsidiaries in IFSC, and outside IFSC only if the individual has **no control**. Units of an IFSC fund are OPI. | OIMD 24(1)–(2) |
| 32 | **Income on LRS investments**: may be **retained or reinvested**; unspent/realised foreign exchange not reinvested is **repatriated and surrendered within 180 days** (R9 reg 7); ODI dues follow the OI rule of **90 days** (F21). | LRS A.17; FAQ Q4; OIRg reg 9(4) |
| 33 | **Sole proprietor and unregistered firm**: their overseas investment is made by the **proprietor / individual partners within their own LRS** under Schedule III; above that only via the Government route for strategic sectors. | OIMD 23(5) |

## Points: reporting under LRS (A2.8)
| # | Testable point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 34 | **What the customer files**: **Form A2** (purpose, own funds, declaration under s. 10(5)) for every LRS remittance, with **PAN**; for ODI also **Form FC**. The AD **certifies** the remittance is not by/to ineligible entities and keeps A2 **one year** for internal audit [A16 F23]. | LRS A.15–A.16, B.9; ORF 6.4–6.5; OIMD 27(1) |
| 35 | **What the bank files: the LRS daily return**: every **AD Category-I bank** uploads **transaction-wise** LRS data in the **"LRS daily return" (CIMS return code R010)** **at the close of business of the next working day** on **CIMS** (sankalan.rbi.org.in); **a NIL report** on a day with no LRS deals. The **LRS monthly return (R089) was discontinued from the reporting month of September 2024**. LRS remittances are also reported in **FETERS** in the normal course. | DIR16 paras 2–3; LRS B.12 |
| 36 | **From 1 Jan 2026: AD Category-II and FFMCs file directly**: they get CIMS access, submit their own **LRS daily return (including nil)**, and **stop routing** LRS deals through their AD Category-I bank. Purpose stated by RBI: they can **check the cumulative amount a resident has remitted (PAN-wise) in the current FY before the next LRS transaction**. | DIR17 paras 1–3 |
| 37 | **Why daily reporting (the exam "purpose" question)**: the USD 250,000 is **per person across all banks and money changers** ("all sources in India", FAQ Q8); only a **central, PAN-wise, near-real-time** record lets the next bank see what is already used.
