## Points
| # | Testable point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | At the customer's request, unless FEMA says otherwise, it is **optional** for a bank to (a) accept or give **early delivery**, or (b) **extend** a contract. The customer must deliver, or ask for extension or cancellation, **on or before the maturity date**. | FEDAI 6.1 |
| 2 | **Early delivery**: the bank recovers or pays the **swap difference**, plus interest on the **outlay / inflow of funds** for the swap. | FEDAI 6.2, 6.6 |
| 3 | **Swap cost** is recovered in **all** cases of early delivery, **whether or not an actual swap is made**; upfront or back-ended at the bank's discretion. Swap **gain** paid at the start or end of the swap period per the bank's policy. | FEDAI 6.5 |
| 4 | Outlay / inflow = the difference between the **original contract rate** and the rate at which the swap could be arranged; the bank recovers interest on an outlay and pays interest on an inflow; rate and threshold per bank policy. For an option-dated contract delivered early, interest up to the date to which the swap is done. | FEDAI 6.6 |
| 5 | **Extension** (FEDAI text): the contract is **cancelled** at the appropriate selling or buying rate on the date of cancellation and **rebooked simultaneously only at the current rate**; the difference between the contract rate and the cancellation rate is recovered from / paid to the customer (as 6.4); the request must be made **on or before maturity**. | FEDAI 6.3 |
| 6 | **RBI, 10 Oct 2026 (immediate)**: an AD **shall not permit users to rebook** any INR foreign exchange derivative (deliverable or non-deliverable) **cancelled** with any AD after the directions. ADs **may continue to permit rollover on maturity**, subject to the Master Direction. So the old "cancel and rebook" route is closed; **rollover on maturity** remains. (How FEDAI 6.3 will be re-worded: open; re-check FEDAI AR circulars at the freeze.) | RBI25 para 2(a), (b) |
| 7 | **Cancellation at the customer's request** (on or before maturity): the AD recovers / pays the **difference between the contract rate and the rate at which it is cancelled**. | FEDAI 6.4(i)(a) |
| 8 | Cancellation rates: **purchase contracts at the AD's TT selling rate**; **sale contracts at its TT buying rate**; **before maturity, the appropriate forward TT rate**. (Purchase/sale = from the bank's side.) | FEDAI 6.4(ii) |
| 9 | Recovery of the exchange difference on cancellation (on or before maturity): **lump sum or instalments**; not beyond the original maturity; instalments uniform, **at least once a quarter**; Board-approved policy, disclosed upfront. | FEDAI 6.4(i)(b), (c) |
| 10 | A contract that becomes impossible to perform (even by a Government prohibitory order) is **not void**; the customer must apply for cancellation. | FEDAI 6.4(iii) |
| 11 | **Automatic cancellation**: with no instructions, a matured contract is cancelled by the bank **within 3 working business days after the maturity date**, per its policy; swap cost, if any, recovered under advice. | FEDAI 6.4(iv)(a), (b) |
| 12 | On cancellation **after maturity** (automatic), the customer is **not entitled to an exchange gain** but **must pay any loss**; the bank may pass the gain only if satisfied the delay was beyond the client's control, and records why. | FEDAI 6.4(iv)(c) |
| 13 | **Early realisation of an export bill**: interest for the unexpired period refunded; notional swap cost paid / recovered as for early delivery; interest on outlay / inflow per 6.6. | FEDAI 2.2(c) |
| 14 | **Hedging without proving the underlying** (contracted exposure, OTC): **USD 5 million** notional outstanding across all ADs (was USD 100 million); same USD 5 million for exchange-traded currency derivatives across all exchanges. AD takes an **undertaking** that the exposure is not hedged with another AD (amounts with other ADs stated); documents kept **2 years**. IIBF Vision (Nov 2026) prints the old USD 100 million: teach USD 5 million. | RBI25 para 2(c), (d), 3 |
| 15 | **FERR** (one line): the AD keeps **20%** of the INR equivalent of the notional **in cash with RBI** for specified INR forex derivatives (user > USD 2 million hedging a current-account exposure by buying foreign currency; the AD's own hedges buying foreign currency), for contracts booked after issue. Sum in C08. | RBI26/27 (updates file) |
| 16 | Arithmetic convention for cancellation sums: the opposite merchant deal at the cancellation date; **exchange difference = (contract rate − cancellation rate) × amount**, signed from the customer's side (exporter who sold forward: gains if the cancellation TT selling rate is below his contract rate; pays if above). | FEDAI 6.4 (arithmetic; label "worked method") |

## Traps
- Cancelling a purchase contract at TT buying (the contract side) instead of TT selling.
- Passing the exchange gain to the customer on automatic cancellation after maturity.
- "No swap was actually done, so no swap cost" (6.5: recovered in all cases).
- "Cancel and rebook freely" (barred for INR derivatives cancelled after 10 Oct 2026; rollover on maturity allowed).
- "USD 100 million without underlying" (now USD 5 million).
- Automatic cancellation "on the maturity date" or "after 15 days" (FEDAI: within 3 working business days after maturity).
