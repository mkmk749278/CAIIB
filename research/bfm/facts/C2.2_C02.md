# Fact sheet · C2.2 Money market products (video C02)
Layer 2 (PLAYBOOK §4c). Written 2026-11-24. Rules as at the 31 Dec 2026 cut-off (exam May/June 2027). Rows cited as [F1]–[F45]. Our own words; every row cites its primary text with its full https:// URL opened during Deep Research.


What this lesson must carry. The money market handles short-term funds up to one year. In the unsecured interbank segment (Call, Notice and Term Money Directions, 2021, updated 8 June 2023), Call Money is overnight, Notice Money is 2 to 14 days, and Term Money is 15 days to 1 year. Only banks (SCBs excluding LABs, Payment Banks, SFBs, RRBs, Co-operative Banks) and Primary Dealers may participate, all as both borrowers and lenders. Since 8 June 2023, SCBs (excluding SFBs and Payment Banks) set internal Board-approved borrowing limits within DoR inter-bank liability ceilings (the old 100%/125% cap no longer applies to them). SFBs remain capped at 100% (fortnightly average) and 125% (single day) of capital funds in Call/Notice (Board-approved for Term); Payment Banks and RRBs at 100% / 125% of capital funds across all three; Co-operative Banks at 2.0% of previous-FY aggregate deposits across all three; and Primary Dealers at 225% of previous-FY NOF. RBI sets no fixed lending cap (Board decides within DoR exposure norms). OTC deals outside NDS-CALL are reported within 15 minutes; Call Money hours extended to 9:00 AM–7:00 PM from 1 July 2025. In collateralised borrowing, the Repurchase Transactions (Repo) Directions, 2025 (11 November 2025) cover market repos of 1 day to 1 year, first leg T+0 or T+1, DvP settlement, bar borrowing against own or related-entity securities, and include Municipal Debt Securities (added 11 Nov 2025, min 2% haircut) and NaBFID (added 1 Jan 2025). TREPS (Clearcorp platform, CCIL as CCP and Tri-Party Agent) replaced CBLO on 5 November 2018. Under CP & NCD Directions, 2024 (effective 1 April 2024), Commercial Paper is an unsecured demat promissory note issued at a discount for 7 days to 1 year, minimum ₹5 lakh, minimum rating 'A3' (the 2017 ₹1,000 crore dual-rating rule was dropped), Standard fund-based facilities across banks/AIFIs/NBFCs, LLPs and co-op societies eligible at ₹100 crore net worth, IPA must be a Scheduled Commercial Bank, buyback allowed after 7 days (down from 30), individual+HUF primary cap 25%, and related-party investment barred. Under CD Directions, 2021, bank CDs (SCBs, RRBs, SFBs) run 7 days to 1 year, minimum ₹5 lakh (up from ₹1 lakh), demat, at discount or fixed/floating rate, no loans against CDs, and issuing banks may buy back CDs after 7 days — whereas AIFIs (1 to 3 years, AIFI Directions 2025) cannot buy back their CDs. Under Credit Facilities Directions, 2025 (Ch. X), Bill Rediscounting allows genuine trade bills only (DUPN 15 to 90 days, up-front discount); accommodation bills, services bills, and repos against discounted bills are barred. OMOs are RBI's outright G-sec purchases (inject) and sales (absorb) for durable liquidity.
