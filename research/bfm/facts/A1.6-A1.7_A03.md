# Fact sheet · A1.6 FEDAI merchant-rate rules · A1.7 Forex arithmetic (video A03, the drill)

Layer 2 (PLAYBOOK §4c). A03 is a numerical drill on A01's rules. Its rule rows are the A01 fact sheet
(`A1.6-A1.7_A01.md`, rows 1–26; cited here as [A01-F13] etc.); this sheet adds only what A03 uses beyond them.
Written 2026-11-21. Rules as at the 31 Dec 2026 cut-off. Source: FEDAI Rules 10th ed., updated to 20 Aug 2026
(AR 03/2026, 7 Sep 2026), FEDAI's own PDF (see the A01 sheet).

| # | Testable point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The four merchant rates and the exam convention for building them: TT selling = ask + margin; bill selling = **unrounded** TT selling + further margin; TT buying = bid − margin; bill buying = bid − margin. Rates to 4 decimals; rupee amounts to paise. Follow the question when it states another base. | [A01-F10]; FEDAI 5.8 (rounding per bank policy) |
| 2 | Multiplier method on a basic calculator: "plus m%" = × (1 + m/100); "less m%" = × (1 − m/100). E.g. less 0.15% = × 0.9985; plus 0.12% = × 1.0012. | Arithmetic; `tools/check/fx_merchant_rates.py` |
| 3 | Inward remittance converted to rupees → TT buying. | FEDAI 4.4 [A01-F13] |
| 4 | Outward remittance → TT selling. | FEDAI 4.1 [A01-F14] |
| 5 | Export bill purchased / discounted / negotiated → bill buying. | FEDAI 2.1(a) [A01-F15] |
| 6 | Import bill retired without cover → bill selling. | FEDAI 3.1(a) [A01-F17] |
| 7 | Export bill dishonoured (returned unpaid) before crystallisation → recover at **TT selling** plus interest and charges. | FEDAI 2.1(d) [A01-F20] |
| 8 | Foreign currency notes, TCs, prepaid cards bought from or sold to a customer → the bank's buying or selling rate **for that instrument** (notes: the widest spread; given in the sum). | FEDAI 4.2 [A01-F12, F25] |
| 9 | **FCNR(B) deposit paid out in rupees** (or credited to an NRE account): foreign currency comes into the bank's hands and is converted → **TT buying**. Funding an FCNR(B) deposit from rupees → **TT selling**. Not stated in the FEDAI Rules; it is the in/out principle of 4.1 and 4.4 applied, the convention in exam sums. | Principle of FEDAI 4.1, 4.4 (label as convention) |
| 10 | **Foreign currency cheque or demand draft purchased** (bank pays the rupees before it collects the money abroad) → **bill buying** in exam sums; if sent for collection, TT buying after the Nostro credit. Not stated in the FEDAI Rules for clean instruments; principle of 2.1(a) and 2.5(a). | Principle of FEDAI 2.1(a), 2.5(a) (label as convention) |
| 11 | A booked contract overrides the card rate in every case. | [A01-F26] |

## Traps
- Adding both selling margins to the ask in one step (GBP example: 114.8593 instead of 114.8595).
- Loading bill selling on the **rounded** TT selling rate (usually the same 4th decimal; use the unrounded figure).
- Taking a buying margin off the ask, or a selling margin off the bid.
- Customer's side instead of the bank's (A01).

## Question blueprint
calculate (all four rates; the rupee amount of a deal, to paise) · which (rate for each of 8 deals) · compare (TT vs
bill) · exception (FCNR(B) payout; returned export bill) · apply (a keyed, no-options sum).
