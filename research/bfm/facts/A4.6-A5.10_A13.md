# Fact sheet · A5.7, A5.8, A5.9, A5.10 (also A4.6 import side, C3.5) · Import rules and finance (video A13)

Layer 2 (PLAYBOOK §4c). Draft 2, 2026-11-22 (c1 review fixes). Rules as at the **31 Dec 2026** cut-off (exam May/June 2027). Rows cited
as [F3]. Our own words; short quotes in quotation marks are the source's words. Builds on A11 (`A5.1-A5.5_A11.md`:
the 2026 regulations, EDPMS, ₹10 lakh closure) and A01/A03 (bill selling rate, FEDAI Rule 3).

**The big change this lesson must carry:** from **1 October 2026** imports are governed by the same new FEM (Export
and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026 that A11 taught for exports; RBI's directions of 16 Jan 2026
(A.P. DIR 20) supersede the **Master Direction – Import of Goods and Services** and **71** import circulars on that
day [F1]. The fixed numbers older books teach for imports (pay within **6 months of shipment**; advance remittance
without guarantee up to **USD 200,000**, board-policy waiver up to **USD 5 million**, Ministry of Finance waiver for
Government/PSU above **USD 100,000**; evidence of import within **3 months**, then 3 months' follow-up; physical import
within 6 months / 3 years of an advance) are **not in the 2026 rules**: the contract and the AD's policy decide. They
appear only as trap cards ("older books say…"). **Trade credit is unchanged**: the 2026 ECB overhaul replaced the ECB
schedule only; the TC framework in the ECB-TC Master Direction (updated 8 Sep 2026) still has its amounts, periods
and cost ceiling [F14–F20].

## Points
| # | Testable point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Which rule book**: from 1 Oct 2026 the FEM (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations 2026 cover **imports too**; A.P. DIR 20 supersedes the **Import MD** and 71 import circulars (advance remittance, evidence of import, IDPMS, direct receipt of documents, extension of time, gold, diamonds…). ADs follow FEMA, rules, regulations, directions **and the Foreign Trade Policy**. | R26 reg 1; DIR paras 2, 4, Annex B |
| 2 | **IEC and FTP**: no import of goods without an **IEC** (10 characters, same as PAN, issued by DGFT) unless exempted; the AD checks the import is permitted under the FTP (freely importable, or **restricted** → an import authorisation/licence; **prohibited** → not at all). | FTP 2.05 (A11 F3); DIR para 2 |
| 3 | **Manner of payment**: through an authorised bank/person. **Nepal and Bhutan → INR**. **ACU members (other than Nepal and Bhutan)**: payments between residents of participant countries go **through the ACU mechanism** (or as RBI directs); all other transactions as for others. **Other countries → INR or any foreign currency.** Also any manner in the FTP; also by debit/credit to an account kept under FEMA. | MRP reg 3(2)(I)(a)(i)–(iii), (b), proviso; R26 reg 4(1) |
| 4 | **ACU**: the Asian Monetary Unit is denominated **ACU Dollar, ACU Euro, ACU Yen** (= 1 USD, 1 EUR, 1 JPY). ACU Euro from 1 Jan 2009; ACU Yen from 6 Mar 2020; **ACU Euro operations suspended since 1 Jul 2016**. ADs keep ACU Dollar/Euro/Yen accounts with correspondents in other participant countries, **separate** from ordinary USD/EUR/JPY accounts; eligible payments settle through them. (Old MRP proviso, deleted Feb 2025: goods shipped from an ACU country by a non-ACU supplier could be paid outside ACU; now "for all other transactions" covers it.) | ACU 2020 paras 1, 3, 4, 6; ACU 2008 para 2; MRP footnote 1 |
| 5 | **Payment period for imports**: the AD **monitors IDPMS** and follows up so the importer pays **within the period in the underlying contract**; the AD may **extend** on the importer's reasoned request if satisfied. **No fixed "6 months from shipment"** in the 2026 rules. | R26 reg 9 |
| 6 | **Trap (old rule)**: OMD B.5: import remittances to be completed **not later than six months from the date of shipment** (except amounts withheld as performance guarantee); COVID: 12 months for imports up to 31 Jul 2020; AD could extend **up to 6 months at a time, maximum 3 years**. All superseded 1 Oct 2026. | OMD B.5.1(i), B.5.4(i) |
| 7 | **Advance payment for imports**: AD may permit after satisfying itself of the **genuineness of the need** for an advance; the AD **may specify thresholds** above which a **standby LC or a guarantee** is required (the AD's own policy; no RBI figure). The importer routes the advance **and later payments through the same AD** (may switch after informing both ADs). | R26 reg 10(2), 10(3) |
| 8 | **Trap (old rule)**: OMD C.1.1: advance **without ceiling**, but **above USD 200,000** an unconditional irrevocable SBLC or guarantee from an international bank of repute (or an Indian AD's guarantee against such a bank's counter-guarantee); AD could waive it **up to USD 5,000,000** under a **Board-approved policy** for importers with a good track record (not PSUs); a **PSU / Government** body needed a specific **Ministry of Finance waiver above USD 100,000**; physical import within **6 months (3 years for capital goods)** of the remittance. All superseded. | OMD C.1.1(i)(a)–(c), (v) |
| 9 | **Gold and silver**: **no advance remittance** for import of gold or silver (the one fixed ban that stays). | R26 reg 11 |
| 9a | **The gold/silver exception (reg 11 "save as otherwise provided in the … Directions")**: RBI's directions let **qualified jewellers** (notified by IFSCA) import **gold** (A.P. DIR 04, 25 May 2022) and **silver** (A.P. DIR 07, 10 Nov 2023), and **India-UAE CEPA tariff-rate-quota holders** import gold (A.P. DIR 14, 31 Jan 2024), through the **IIBX** (India International Bullion Exchange, GIFT City IFSC) with an **advance remittance for 11 days**; unused advance back to the same AD within 11 days; bill of entry to the AD. These three circulars are **not** in A.P. DIR 20's Annex B list, so read as still in force (the old MD C.11.3 that compiled them is superseded). **Re-check at the freeze.** | R26 reg 11; OMD C.11.3; DIR Annex B (checked: not listed); c1 expert review (DIR 04 read) |
| 10 | **Import not materialised**: the importer must **repatriate the advance**. If not repatriated within the contract period (or extended period), or the IDPMS entry is not closed under reg 18(1)(j), **every future advance** by that importer needs an **unconditional irrevocable SBLC**, or a guarantee from an **international bank of repute**, or an **AD's guarantee against such a bank's counter-guarantee**. | R26 reg 12 |
| 11 | **Interest cap**: interest on **delayed import payments** (and on export advances) must not exceed the **all-in-cost ceiling of trade credit** (FEM Borrowing and Lending Regs 2018). | R26 reg 10(4) |
| 12 | **Third-party payments** for imports: AD may permit if satisfied of the bona fides (old conditions: tripartite agreement, invoice narration: superseded). | R26 reg 8; OMD B.7 |
| 13 | **Set-off**: import payables may be set off against export receivables from/to the same overseas party or its group/associates (A11 F15). | R26 reg 7 |
| 14 | **Trade credit (TC), definition**: credit extended by the **overseas supplier or a financial institution** for **permissible imports into India**; includes **suppliers' credit** (credit given by the **overseas supplier**) and **buyers' credit** (a **loan arranged by the importer** from an **overseas bank or FI** to pay for the import). | BL 2(1)(ah) and Explanation; TC 13 |
| 15 | **TC is not ECB**: "Trade Credit with original maturity **up to three years**" raised under the regulations is **not treated as ECB**. (TC beyond 3 years is in ECB territory.) | BL Schedule I para 4(3)(a) |
| 16 | **TC framework (automatic route)**: **eligible borrower** = a person resident in India acting as an importer; forms: buyers' credit and suppliers' credit; **FCY or INR** denominated. **Amount**: up to **USD 150 million** per import transaction for **oil/gas refining & marketing, airline and shipping companies**; **USD 50 million** for others. | TC 14 (i)–(iii) |
| 17 | **Recognised lenders**: suppliers' credit → the **supplier of goods located outside India**. Buyers' credit → **banks, FIs, foreign equity holder(s) located outside India, and FIs in IFSCs in India**. **Foreign branches/subsidiaries of Indian banks**: recognised lenders **only for FCY TC**. | TC 14 (iv) and Note |
| 18 | **Period** (reckoned **from the date of shipment**): **capital goods up to 3 years**; **non-capital goods up to one year or the operating cycle, whichever is less**; shipyards/shipbuilders: non-capital goods up to 3 years. AD must ensure the "lower of operating cycle or one year". | TC 14 (v), 18 |
| 19 | **All-in-cost ceiling** (a year): **FCY TC: benchmark rate + 300 bps** (new TCs; +350 bps for old LIBOR-linked TCs moved to ARR); **INR TC: benchmark + 250 bps**. Benchmark: 6-month interbank rate or ARR for the currency (FCY); G-sec yield of matching maturity (INR). (The 2026 overhaul removed the **ECB** ceiling, not the TC ceiling; ECBs under 3 years must even follow the TC ceiling; ECBs of 3 years+ are at market cost.) | TC 14 (vi); BL 2(1)(e); Schedule I para 7(1), 7(2) |
| 20 | **TC security and reporting**: ADs may give **bank guarantees** for TC (not above the TC amount, not beyond the TC period); **Letters of Undertaking / Comfort for TC are not permitted**. Importer may offer movable/immovable assets or corporate/personal guarantee. ADs report TC **monthly in Form TC by the 10th** of the next month; suppliers' credit **beyond 180 days** is also reported. Change of currency: FCY→FCY or FCY→INR free; **INR→FCY not permitted**. TC for gold jewellery: old OMD C.11.2 cap of **90 days** (superseded with the MD; not taught as current). | TC 14 (ix), 16.1, 16.2, 17.1 and Note; OMD C.11.2 |
| 21 | **Import bill rate (FEDAI 3.1)**: **retirement** of an import bill → the **hedge (forward) contract rate** if there is one; otherwise the **prevailing bill selling rate**. **Crystallisation** of an import bill → **the same** (no flip). | FEDAI 3.1(a), (b) |
| 22 | **Import bill under LC unpaid**: crystallised **as per the stated policy of the bank** (no fixed day in the current rules). Older FEDAI editions (lead: eximguru.com transcription of FEDAI Rule 3; not FEDAI's site): sight import bills under LC held in foreign currency for at most **10 days from receipt of documents**, crystallised on the **10th day** (holiday → next working day): taught as history only ("older books"). Bills negotiated under import LCs carry the **commercial rate of interest** applicable to the bank's **domestic advances**; FEDAI fixes the rate, **not the start day**: the lesson's case charges from the day the bank paid abroad (the usual exam set-up) and says so. | FEDAI 3.3, 3.2(a); lead: eximguru.com/exim/reserve-bank/fedai-rules/rules-3-import-transactions.aspx |
| 23 | **Delivery date of a forward for an import bill**: the date of **retirement or crystallisation, whichever is earlier**. | FEDAI 5.4(iii) |
| 24 | **Evidence of import**: for goods, the **bill of entry** (the importer's declaration to Customs, for home consumption or warehousing). At an **EDI port** Customs' bill-of-entry data reaches **IDPMS** directly; for a **non-EDI port** the AD enters the import details in IDPMS **within 5 working days** of receipt of documents. **Import of services**: the AD enters details in IDPMS only for an importer **other than a resident individual** and where the invoice/contract value is **more than ₹10 lakh**, within 5 working days of receipt (2nd Amendment, from 1 Oct 2026). | LAW (Customs Act s. 46); R26 reg 18(1)(c), (d) and footnote 12 |
| 25 | **IDPMS (Import Data Processing and Monitoring System)**: RBI's platform where ADs match each **import payment** (outward remittance) with its **bill of entry**. The AD enters **all outward remittances** for imports; **monitors** outstanding entries and follows up; **marks off** an entry **after ensuring the payment for the import has been made**. The AD debits the importer **only after satisfying itself of genuineness** and **simultaneously closes or updates** the IDPMS entry. "IDPMS" includes any other system RBI specifies. Started **2016** (A.P. DIR 65, 28 Apr 2016; DIR 05, 6 Oct 2016), modelled on EDPMS; hard-copy BoE "exchange control copy" discontinued 1 Dec 2016; it **replaced the separate BEF statement** (needed only till the half year ended Dec 2017); all superseded by R26 reg 18. | R26 regs 4(2), 18(1)(e), (f), (h), footnote 7; DIR Annex B items 64–66; OMD C.8, C.10(ii) |
| 26 | **₹10 lakh simple closure (imports)**: where the **bill of entry (goods) or invoice (services) is up to ₹10 lakh**, the IDPMS entry may be closed on the **importer's declaration** that payment was made in full or otherwise; or a **quarterly** declaration for **bulk closure**. | R26 reg 4(2) second proviso |
| 27 | **Other IDPMS closures**: AD **may** close an **import-advance entry** where no import took place and repatriation of the advance is not possible (reasoned request, genuineness); and an entry **settled at a reduced value** (importer's request). All foreign-trade transactions are also reported in **FETERS**. | R26 reg 18(1)(j), (k), 18(2) |
| 28 | **Trap (old rule)**: OMD C.10: if no evidence of import within **3 months from the date of remittance**, the AD follows up **for the next 3 months**, at least once by registered letter; old MD also allowed a **CEO/auditor certificate** in lieu of the bill of entry. Superseded. | OMD C.10(i), C.7.2 |
| 29 | **AD policy and SOP** (same as exports, A11 F20): documents, **timelines** and charges; **extension of time for import payments**; **advance payments for imports**; **import factoring**; escalation and appeal; no charge or penalty for a customer's regulatory delay; main features on the website. | R26 reg 19 |
| 30 | **Merchanting (MTT)**: goods bought from abroad and sold abroad without entering India, under the FTP; gap between outward and inward legs **≤ 6 months** (AD may extend); pay only the **overseas seller**, receive only from the **overseas buyer** (AD may allow a third party on reasons); documents to the AD; AD closes/updates **both EDPMS and IDPMS** after both legs. | R26 reg 16, 18(1)(l) |
| 31 | **Pledge vs hypothecation** (import loans against goods): **pledge** = goods **delivered to the bank** as security (bailment; possession with the lender, e.g. imported stock in the bank's godown); **hypothecation** = a **charge without delivery of possession** (borrower keeps and uses the goods, e.g. stock in the importer's own warehouse). | LAW (ICA s. 172; SARFAESI s. 2(1)(n)) |
